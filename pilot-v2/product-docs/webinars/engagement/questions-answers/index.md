# Questions and answers (Q&A)

Run a question queue in your webinar separately from chat, choose whether questions are public, private to hosts and moderators, or off, and check how many went unanswered.

- Applies to: Live, simulive and evergreen webinars
- Status: Live
- Verified: 2026-10-16
- Canonical page: https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/product-docs/webinars/engagement/questions-answers/

Q&A in EasyWebinar is a question queue that runs separately from chat. Attendees submit questions, and you and your moderators work through them in the room's **Question** panel, which splits them into **Active** and **Answered**. You decide on the webinar's **Engagement** tab whether questions are visible to everyone, visible only to hosts and moderators, or switched off. After the session, Analytics shows how many questions were asked and how many you answered.

## At a glance {#at-a-glance}

| | |
|---|---|
| **What it is** | A queue of attendee questions, separate from chat |
| **Where you set it** | Webinar → **Engagement** → **Q&A** |
| **Options** | **Public Q&A**, **Private Q&A** or **Disable** |
| **When to set it** | Before the webinar, as part of its setup |
| **In the room** | The **Question** panel on the side rail, split into **Active** and **Answered** |
| **Who answers** | Hosts and moderators; moderators join with the **Moderator Join Link for Live room** |
| **After the session** | **Analytics → Questions & Answers**: questions asked and answered, with a **Not Answered Rate** |
| **Webinar types** | Live, simulive and evergreen, set up the same way on each |

## What Q&A is {#what-it-is}

Chat is conversation: greetings, reactions, side remarks, attendees talking to each other. Q&A is a list. Each entry is a question, and your job is to work through the list during or at the end of the session.

Keeping the two apart matters on any webinar with more than a handful of people. In a busy chat, a real question scrolls away under a stream of "hello from Leeds". In the Q&A queue, it waits until someone answers it.

You also choose who can see the queue:

- **Public**, so attendees can see what has already been asked.
- **Private**, so only hosts and moderators see the questions.
- **Off**, when the webinar shouldn't take questions at all.

## Why Q&A matters {#why-it-matters}

### What it's for {#what-its-for}

Q&A gives attendees a dependable way to ask something and a reasonable expectation that the question will be seen. For the presenter, it turns scattered questions into a list that can be answered in order.

### How it helps {#how-it-helps}

- **Questions don't get lost in chat.** They sit in their own queue until they are answered.
- **Public Q&A cuts repeats.** When people can see a question has already been asked, you don't answer the same thing five times.
- **Private Q&A keeps the room on track.** One attendee's question doesn't set the agenda for everyone else, and you choose what to address on air.
- **You can see what's left.** The **Active** and **Answered** split in the room, and the **Not Answered Rate** afterwards, show what you didn't get to.

### What happens without it {#without-it}

Without a separate queue, questions compete with every other message in chat. The presenter scrolls back looking for them, misses some, and answers others twice. Attendees whose questions were missed leave unsure, and on a selling webinar [an unanswered objection often decides the sale](https://easywebinar.com/blog/webinar-qa-strategy-closing-opportunities/).

### Why buyers care {#why-buyers-care}

Teams that run demos, trainings and sales webinars want to answer questions without losing control of the room. Being able to keep chat sociable while routing questions into a private queue, and then checking afterwards how many were left unanswered, is the practical version of that control.

## Set up Q&A {#set-up-qa}

1. Open your webinar and click the **Engagement** tab.
2. Click **Q&A**.
3. Choose one of the three options.
4. Save your changes.

![The Q&A section of the Engagement tab with Public Q&A selected and Private Q&A and Disable alongside](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/images/engagement/step-02-qna.png)

| Option | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Public Q&A** | Questions are visible to everyone |
| **Private Q&A** | Questions are visible only to hosts and moderators |
| **Disable** | Q&A isn't available during the webinar |

### Public or private? {#public-or-private}

| Choose | When |
|---|---|
| **Public Q&A** | You want attendees to see what's already been asked, and you're happy for questions to be part of the shared experience |
| **Private Q&A** | You want control over what the room sees, or questions may be personal, commercial or off-topic |
| **Disable** | The session is a tightly controlled presentation with no questions taken |

### Pair it with your chat setting {#pair-with-chat}

Q&A and chat are set side by side on the Engagement tab, and they work best chosen together.

| You want | Chat | Q&A |
|---|---|---|
| A lively public webinar | Everyone | **Public Q&A** |
| A sales session with private conversations | Hosts only | **Private Q&A** |
| A tightly controlled presentation | Hosts only | **Disable** |
| A large public webinar you can't moderate by hand | Everyone + AI Moderation | **Private Q&A** |
| An automated webinar that still feels busy | Everyone + Chat Simulation | **Private Q&A** |

**Everyone** for chat plus **Private Q&A** is a common setup: the room stays sociable, and questions reach you in an orderly queue.

## Answer questions in the live room {#answer-in-the-room}

When you [enter the room](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/product-docs/webinars/setup/overview-tab/#enter-room), the side rail holds a **Question** panel. It is the Q&A queue, split into two lists:

| List | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Active** | Questions still waiting for an answer |
| **Answered** | Questions you've dealt with |

Q&A moderation preferences sit in the room's **Settings**, under **Preferences**, alongside chat moderation and appearance.

A few habits that keep the queue under control:

- **Keep an eye on Active during the session**, not only at the end, so a quick answer doesn't wait half an hour.
- **Group similar questions** and answer them together on air.
- **Leave time at the end** to clear what's left in Active before you close.

Ending the session disconnects everyone and nobody can rejoin, so finish the questions you want to answer before you click [**End Session**](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/product-docs/webinars/live-room/run-a-live-webinar/#end-the-session).

### Bring in a moderator {#moderators}

On a large webinar, a moderator can work the queue while the presenter keeps presenting. Send them the [**Moderator Join Link for Live room**](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/product-docs/webinars/registration/sharing-links/#moderator-join-link) from the webinar's **Share** dialog; it is the link for someone handling chat and questions. Keep it private, because anyone holding it can join in that role.

## Q&A on recorded webinars {#recorded-webinars}

On a [**simulive** webinar](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/product-docs/webinars/getting-started/create-a-simulive-webinar/#with-chat), chat and Q&A work normally. Everyone in a session sees the same moment of the video, and you and your moderators answer in real time while the recording plays.

On an [**evergreen** webinar](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/product-docs/webinars/getting-started/create-an-evergreen-webinar/#with-chat-options), Q&A is set up the same way on the Engagement tab. There is also a separate built-in contact form, the **EW Contact Box**, which sends submitted questions to an email address you choose. On a recorded webinar, attendees find it under **Contact** in the room, on its **Question** tab. It is a different thing from the Q&A queue: questions arrive by email rather than in the room's **Question** panel.

| | Q&A queue | Contact box |
|---|---|---|
| **Where questions go** | The room's **Question** panel | **Email address to receive questions** |
| **Who sees them** | Everyone or hosts and moderators, depending on your setting | Whoever reads that inbox |
| **Works with chat off** | Set separately from chat | Yes, even with **No Chat** |

## How Q&A works with other features {#works-with}

### Live chat {#with-chat}

Chat and Q&A are separate controls on the same tab. Chat's [**Who can see messages** setting](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/product-docs/webinars/engagement/live-chat/#who-can-see-messages) and the Q&A option are chosen separately, so you can run a public chat with private questions.

### AI Moderation (Beta) {#with-ai-moderation}

The [AI Moderator](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/product-docs/webinars/getting-started/create-an-evergreen-webinar/#with-ai-moderator), in Beta, has a **Q&A Moderation** screen. With **Suggest Answer** it drafts an answer for you to approve before attendees see it; with **Answer directly** it answers without host approval. It doesn't replace a human moderator on a live webinar.

### Analytics {#with-analytics}

The webinar's **Analytics** Overview includes a [**Questions & Answers** panel](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/product-docs/webinars/analytics/webinar-analytics/#engagement-and-offers) showing how many questions were asked and how many you answered. The **Not Answered Rate** is a useful check after a busy session: it tells you how many questions you left behind.

### Follow-up {#with-follow-up}

Unanswered questions are [a reason to follow up](https://easywebinar.com/blog/post-webinar-sales-strategy-guide/). [**Webinar Follow-ups**](https://easywebinar.com/features/webinar-follow-up/) on the **Notifications** tab can go to everyone who registered, only those who attended, or only no-shows.

## Who uses Q&A, and how {#who-uses-it}

These are typical patterns, to show how the setting changes with the kind of webinar.

### SaaS and product teams {#product-teams}

A [live product demo](https://easywebinar.com/use-cases/product-demos/) runs **Private Q&A**, so prospects can ask about pricing or their own setup without the room seeing. A solutions engineer joins as moderator and works the **Active** list while the presenter demos.

### Course creators {#course-creators}

A [creator teaching a free workshop](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/product-docs/use-cases/sell-courses/) uses **Public Q&A**. Attendees see that someone has already asked about prerequisites, and the creator answers the most common questions in a block before the [enrolment offer](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/product-docs/webinars/engagement/timed-offers/#show-in-room).

### Customer success teams {#customer-success}

A [monthly onboarding webinar](https://easywebinar.com/use-cases/onboard-train-customers/) runs as a simulive session. Q&A stays private, and the team answers setup questions in real time while the recording plays, then checks the **Not Answered Rate** afterwards.

### B2B marketing teams {#b2b-marketing}

A panel discussion is tightly scripted, so Q&A is set to **Disable** and chat to **Hosts only**. Questions still reach the team privately, without interrupting the panel.

## Limits and things to know {#limits}

- **Set Q&A before the webinar.** It's part of the webinar's setup rather than the live room.
- **Private means hosts and moderators.** Attendees don't see private questions.
- **Disable removes Q&A for the whole session.**
- **The contact box is separate.** Contact box questions go to an email address, not to the **Question** panel.
- **Ending the session is final.** **End Session** disconnects everyone and nobody can rejoin.
- **The Not Answered Rate is after the fact.** Use **Active** in the room to see what's waiting while you're live.

## Common questions {#common-questions}

#### How is Q&A different from chat? {#faq-chat-vs-qa}

Chat is a running conversation. Q&A collects questions in a list you work through, and with **Private Q&A** attendees don't see it.

#### Should questions be public or private? {#faq-public-or-private}

Public if you want people to see what's already been asked. Private if you want control over what the room sees.

#### Who can see questions with Private Q&A? {#faq-private-who}

Only hosts and moderators.

#### Can I turn Q&A off? {#faq-disable}

Yes. Choose **Disable**, and Q&A isn't available during the webinar.

#### Where do I see questions during the webinar? {#faq-where-in-room}

In the **Question** panel on the room's side rail, split into **Active** and **Answered**.

#### Can someone else answer questions while I present? {#faq-moderator}

Yes. Send them the **Moderator Join Link for Live room** from the [**Share** dialog](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/product-docs/webinars/registration/sharing-links/#open-share-dialog).

#### Can I change the Q&A setting once the webinar has started? {#faq-change-live}

Choose it before the session. Q&A is set on the Engagement tab as part of the webinar's setup, not from the live room.

#### How do I know how many questions I missed? {#faq-not-answered}

Check the **Questions & Answers** panel in **Analytics**. The **Not Answered Rate** shows how many questions were left behind.

#### Does Q&A work on simulive webinars? {#faq-simulive}

Yes. Chat and Q&A work normally, and you [answer in real time while the recording plays](https://easywebinar.com/simulive-webinars/).

#### Can the AI Moderator answer questions for me? {#faq-ai-moderator}

The AI Moderator is in Beta. In **Suggest Answer** mode it drafts answers for you to approve; in **Answer directly** mode it answers without approval.

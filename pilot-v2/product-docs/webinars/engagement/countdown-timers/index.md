# Countdown timers

How EasyWebinar's countdown timers work on offers and registration pages, why they matter for selling with webinars, and how to set them up honestly.

- Applies to: Live, simulive and evergreen webinars
- Status: Live
- Verified: 2026-10-16
- Canonical page: https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/product-docs/webinars/engagement/countdown-timers/

EasyWebinar has two countdowns. An **offer countdown** sits on an offer card in the webinar room and shows how long that offer stays open. A [**registration-page countdown**](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/product-docs/webinars/registration/registration-page-builder/#countdown-block) shows how long is left until the session starts. Both are built in, and neither needs a separate timer tool or embedded widget.

## At a glance {#at-a-glance}

| | Offer countdown | Registration-page countdown |
|---|---|---|
| **Where it appears** | On an offer card, in the webinar room | On the registration page, before people sign up |
| **What it counts down to** | The end of the offer | The start of the session |
| **How you turn it on** | **Countdown timer** switch in the offer editor | Countdown block in the page builder; already on the page when a new live webinar opens in the builder |
| **Where you set it** | Webinar → **CTAs** → an offer | Webinar → **Content** → **Registration Page** |
| **Webinar types** | Live, simulive and evergreen | Webinars with scheduled sessions |

## What a countdown timer is {#what-it-is}

A countdown timer is a visible clock that shows the time remaining before something happens. In EasyWebinar it does one of two jobs:

- **On an offer**, it tells attendees how long they have to act on what you are selling: a course enrolment, a coaching call, a bonus, a programme.
- **On a registration page**, it tells visitors how soon the session begins, which turns "sometime" into "in two days".

The offer countdown lives inside the **offer** itself, so it travels wherever that offer is shown. The registration-page countdown is a **block** on the page, so you style and position it like any other part of the page.

## Why countdown timers matter {#why-it-matters}

### What it's for {#what-its-for}

Many selling webinars end on an offer with a real end point: launch pricing, an enrolment window or an in-session bonus. Attendees can act on it only if they know it when they decide. Registration page visitors need the start time just as clearly.

### What happens without it {#without-it}

A common approach is to say the deadline aloud, or to embed a separate timer widget and keep it in step with the offer. Anyone who joins late or steps away misses a spoken deadline. Unsure whether the offer is still open, many people wait, and in webinar selling waiting often means not buying.

### How EasyWebinar helps {#how-it-helps}

On [simulive and evergreen webinars](https://easywebinar.com/automated-webinars/) nobody is presenting, so the deadline has to run without you as well. EasyWebinar puts the deadline on the offer itself: the **Countdown timer** switch in the offer editor adds a countdown to that offer's card.

- **The deadline is on screen.** Attendees see a [visible time limit](https://easywebinar.com/blog/scarcity-tactics-for-webinar-conversions/) instead of relying on what they heard.
- **It's set per offer.** Only offers that really end need a timer.
- **It carries over to the replay.** With **Webinar Replica**, offers appear on the replay page too.
- **The start time counts down as well.** A countdown block on the registration page gives visitors [a reason to register now](https://easywebinar.com/blog/improve-your-webinar-registration-page/).

### Why buyers care {#why-buyers-care}

In the offer editor, **Countdown timer** sits next to **Offer Price**, so the deadline and the price it applies to are set together. That matters most to course creators, coaches and consultants whose pitch rests on [a clear reason to act](https://easywebinar.com/blog/webinar-copywriting-psychological-triggers/).

## Set up an offer countdown {#set-up-offer-countdown}

1. Open your webinar and click the **CTAs** tab.
2. Open an existing offer, or use **+** at the bottom of the list on the left to create one.
3. Switch on **Countdown timer**.
4. Fill in the rest of the offer: **Offer Style** (**Text Offer** or **Image Offer**), **Offer Price** (or switch on **Free**), the **Headline** and **Sub Headline**, and the **Button** text and URL.
5. Check the preview in the centre of the editor. It shows the offer exactly as attendees will see it and updates as you type.
6. Save.

![The offer editor showing style, countdown timer, price, copy and button settings](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/images/ctas/step-03-offer-editor.png)

*The offer editor: 1 Offer Style · 2 Countdown timer · 3 Offer Price · 4 Title, Headline and Sub Headline · 5 Button · 6 Colours.*

### Offer settings that work with the countdown {#offer-settings}

| Setting | What it does | Why it matters with a countdown |
|---|---|---|
| **Offer Style** | Text Offer or Image Offer | Choose the style that keeps the timer easy to see |
| **Offer Price** | A price, or **Free** | A countdown on a priced offer usually signals a price or bonus that ends |
| **Headline** and **Sub Headline** | What attendees read | Say what ends when the timer runs out, in plain words |
| **Button** text and URL | Where the click goes | Point it at the checkout or booking page for exactly this offer |
| **Colours** | Button and button text colour | Keep the button readable next to the timer |

The offer's **Title** is internal. Attendees never see it, so name it in a way that helps your team, for example "Course enrolment, launch week".

### Save without showing it yet {#prepare-in-advance}

You can build the offer, countdown included, before the webinar and hold it back:

- Turn the offer's toggle off in the **CTAs** list, or
- Set **Published** to off in the editor.

The offer stays ready but does not appear until you switch it on.

## Set up the registration-page countdown {#set-up-registration-countdown}

When you [create a live webinar](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/product-docs/webinars/getting-started/create-a-live-webinar/#registration-template), the registration page builder opens with your schedule applied and a countdown to the session already running. On other webinar types, open the page in the builder to check for the countdown block.

![The registration page builder showing the webinar date, a countdown timer and the registration form](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/images/create-live/step-05-registration-page-builder.png)

To change it:

1. Open your webinar and click **Content**, then [**Edit** on **Registration Page**](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/product-docs/webinars/registration/registration-page-builder/#open-the-builder).
2. Click the countdown block on the canvas. An orange toolbar appears above it, and the settings panel on the right fills with its options.
3. Adjust its settings across the **Settings** and **Advanced** tabs.
4. Use the device toggle to check the mobile layout, then **Preview**, **Save** and **Publish**.

### Countdown block settings {#registration-countdown-settings}

| Setting | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Visibility** | Where the block shows, including a **Desktop Only** option |
| **Margins** and **width** | The block's spacing and size on the page |
| **Fonts**, **sizes** and **alignment** | How the date, time and timezone are styled |

> **Publish to make it public**
> Changes to the registration page, including the countdown, only reach visitors after you click **Publish**. **Save** keeps a draft.

## How countdowns work with other EasyWebinar features {#works-with}

### Offers and checkout {#with-offers}

The countdown is part of an offer, so everything about the offer still applies: its price, its button and where that button goes. Point the button at the checkout for exactly the offer the timer describes.

### Replays and replay deadlines {#with-replays}

With **Webinar Replica**, the replay page shows the same video, offers, polls and chat replay as the session, so your offers keep selling after the event. The replay itself can also have a deadline: [**Expire after days**](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/product-docs/webinars/media/replay-pages/#availability) closes it a set number of days after the session, and **Specific date** closes it at a time you choose. A replay deadline and an offer deadline are separate settings; keep them consistent so attendees aren't told two different end times.

### Create with AI {#with-create-with-ai}

**Create with AI** [writes offers for you](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/product-docs/ai/create-with-ai/#generate-offers). Its **Direct Pitch** positioning is "offer-focused, repeated CTAs, strong urgency", while **Soft Pitch** is education-focused with the offer at the end. Whichever you choose, the offers it creates are normal offers on the **CTAs** tab, so you can add a countdown to any of them.

### Analytics {#with-analytics}

After the webinar, open [**Analytics**](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/product-docs/webinars/analytics/webinar-analytics/#engagement-and-offers). **Offers Performance** shows views, clicks and purchases for each offer with a click rate. **Views to Clicks** and **Clicks to Purchases** show where the offer loses people, and **Revenue by Offer** shows what each offer earned. Comparing an offer with and without a countdown across sessions is the clearest way to see what the timer is doing for you.

### Follow-up and CRM tags {#with-follow-up}

People who click an offer can be treated differently afterwards:

- [**Attendee Action Follow-ups**](https://easywebinar.com/features/webinar-follow-up/) on the **Notifications** tab send emails based on what someone did, including whether they clicked an offer.
- In a connected CRM, the [**Clicked offer** event](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/product-docs/integrations/map-webinar-data/#events) can apply a tag, for example `webinar-hot`, so sales can follow up.

## Who uses countdown timers, and how {#who-uses-it}

These are typical patterns, shown to illustrate how the feature fits each kind of webinar.

### Course creators {#course-creators}

An evergreen webinar teaches the core idea of a course and ends with an [enrolment offer](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/product-docs/use-cases/sell-courses/#sell-during-the-webinar). A countdown on the offer marks the window for an enrolment bonus. Because the webinar is automated, the timer carries the deadline for every session without the creator being there.

### Coaches and consultants {#coaches}

A [live masterclass](https://easywebinar.com/webinar-platform-for-coaches-course-creators/) ends with an offer to book a call. The countdown shows how long the booking link stays open after the session, and the registration-page countdown builds attendance in the days before.

### Paid workshops and live events {#paid-workshops}

For a [paid workshop](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/product-docs/webinars/selling/paid-webinars/#paid-workshops), the registration-page countdown shows how long is left to register before the session starts. Inside the workshop, a countdown on an offer can mark a follow-on programme available to attendees only.

### Product and SaaS teams {#product-teams}

A [recorded product demo](https://easywebinar.com/use-cases/product-demos/) ends with an offer for a trial or a call with sales. A countdown is optional here; many teams use it only during launch periods.

## Use countdowns honestly {#use-honestly}

A countdown persuades because people trust it. Keep that trust:

- **Only use a countdown for a real deadline.** The time shown should match what actually happens when it ends.
- **Say what ends.** Use the headline or sub headline to state what expires: the bonus, the price or the enrolment window.
- **Keep deadlines consistent.** Match the offer countdown, the replay deadline and what your follow-up emails say.

## How countdown timers compare {#compare}

EasyWebinar builds in both countdowns: a **Countdown timer** switch on each offer, and a countdown block on the registration page itself. Several tools document only one of the two, or show the start time on a separate waiting page. Some go further in the room, with scarcity counters or timers on polls.

Countdown features in each platform's public documentation:

| Platform | Offers it | What it does | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **EasyWebinar** | Yes | **Countdown timer** switch on each offer; countdown block on the registration page | Offer countdown on live, simulive and evergreen webinars |
| **WebinarJam** | Yes | **Apply urgency** on a product offer shows a countdown that starts when the offer is first displayed | Offers can also show scarcity; the start-time countdown is on a separate holding page |
| **EverWebinar** | Yes | A product offer on the automated timeline has a start and end time, with an optional **Apply urgency** countdown | People who open the link early wait on a countdown page |
| **Demio** | Partly | Countdown at the top of the registration form; waiting-room countdown to the session start | Offer countdown: not in public docs |
| **eWebinar** | Partly | Its interactions guide shows an offer countdown in one example; polls can show a countdown | The start-time countdown comes from widgets on your own landing page |
| **Livestorm** | Partly | Waiting page with a countdown to the start; an in-room Timer app | Offer countdown: not in public docs |

### Where EasyWebinar's version is stronger {#compare-stronger}

- **A documented offer countdown.** EasyWebinar's offer editor has a **Countdown timer** switch. Demio documents its Featured Actions, and Livestorm its in-room link promotion, without a countdown setting. See [EasyWebinar vs Demio](https://easywebinar.com/compare/easywebinar-vs-demio/) and [EasyWebinar vs Livestorm](https://easywebinar.com/compare/easywebinar-vs-livestorm/).
- **The countdown is on the registration page.** EasyWebinar's countdown is a block on the page people sign up on. WebinarJam's help docs describe its countdown on a separate holding page for people who open the live link early. See [EasyWebinar vs WebinarJam and EverWebinar](https://easywebinar.com/compare/easywebinar-vs-everwebinar-webinarjam/).
- **No widget to install elsewhere.** eWebinar's countdown to the start comes from widgets you place on your own landing page. EasyWebinar's is a block in its own page builder. See [EasyWebinar vs eWebinar](https://easywebinar.com/compare/easywebinar-vs-ewebinar/).

### Where other platforms go further {#compare-further}

- **Scarcity alongside the timer.** A WebinarJam offer can also **Apply scarcity**, showing units remaining, and broadcast sales in real time. EasyWebinar's offer card carries a countdown only.
- **Countdowns beyond offers.** eWebinar polls can show a countdown while they're open. Its **Display countdown clock** setting shows the time left in the session. EasyWebinar polls have no countdown setting.
- **A general in-room timer.** Livestorm's Timer app can show a countdown to everyone in the room, not tied to an offer.

## Limits and things to know {#limits}

- **The offer countdown is set per offer.** Each offer has its own **Countdown timer** switch.
- **Polls have no countdown setting.** The **Countdown timer** switch is in the offer editor only.
- **The registration-page countdown is a page block.** It can be hidden on mobile with **Desktop Only** visibility, styled, or removed like any other block.
- **Live-room timers are different.** Once you go live, the [room header shows **LIVE**](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v2/product-docs/webinars/live-room/run-a-live-webinar/#go-live) with a timer of how long you have been broadcasting. That timer counts up and is separate from offer countdowns.

## Common questions {#common-questions}

#### Can I add a countdown to an offer? {#faq-add-countdown}

Yes. Open the offer on the **CTAs** tab and switch on **Countdown timer**.

#### Does every offer need a countdown? {#faq-every-offer}

No. It's optional per offer. Use it when the offer genuinely ends or changes at a set time.

#### Can I add a countdown to a poll? {#faq-poll-countdown}

No. The countdown setting is in the offer editor; the poll editor has question, response options and **Published**.

#### Is there a countdown on my registration page? {#faq-registration-page}

On a new live webinar, yes: the registration page builder opens with a countdown to the session. On other types, check the page in the builder. You can restyle the block, hide it on mobile with **Desktop Only**, or remove it.

#### Will my offers, and their countdowns, show on the replay? {#faq-replay}

Offers carry over to the replay when the replay page uses **Webinar Replica**. Use **Preview** on the replay page to check it looks the way you want.

#### Can I prepare an offer with a countdown before the webinar? {#faq-prepare}

Yes. Build it, then turn its toggle off in the **CTAs** list, or set **Published** to off, until you want it shown.

#### How do I know whether the countdown is helping? {#faq-measure}

Compare sessions in **Analytics → Offers Performance**: views, clicks, purchases and click rate. **Clicks to Purchases** tells you whether people who clicked went on to buy.

#### Does EasyWebinar write the offer for me? {#faq-ai}

It can. **Create with AI** generates offers with a title, headline, sub headline, button text and link, and you can add a countdown to any of them.

#### Do I need a separate countdown tool? {#faq-separate-tool}

No. Both countdowns are built into EasyWebinar.

The Library

Find, add and manage the videos, webinar recordings, images and documents your webinars use, see which files count towards your storage, and put a library video to work in a simulive or evergreen webinar.

Applies to
Your whole account · recordings come from live sessions with Auto Recording on
Status

Verified

Page type
How-to (multi-feature)
Markdown

Use the Library to find and manage everything your webinars play or display: videos you uploaded or imported, recordings of sessions you have already run, images for your pages, and documents. When you're done, you'll know where each item lives, whether it is using your storage allowance, and how to put a video or recording to work in a simulive or evergreen webinar.

Before you begin

  • Find it in the sidebar. The Library sits in the main sidebar, alongside My Webinars, Create with AI and Integrations. It isn't inside a single webinar, so anything you add can be reused across your webinars.
  • Know what uses storage. Only files uploaded to EasyWebinar count towards your storage. Videos imported from a URL use none.
  • Keep live recording on if you want your sessions to land here. Recording is on by default in the live room, where the header reads Auto Recording Enabled.
  • Know your upload limit. Upload video as MP4, up to 4 GB per file. See video and storage limits.

Manage your media

1. Open the Library

Click Library in the sidebar. It has four tabs, and your storage is shown at the top of every one of them.

TabWhat it holds
VideosVideos you added yourself, uploaded from your computer or imported from a URL
RecordingsRecordings of webinars you have already run
ImagesImages you uploaded, for use on your pages
FilesDocuments such as PDFs

2. Check your videos and where each one is hosted

Open the Videos tab. Each card shows the video's duration and when it was added, with a menu for renaming, previewing or removing it.

1 The four tabs · 2 Your storage allowance · 3 A YouTube badge · 4 An EasyWebinar badge. Account details and some thumbnails are blurred.

The small badge in the corner of each card shows where the video is hosted:

BadgeWhat it meansUses your storage?
EasyWebinarThe file is stored on EasyWebinarYes
YouTube, Vimeo or WistiaYou imported a link; the video stays on that platformNo

Check the badge before you delete anything on another platform. Removing a YouTube video breaks the webinar that plays it, while removing an EasyWebinar-hosted video is contained within EasyWebinar.

3. Find recordings of webinars you've run

Open the Recordings tab. Each recording is named after its webinar, with the duration and the date it was recorded.

Recordings of sessions already run, each named after its webinar. Account details are blurred.

Recordings are captured automatically when Auto Recording is on in the live room, and appear here after the session. They also show on the Recordings tab of the video picker when you set up a simulive or evergreen webinar.

4. Add images for your pages

Open the Images tab to see images you've uploaded for your pages. Click Add Media to upload more.

5. Keep documents in Files

Open the Files tab for documents, such as PDFs. Each file shows its name, its upload date and its page count.

The Files tab is also where the AI Moderator's documents live. The AI Moderator is in Beta: it answers attendee questions from documents you give it, reading PDF, DOCX or PPTX files up to 25MB each, and it retrains every time you add a source.

6. Add new media

Click Add Media. It works the same on every tab and offers two routes:

RouteWhat happens
Local importUploads from your computer, and EasyWebinar hosts it
Import from URLPoints at YouTube, Vimeo, Wistia or a direct MP4 link

For an imported video you also enter its length, because EasyWebinar needs the duration to schedule sessions. See Video hosting for both routes step by step.

7. Use a video or recording in a webinar

Simulive and evergreen webinars play a video from your library:

  1. When you create the webinar, the Video Selection step shows your library, filtered by All, Videos or Recordings.
  2. Click the video you want, or use Add Video to add a new one without leaving the step.
  3. To change it later, open the webinar's Content tab and use the Webinar Video panel. Preview plays the current video and Change Video swaps it.

Changing the video doesn't affect the webinar's schedule, pages or registrations.

Verify the result

  • A new upload appears on the Videos tab with the EasyWebinar badge, and your storage figure at the top of the Library goes up.
  • An imported video appears with its platform's badge, and your storage figure doesn't change.
  • A live session you recorded appears on the Recordings tab after the session, named after its webinar.
  • A new document appears on the Files tab with its name, upload date and page count.
  • A library video in use shows in the Webinar Video panel on that webinar's Content tab.

Why the Library matters

What it's for

The Library is the shared store behind your webinars. It is where simulive and evergreen webinars get their content, where finished live sessions end up, and where your storage allowance is spent. Keeping it in one place means a video added once can be used by any webinar that needs it.

How it helps

  • One place for every recording. Live sessions with recording on land in Recordings without any exporting or uploading.
  • Live sessions become reusable content. A recording can be picked as the video for a simulive or evergreen webinar.
  • Storage is visible. The allowance at the top of each tab, and the badge on each video, show what is and isn't using it.
  • Safer deletions. The host badge tells you whether a video depends on another platform before you remove anything.

What happens without it

Without a shared library, every recorded webinar would need its video uploaded again, and recordings of live sessions would have to be downloaded and re-added before you could reuse them. It would also be harder to tell which videos sit on YouTube or another platform, which is how a webinar ends up playing a video someone has since deleted elsewhere.

Why buyers care

Teams that plan to run recorded webinars want to know where the video lives, whether they need a separate video host, and how storage is counted. In EasyWebinar the answers sit in one screen: EasyWebinar can host the file itself, imported links use no storage, and live recordings arrive in the same place ready to reuse.

How it works with other features

Video hosting

Add Media is where you choose between hosting a video on EasyWebinar and importing it from YouTube, Vimeo, Wistia or a direct MP4 link. The choice decides whether the video uses your storage. See Video hosting.

The live room

The live room's header shows Auto Recording Enabled when recording is on, and the badge changes to Recording once you're live. Recording can be changed under Settings → Recording Settings, and recorded sessions land in Library → Recordings. See Run a live webinar.

Turning a live webinar into an evergreen one

A webinar's type can't be changed, so the route from live to automated is cloning. Clone the live webinar, choose Evergreen for the copy, and pick the live session's recording on the new webinar's Content tab. The recording is already in your library. See Clone a webinar.

Simulive and evergreen webinars

Both types play a recording, and both choose it from the library on the Video Selection step. See Create a simulive webinar and Create an evergreen webinar.

Storage and your plan

Under Settings → Manage Plan, the Plan tab has a Storage meter for space used by your videos and recordings, with Buy More, and the Add-ons tab offers Additional Storage (5GB). Both are covered in Your plan, billing and add-ons.

AI Moderator (Beta)

The AI Moderator's knowledge base documents live on the Files tab. Because the moderator retrains with each new source, give it a moment after an upload before you test it.

Who uses it, and how

These are typical patterns, shown to illustrate how the Library fits different kinds of work.

Course creators

A creator runs a launch webinar live with recording on. Afterwards, the session sits in Recordings, and the creator clones the webinar as evergreen and picks that recording, so the version that sold well live keeps running. See selling online courses with webinars.

SaaS and product teams

A product team uploads its demo with Local import rather than publishing it on YouTube, so the video stays private to the webinar. The EasyWebinar badge confirms where it is hosted, and the storage meter shows the space it takes.

Agencies

An agency managing webinars that play videos from a client's YouTube channel checks the badges on the Videos tab before the client tidies up their channel, because removing a YouTube video breaks the webinar that plays it.

Customer success teams

A customer success team keeps its product FAQ and onboarding guides on the Files tab as the AI Moderator's knowledge base (Beta), so questions on an unattended evergreen onboarding webinar can be answered from the team's own documents.

Limits and things to know

  • Only EasyWebinar-hosted files count towards storage. Imported URLs use none.
  • Upload video as MP4, up to 4 GB. The Video Selection screen lists MP4, MOV or WebM, but the Upload media dialog says it supports MP4 files only, so upload MP4 to be safe.
  • More storage is an add-on. Additional Storage (5GB), or Buy More beside the storage meter.
  • Recordings appear only if recording was on. Recording is on by default; check the room header before you go live.
  • Don't delete a video a webinar is using. The webinar plays it.
  • Removing a video on YouTube, Vimeo or Wistia breaks any webinar that plays the imported link.
  • AI Moderator documents are PDF, DOCX or PPTX, up to 25MB each, and the AI Moderator is in Beta.

Common questions

Where are my webinar recordings?

On the Recordings tab of the Library. Each is named after its webinar, with its duration and recording date.

Why isn't my live session in Recordings?

Recordings are captured when Auto Recording is on in the live room. If the header didn't read Auto Recording Enabled, the session wasn't recorded.

How can I tell if a video is hosted on EasyWebinar or YouTube?

By the badge on its card: EasyWebinar, or the platform's own logo.

Do imported videos use my storage?

No. Only files uploaded to EasyWebinar count towards your storage.

How much storage do I have?

It's shown at the top of every Library tab. More is available with the Additional Storage (5GB) add-on or Buy More.

Can I use a recording as the content for an evergreen webinar?

Yes. Pick it from the Recordings tab when choosing the video, or swap it in later from the Webinar Video panel.

Can I delete a video a webinar is using?

You shouldn't. The webinar plays that video, so removing it leaves the webinar without its content.

Where do I upload documents?

On the Files tab. It's also where the AI Moderator (Beta) keeps the documents it answers from.

What file types can the AI Moderator read?

PDF, DOCX or PPTX, up to 25MB per file. The AI Moderator is in Beta.

Can I rename a video?

Yes. Each video card has a menu for renaming, previewing or removing it.

# Create with AI

How EasyWebinar's Create with AI builds a webinar from a description, writing the script, registration pages, email sequence, polls and offers, and what each step costs in credits.

- Applies to: Live, simulive and evergreen webinars
- Status: Live
- Verified: 2026-10-16
- Canonical page: https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/product-docs/ai/create-with-ai/

**Create with AI** builds a complete EasyWebinar webinar from a written description. In five steps it writes a [slide-by-slide presentation script](https://easywebinar.com/ai-webinar-script-generator/), fills your [registration, thank-you and waiting-area pages](https://easywebinar.com/ai-webinar-registration-page-builder/), drafts the [email sequence](https://easywebinar.com/ai-webinar-email-generator/), and creates polls and offers. A full build takes about ten minutes and uses around 23 credits, and everything it produces opens in the normal editors so you can change it.

## What Create with AI is {#what-it-is}

Building a webinar by hand means writing a presentation, designing a registration page, writing a confirmation, reminders and follow-ups, and thinking up polls and an offer. Create with AI does a first draft of all of that from one prompt.

It works in sequence, one step after another. Your prompt becomes a script, and the AI then uses that script as source material when it writes your pages. That ordering matters: the script is the foundation, so getting it right first improves everything built after it.

The result is a normal EasyWebinar webinar. It has the same nine tabs and the same settings as one built by hand. The only visible difference is the **AI** badge on its card in **My Webinars**.

## Why Create with AI matters {#why-it-matters}

### What it's for {#what-its-for}

Create with AI is for getting [from an idea to a webinar funnel](https://easywebinar.com/ai-webinar-funnel-builder/) you can run, without starting each piece from a blank page. It suits a first webinar, a new topic, or a team that needs a launch-ready draft quickly.

### How it helps {#how-it-helps}

- **One prompt, five deliverables.** Script, pages, emails, polls and offers all start from the same description.
- **The script is presentation-ready.** It's written slide by slide in named sections, each with a timing and speaker notes, and downloads as a PDF.
- **Emails arrive wired up.** The sequence comes with merge tags already in place, so dates, times and join links fill themselves in.
- **Polls have a purpose.** You choose what the polls should achieve, and each one comes with an explanation of why it's there and where it fits in the run of show.
- **Offers match your pitch.** You choose how hard to sell, and the offers come back complete with a headline, button and link.

### What happens without it {#without-it}

Without it, the setup work is spread across several blank pages: a script document, a page builder, five or six emails and an offer. That's often where webinars stall, especially for a first session, and the pieces written on different days tend not to match each other.

### Why buyers care {#why-buyers-care}

For a coach, course creator or small team, the hours before a first webinar are the main barrier to running one. Buyers want to know how much of the setup a platform can draft for them, whether the output is editable, and what it costs to generate. Create with AI answers all three: five deliverables, all editable, with a visible credit cost per step.

## Before you begin {#before-you-begin}

- **Check your credits.** [AI generation uses credits](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/product-docs/reference/limits-and-defaults/#ai-credits), and your balance is shown at the top right of every step. A full build costs around 23.
- **Know your audience and offer.** The prompt drives everything. Have a specific audience, three or four teaching points, a length, a tone, and what you'll sell at the end ready before you start.
- **Decide the webinar type.** Choose **Live**, **Simulive** or **Evergreen** before generating. A [webinar's type](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/product-docs/webinars/#which-to-choose) can't be changed after it's created.

## Build a webinar with AI {#build-it}

### 1. Describe your webinar {#describe-it}

Click **Create with AI** in the sidebar.

![The Create with AI screen with the prompt box, the Type selector, the output chips, Generate and the credit balance marked](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/images/create-with-ai/step-01-prompt.png)

| | Control | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Prompt box** | Describe the webinar you want |
| 2 | **Type** | Live, Simulive or Evergreen |
| 3 | **Output chips** | Script, Landing pages, Emails, Polls, Offers |
| 4 | **Generate** | Starts the build |
| 5 | **Credits** | Your remaining balance |

Script, Landing pages and Emails are on by default. Click **Polls** and **Offers** to include them. Turn off any chip you don't want generated.

#### Write a prompt that works {#write-a-good-prompt}

Output quality depends on the prompt more than anything else. A vague prompt produces a generic webinar. Include:

- **Who it's for, specifically.** "Experienced freelance designers stuck at capacity", not "designers".
- **What it covers.** The three or four things you'll actually teach.
- **How long it runs.**
- **The tone you want.**
- **What you're selling at the end,** and the price.

For example: a 45-minute live masterclass for experienced freelance designers who want recurring revenue without hiring, covering why hourly billing caps growth, how to turn a repeatable service into a fixed-scope offer, and how to price it. Warm and practical, no hype. End with an offer for a six-week group programme with templates and weekly calls, plus its price.

Click **Generate**.

### 2. Review the script {#review-the-script}

You're asked for the number of **slides** and the **duration**, then the script is written slide by slide.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Webinar topic** | Your prompt, still editable |
| **Slides and duration** | Change either and regenerate |
| **Regenerate** | Writes a fresh script |
| **Download PDF** | Saves the script to present from |

The script is organised into named sections, such as the hook and welcome and the problem framing, each with its own timing and speaker notes. Read it before you continue. Fixing the script now is much easier than fixing the pages and emails built from it. Click **Continue to landing pages**.

### 3. Generate the pages {#generate-pages}

Choose **AI picks for you** to have a template selected automatically, or **Choose manually** to browse templates yourself.

The AI writes your registration page, thank-you page and waiting area, using the script as source material: headline, what attendees will learn, an instructor bio and the rest. The pages open in an editor with **Edit**, **Preview**, **SEO** and **Scripts** tabs and a desktop and mobile view. Treat them as a strong first draft. Click **Continue**.

### 4. Generate the emails {#generate-emails}

The AI drafts a full sequence:

| Email | When it sends |
|---|---|
| Welcome email | On registration |
| Reminder — 1d | A day before |
| Reminder — 1h | An hour before |
| Reminder — 15m | Fifteen minutes before |
| Follow-up — Instant | Right after the webinar ends |
| Follow-up — 1d | A day afterwards |

Merge tags are already in place, so dates, times and join links fill in automatically. **Add email** adds another. Click **Continue**.

### 5. Generate the polls {#generate-polls}

![Poll generation asking what the polls should help you accomplish, with seven goals and Generate polls](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/images/create-with-ai/step-03-poll-goals.png)

Instead of asking what questions you want, this step asks what the polls are for. Choose any combination of goals:

- increasing engagement
- understanding pain points
- gauging interest in your offer
- qualifying leads
- gathering background
- getting feedback on delivery
- segmenting for follow-up

Click **Generate polls**. You get [five polls](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/product-docs/webinars/engagement/polls/#polls-with-ai), each with its question, its options, and a short note on why it's there and where it fits in the run of show. Click **Continue**.

### 6. Generate the offers {#generate-offers}

![Offer generation with the three positioning options, a description box for the offer and a field for the button destination](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/images/create-with-ai/step-04-offer-positioning.png)

First choose how you want to position the offer:

| Positioning | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Soft Pitch** | Education-focused, offer at the end |
| **Balanced Approach** | Value plus a clear offer throughout, strong close |
| **Direct Pitch** | Offer-focused, repeated CTAs, strong urgency |

Then describe what you're selling and where the button should send people, and pick an offer category: online course, coaching programme, membership, digital product or service package. Click **Generate Offers**.

You get [complete offers](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/product-docs/webinars/engagement/timed-offers/#offers-with-ai) with a title, headline, sub-headline, button text and link. Select the ones you want and click **Save and continue**.

### 7. Land on your finished webinar {#finished-webinar}

![The finished webinar's Overview tab, with the AI badge beside the title and the nine tabs marked](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/images/create-with-ai/step-05-finished.png)

*1 The webinar title, with its AI badge · 2 The nine tabs.*

The flow ends on the new webinar's **Overview**. From here it works exactly like a webinar built by hand.

## What it costs {#credits}

| Step | Credits |
|---|---|
| Script | 10 |
| Landing pages | 10 |
| Emails | 1 |
| Polls | 1 |
| Offers | 1 |
| **Full build** | **23** |

The script and pages are the expensive steps, which is another reason to get the prompt right before you generate. Each step shows your balance before you commit, and you can switch off any output chip to skip that part.

## After generating: finish the webinar {#after-generating}

Create with AI drafts the content. A few things are still yours to do:

- **Set the schedule.** The build doesn't schedule sessions for you; [set them on the Overview tab](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/product-docs/webinars/setup/overview-tab/#scheduling-options).
- **[Choose a video](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/product-docs/webinars/media/video-hosting/#change-video)** for simulive and evergreen webinars, which play a recording.
- **Read every page and email** before publishing, especially anything about price and what's included.
- **[Publish the pages](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/product-docs/webinars/registration/registration-page-builder/#publish).** A registration link doesn't work until its page is published.
- **Tune the offers.** Add a [countdown timer](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/product-docs/webinars/engagement/countdown-timers/) to an offer with a real deadline, or adjust the button URL.

## How Create with AI works with other features {#works-with}

### Pages {#with-pages}

Generated pages open in the same drag-and-drop builder as any other, on the webinar's **Content** tab. Change templates, edit blocks, add **UTM Params** to the form, then publish. See [Registration page builder](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/product-docs/webinars/registration/registration-page-builder/).

### Emails {#with-emails}

The generated sequence lives on the **Notifications** tab with every other email, where you can change subjects, content and timing. See [Confirmation emails](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/product-docs/webinars/notifications/confirmation-emails/) and [Reminder emails](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/product-docs/webinars/notifications/reminder-emails/).

### Polls and offers {#with-polls-and-offers}

Generated polls and offers appear on the **CTAs** tab, already written and editable like any other. See [Polls](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/product-docs/webinars/engagement/polls/) and [Offers](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/product-docs/webinars/engagement/timed-offers/).

### Paid webinars {#with-paid-webinars}

A webinar built with AI has the same pricing options as one built by hand. If you're charging for registration, read the generated pages and emails carefully so they describe the price and what's included correctly. See [Paid webinars](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/product-docs/webinars/selling/paid-webinars/).

### Other AI features {#other-ai-features}

Create with AI is one of several [AI features in EasyWebinar](https://easywebinar.com/ai/). **Chat Simulation** can use AI to populate the chat on a recorded webinar; see [Chat simulation](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/product-docs/webinars/engagement/chat-simulation/). ## Who uses Create with AI, and how {#who-uses-it}

These are typical patterns, shown to illustrate how the feature fits different users.

### Course creators {#course-creators}

A creator describes a training webinar that teaches the core idea of a course, chooses the online course category and **Balanced Approach** for the offer, and edits the script before generating pages. See [Course creators](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-v3/product-docs/use-cases/sell-courses/).

### Coaches and consultants {#coaches}

A [coach launching a new programme](https://easywebinar.com/webinar-platform-for-coaches-course-creators/) generates the whole webinar in one sitting, downloads the script as a PDF to rehearse from, and picks **Soft Pitch** because the audience is new to them.

### Small businesses and solo operators {#small-business}

A solo operator without time to write six emails keeps the default chips (script, pages and emails), leaves **Polls** and **Offers** off, and builds a single offer by hand on the **CTAs** tab.

### SaaS and product teams {#product-teams}

A product team sets **Type** to **Evergreen** for an [always-on product walkthrough](https://easywebinar.com/evergreen-webinars/), chooses poll goals for qualifying leads and segmenting for follow-up, then attaches their recorded demo as the video.

## Limits and things to know {#limits}

- **Credits are spent per step.** About 23 for a full build; the script and pages are 10 each.
- **The prompt sets the quality.** Vague prompts produce generic webinars.
- **Scheduling isn't generated.** Set sessions yourself after the build.
- **Simulive and evergreen still need a video** after generation.
- **Five polls per poll step.** Each comes with its question, options and reasoning.
- **Everything is a draft.** Check pages and emails before you publish, particularly prices and inclusions.
- **The webinar type is fixed** once the webinar is created, so set **Type** before generating.

## Common questions {#common-questions}

#### How long does Create with AI take? {#faq-how-long}

About ten minutes end to end. The script is the slowest part, at roughly a minute and a half.

#### How many credits does it use? {#faq-credits}

Around 23 for all five parts: 10 for the script, 10 for the pages, and 1 each for emails, polls and offers.

#### Can I generate only some parts? {#faq-some-parts}

Yes. Turn off any of the five output chips before you click **Generate**.

#### Can I edit what the AI produced? {#faq-edit}

Yes, all of it. Pages open in the page builder, emails on the **Notifications** tab, and polls and offers on the **CTAs** tab.

#### Can I regenerate just the script? {#faq-regenerate-script}

Yes. Use **Regenerate** on the script step. Change the number of slides or the duration first if you want a different result.

#### Does it work for simulive and evergreen webinars? {#faq-types}

Yes. Set **Type** before generating, then choose a video once the webinar is built.

#### Can I present from the script? {#faq-present-script}

Yes. **Download PDF** on the script step saves it as a PDF.

#### How do I tell which webinars were built with AI? {#faq-ai-badge}

They carry an **AI** badge on their card in **My Webinars**.

#### What makes a good prompt? {#faq-good-prompt}

Be specific about the audience, what you'll cover, how long it runs, the tone, and what you're selling.

#### How hard will the generated offer sell? {#faq-offer-positioning}

You choose: **Soft Pitch**, **Balanced Approach** or **Direct Pitch**.

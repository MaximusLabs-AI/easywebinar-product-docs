What Create with AI is

Building a webinar by hand means writing a presentation, designing a registration page, writing a confirmation, reminders and follow-ups, and thinking up polls and an offer. Create with AI does a first draft of all of that from one prompt.

It works in sequence, one step after another. Your prompt becomes a script, and the AI then uses that script as source material when it writes your pages. That ordering matters: the script is the foundation, so getting it right first improves everything built after it.

The result is a normal EasyWebinar webinar. It has the same nine tabs and the same settings as one built by hand. The only visible difference is the AI badge on its card in My Webinars.

Why Create with AI matters

What it's for

Create with AI is for getting from an idea to a webinar funnel you can run, without starting each piece from a blank page. It suits a first webinar, a new topic, or a team that needs a launch-ready draft quickly.

How it helps

  • One prompt, five deliverables. Script, pages, emails, polls and offers all start from the same description.
  • The script is presentation-ready. It comes in named sections, each with a timing and speaker notes, and downloads as a PDF.
  • Emails arrive wired up. Merge tags are already in place, so dates, times and join links fill themselves in.
  • Polls have a purpose. You choose what the polls should achieve. Each one explains why it's there and where it fits.
  • Offers match your pitch. You choose how hard to sell, and each offer comes back with a headline, button and link.

What happens without it

Without it, the setup work is spread across several blank pages: a script document, a page builder, five or six emails and an offer. That's often where webinars stall, especially a first one. Pieces written on different days also tend not to match each other.

Why buyers care

Create with AI matters most to coaches, course creators and small teams who run their own marketing. It matters in the week before a first webinar, when the setup hours are the main thing standing between an idea and a date.

The EasyWebinar approach to Create with AI

Take a typical case. A solo marketing consultant who helps local accountancy firms win clients plans her first webinar. It's a 40-minute live session on getting referrals through LinkedIn, two weeks away. She has about 400 contacts and wants to end with an invitation to her three-month advisory programme.

A common workaround is to build each piece in a different tool: slides in one, a landing page in another, emails from a template. That can take several evenings. The pieces may drift apart as they're written, and a first webinar can slip while the setup drags on.

The pieces don't really need separate starting points. The script, the page, the emails, the polls and the offer all come from the same idea. If that idea is written down once, each piece can be drafted from it, and her time goes into editing instead.

EasyWebinar turns her description into a complete webinar through Create with AI. She sets Type to Live, adds the Polls and Offers chips and clicks Generate. The script comes first, and the pages are written from it. She chooses Balanced Approach for the offer and downloads the script as a PDF to rehearse.

Before you begin

  • Check your credits. AI generation uses credits, and your balance is shown at the top right of every step. A full build costs around 23.
  • Know your audience and offer. The prompt drives everything. Have a specific audience, three or four teaching points, a length, a tone, and what you'll sell at the end ready before you start.
  • Decide the webinar type. Choose Live, Simulive or Evergreen before generating. A webinar's type can't be changed after it's created.

Build a webinar with AI

1. Describe your webinar

Click Create with AI in the sidebar.

ControlWhat it does
1Prompt boxDescribe the webinar you want
2TypeLive, Simulive or Evergreen
3Output chipsScript, Landing pages, Emails, Polls, Offers
4GenerateStarts the build
5CreditsYour remaining balance

Script, Landing pages and Emails are on by default. Click Polls and Offers to include them. Turn off any chip you don't want generated.

Write a prompt that works

Output quality depends on the prompt more than anything else. A vague prompt produces a generic webinar. Include:

  • Who it's for, specifically. "Experienced freelance designers stuck at capacity", not "designers".
  • What it covers. The three or four things you'll actually teach.
  • How long it runs.
  • The tone you want.
  • What you're selling at the end, and the price.

For example: a 45-minute live masterclass for experienced freelance designers who want recurring revenue without hiring, covering why hourly billing caps growth, how to turn a repeatable service into a fixed-scope offer, and how to price it. Warm and practical, no hype. End with an offer for a six-week group programme with templates and weekly calls, plus its price.

Click Generate.

2. Review the script

You're asked for the number of slides and the duration, then the script is written slide by slide.

ControlWhat it does
Webinar topicYour prompt, still editable
Slides and durationChange either and regenerate
RegenerateWrites a fresh script

The script is organised into named sections, such as the hook and welcome and the problem framing, each with its own timing and speaker notes. Read it before you continue. Fixing the script now is much easier than fixing the pages and emails built from it. Click Continue to landing pages.

3. Generate the pages

Choose AI picks for you to have a template selected automatically, or Choose manually to browse templates yourself.

The AI writes your registration page, thank-you page and waiting area, using the script as source material: headline, what attendees will learn, an instructor bio and the rest. The pages open in an editor with Edit, Preview, SEO and Scripts tabs and a desktop and mobile view. Treat them as a strong first draft. Click Continue.

4. Generate the emails

The AI drafts a full sequence:

EmailWhen it sends
Welcome emailOn registration
Reminder — 1dA day before
Reminder — 1hAn hour before
Reminder — 15mFifteen minutes before
Follow-up — InstantRight after the webinar ends
Follow-up — 1dA day afterwards

Merge tags are already in place, so dates, times and join links fill in automatically. Add email adds another. Click Continue.

5. Generate the polls

Instead of asking what questions you want, this step asks what the polls are for. Choose any combination of goals:

  • increasing engagement
  • understanding pain points
  • gauging interest in your offer
  • qualifying leads
  • gathering background
  • getting feedback on delivery
  • segmenting for follow-up

Click Generate polls. You get five polls, each with its question, its options, and a short note on why it's there and where it fits in the run of show. Click Continue.

6. Generate the offers

First choose how you want to position the offer:

PositioningWhat it does
Soft PitchEducation-focused, offer at the end
Balanced ApproachValue plus a clear offer throughout, strong close
Direct PitchOffer-focused, repeated CTAs, strong urgency

Then describe what you're selling and where the button should send people, and pick an offer category: online course, coaching programme, membership, digital product or service package. Click Generate Offers.

You get complete offers with a title, headline, sub-headline, button text and link. Select the ones you want and click Save and continue.

7. Land on your finished webinar

The flow ends on the new webinar's Overview. From here it works exactly like a webinar built by hand.

After generating: finish the webinar

Create with AI drafts the content. A few things are still yours to do:

  • Set the schedule. The build doesn't schedule sessions for you; set them on the Overview tab.
  • Choose a video for simulive and evergreen webinars, which play a recording.
  • Read every page and email before publishing, especially anything about price and what's included.
  • Publish the pages. A registration link doesn't work until its page is published.
  • Tune the offers. Add a countdown timer to an offer with a real deadline, or adjust the button URL.

How Create with AI works with other features

Pages

Generated pages open in the same drag-and-drop builder as any other, on the webinar's Content tab. Change templates, edit blocks, add UTM Params to the form, then publish. See Registration page builder.

Emails

The generated sequence lives on the Notifications tab with every other email, where you can change subjects, content and timing. See Confirmation emails and Reminder emails.

Polls and offers

Generated polls and offers appear on the CTAs tab, already written and editable like any other. See Polls and Offers.

Paid webinars

A webinar built with AI has the same pricing options as one built by hand. If you're charging for registration, read the generated pages and emails carefully so they describe the price and what's included correctly. See Paid webinars.

Who uses Create with AI, and how

Course creators

Limits and things to know

  • Credits are spent per step. About 23 for a full build; the script and pages are 10 each.
  • The prompt sets the quality. Vague prompts produce generic webinars.
  • Scheduling isn't generated. Set sessions yourself after the build.
  • Simulive and evergreen still need a video after generation.
  • Five polls per poll step. Each comes with its question, options and reasoning.
  • Everything is a draft. Check pages and emails before you publish, particularly prices and inclusions.
  • The webinar type is fixed once the webinar is created, so set Type before generating.

Common questions

How long does Create with AI take?

About ten minutes end to end. The script is the slowest part, at roughly a minute and a half.

About 10 to 15 minutes end to end. The script is the slowest part, at roughly a minute and a half.

How many credits does it use?

Around 23 for all five parts: 10 for the script, 10 for the pages, and 1 each for emails, polls and offers.

Can I generate only some parts?

Yes. Turn off any of the five output chips before you click Generate.

Can I edit what the AI produced?

Yes, all of it. Pages open in the page builder, emails on the Notifications tab, and polls and offers on the CTAs tab.

Can I regenerate just the script?

Yes. Use Regenerate on the script step. Change the number of slides or the duration first if you want a different result.

Does it work for simulive and evergreen webinars?

Yes. Set Type before generating, then choose a video once the webinar is built.

Can I present from the script?

Yes. Download PDF on the script step saves it as a PDF.

How do I tell which webinars were built with AI?

They carry an AI badge on their card in My Webinars.

What makes a good prompt?

Be specific about the audience, what you'll cover, how long it runs, the tone, and what you're selling.

How hard will the generated offer sell?

You choose: Soft Pitch, Balanced Approach or Direct Pitch.

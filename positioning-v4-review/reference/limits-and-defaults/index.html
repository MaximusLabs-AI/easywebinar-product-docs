Launch50Starts at Growth

Capacities as shown in the plan chooser. Plans are billed monthly or yearly. The Plan tab under Settings meters live attendees, automated attendees and storage.

  • Live attendees counts people joining live sessions in the current period.
  • Automated attendees counts people watching simulive and evergreen sessions.
  • Usage resets with your billing period, so the meters show the current period only.

Check the meters before a big promotion. Running out of live attendee capacity mid-launch is an avoidable problem, and add-ons for high-traffic webinars covers planning for a large audience.

Check the meters before a big promotion. Your webinar never stops at your plan's limit: if more people join, the session keeps running and extra attendees are billed as overage under our terms. Add-ons for high-traffic webinars covers planning for a large audience.

EasyCRM sending allowance

ChannelIncluded each month
Emails1,000
SMS100 messages
Call minutes100

This allowance applies only if you use EasyCRM. It's your CRM or ours: keep HubSpot, Salesforce Pardot (Account Engagement), ActiveCampaign, Mailchimp or Kit (formerly ConvertKit), or use EasyCRM natively.

Everything EasyCRM sends draws on this monthly allowance, and its Wallet screen shows what's left. The allowance resets on your signup date, not the first of the month. Broadcasts and workflows draw on the same allowance, and each channel is counted separately.

Add-ons

Every plan includes live webinars with browser join. Automation, EasyCRM, checkout and EasyCast start at Growth. AI Moderator answers attendee questions from Pro onward.

Add-ons extend your plan without changing it. Switch one on with Add to plan on the Add-ons tab under Settings → Manage Plan.

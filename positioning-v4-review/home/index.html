What EasyWebinar is

EasyWebinar is a webinar platform for running live and recorded webinars that collect leads and sell. You build a webinar with a registration page, emails, polls and offers, run it live or from a recording, and follow up through analytics, integrations and the built-in EasyCRM.

EasyWebinar is the webinar platform built for conversions: live, automated and on demand. You build a webinar with a registration page, emails, polls and offers, run it live or from a recording, and follow up through analytics and your CRM or ours. Keep HubSpot, Salesforce Pardot (Account Engagement), ActiveCampaign, Mailchimp or Kit (formerly ConvertKit), or use the built-in EasyCRM natively.

How the parts connect

A webinar's registration page collects registrants and can take payment through your own Stripe account. Registrants get reminder emails from the Notifications tab, and SMS or WhatsApp messages, both currently in Beta. They join the room live, or a recording plays at a scheduled time (simulive) or on demand (evergreen). Attendance, offer clicks and revenue flow into the webinar's Analytics and the People view, and out to connected CRMs and email tools, or into EasyCRM.

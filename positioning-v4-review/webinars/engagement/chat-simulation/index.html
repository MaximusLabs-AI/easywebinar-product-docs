Chat simulation

Fill the chat on a simulive or evergreen webinar with a conversation generated by AI from a cast of simulated attendees, or scripted by you in a timed CSV.

Chat simulation plays a conversation in the chat of a recorded webinar, so a session doesn't open to an empty room. In EasyWebinar you set it up on a simulive or evergreen webinar's Engagement tab, under Chat Simulation, in one of two ways. AI Simulation builds a cast of simulated attendees and generates their chat and questions. Manual Simulation plays a script you write in a CSV, with each message timed against your video. The Chat Simulation section carries a Beta label in the app.

What chat simulation is

A live webinar has a shared room: everyone is there at the same time, so the chat fills itself. An evergreen webinar doesn't. Each registrant picks their own session, attendees arrive at different times, and a genuinely shared chat isn't possible. Without anything in it, the chat panel looks abandoned.

Chat simulation solves that by playing messages into the chat at set points in the video. You choose how those messages are made:

  • AI Simulation: you describe a cast of attendees, and the AI generates what they say.
  • Manual Simulation: you write every line yourself, with a timestamp, a name and a role.

Only one can be active on a webinar at a time.

Why chat simulation matters

What it's for

Chat simulation gives a recorded webinar the feel of an attended session. A greeting in the first seconds, a question a few minutes in and a host reply tell a newcomer the room is active. That impression keeps people watching long enough for the content to work.

How it helps

  • The room never opens empty. Messages can appear within seconds of the video starting.
  • It plays in every session. On an evergreen webinar that runs all day, the same setup plays each time without you.
  • You choose how much control to keep. AI Simulation fills a room naturally, while Manual Simulation plays exactly what you wrote.
  • Common questions get answered in view. A scripted question with an Admin reply puts an objection and its answer in front of everyone.

What happens without it

On a recorded webinar with an empty chat, the first impression is a room nobody else came to. Attendees already suspect they may be watching a recording, and a silent panel confirms it. Many leave before the content has had a chance to work.

Why buyers care

It matters to anyone who relies on evergreen webinars inside a funnel, where nobody presents live. Those teams need the automated session to hold attention the way a live one does, most of all when it plays many times a day.

The EasyWebinar approach to chat simulation

Take a typical case. A novelist is launching a monthly online book club for readers of literary fiction. She records a 30-minute introduction to the club and runs it as an evergreen webinar, linked from her newsletter. She expects around 40 viewers a day, spread across several sessions.

A common approach is to leave the chat on and hope viewers start talking. In practice, people who arrive alone tend to stay quiet, so each one sees an empty panel. Questions about reading pace and meeting times may go unasked, and some readers leave unsure whether the club suits them.

Nothing about the video has to change. What helps is a chat that raises the questions readers usually ask, at the moment the video covers them, with an answer from the host. A room with a few voices also reads as a club that people actually join.

EasyWebinar handles this with Chat Simulation (Beta) on the webinar's Engagement tab. The author picks Manual Simulation and uploads a CSV of timed messages. In the script, Attendee lines ask about pace and meeting times, and Admin lines answer as the host. Preview Simulated Chat shows the whole conversation before she saves.

The screenshot under Set up Manual Simulation shows the CSV upload beside Preview Simulated Chat and Attendees on Event Page. How other platforms handle simulated chat is covered in How chat simulation compares, below.

Before you begin

  • Use a simulive or evergreen webinar. Chat simulation isn't available on live webinars.
  • Select Easywebinar Chat on Engagement → Chat. Simulated chat works with EasyWebinar's own chat panel; Third party replaces it.
  • Know your video. For a manual script, you'll time messages against the recording, so have it to hand.

Set up AI Simulation

  1. Open your simulive or evergreen webinar and click Engagement, then Chat Simulation.
  2. Under AI Simulation, use Add New to create a simulation, or pick a saved one from the selector.
  3. Turn the simulation on with its Active toggle. This disables Manual Simulation.
  4. Review the attendee list and adjust each simulated attendee.
  5. Click Add Attendee to add more people to the cast.
  6. Check the counts for attendees, chat messages and questions.
  7. Click Save Changes.

Build a believable cast

The mix of attendees decides whether the chat reads as real. A room full of enthusiastic experts posting constantly looks staged. A few beginners asking basic questions, one sceptic, a couple of quiet observers on low engagement and some mid-level regulars looks like an audience.

Build a natural cast

The mix of attendees decides whether the chat reads naturally. A room full of enthusiastic experts posting constantly looks staged. A few beginners asking basic questions, one sceptic, a couple of quiet observers on low engagement and some mid-level regulars reads like a natural conversation.

A useful check: look down the attendee list as a newcomer would meet that room. If almost everyone is an enthusiastic expert on high engagement, add curious and sceptical attendees, and move some of the most active ones to Medium or Low.

Set up Manual Simulation

  1. Open your simulive or evergreen webinar and click Engagement, then Chat Simulation.
  2. Select Manual Simulation and make it active. This disables AI Simulation.
  3. Click Download sample CSV to get the template.
  4. Write your script in the four columns and save the file as CSV (UTF-8).
  5. Drag the file onto the upload area, or use Choose File.
  6. Add the names for Attendees on Event Page, separated by commas.
  7. Read through Preview Simulated Chat.
  8. Click Save Changes.

Write a script that reads like a real room

  • Line messages up with the video. A question about a slide should land while that slide is on screen.
  • Open early. A message in the first few seconds tells new arrivals the room is active.
  • Mix the roles. Attendee questions answered by Admin replies read as a conversation. Attendee messages alone read as a wall.
  • Let it breathe. Real chat comes in bursts with quiet stretches, not one message every thirty seconds.
  • Check the preview. It shows timestamps, names and roles as attendees will see them.

Use simulated chat honestly

Simulated chat is there to stop an automated room looking empty. It isn't a place for social proof that didn't happen.

Simulated chat keeps an automated room from feeling empty. It doesn't make the webinar live, so never present it as live, and it isn't a place for social proof that didn't happen.

  • No invented testimonials. Simulated attendees shouldn't claim results or endorse your product.
  • Don't impersonate real customers. A simulated message shouldn't pass itself off as something a real customer said.
  • Keep host answers accurate. An Admin reply is read as your answer, so it should be one you'd give live.

How chat simulation works with other features

The Chat setting

With Easywebinar Chat selected, a recorded webinar's Chat screen offers Simulated Chat, which plays back a pre-written chat during the webinar, and Simulated Attendees, which shows a simulated attendee list in the room. The script, attendee count and names are set on the Chat Simulation tab. Choosing Third party replaces the built-in chat, so simulation stops applying.

The attendee list

Simulated attendees can show in the attendee list too, with Show attendee list on event page. The webinar-wide Show Attendees setting on the Settings tab controls whether attendees see the list at all.

Q&A

For an automated webinar that should still feel busy, pair Everyone chat and Chat Simulation with Private Q&A, so real questions reach your team in a queue.

Evergreen schedules

Chat simulation suits evergreen webinars running just-in-time or on demand sessions, where attendees arrive in ones and twos at all hours.

Simulive webinars

On a simulive webinar, everyone in a session watches the same moment, so real chat works and you can answer live while the recording plays. Chat Simulation is available on simulive webinars as well as evergreen ones.

Who uses chat simulation, and how

These are typical patterns, to show how the two modes fit different automated webinars.

Course creators

A creator turns their best live session into an evergreen webinar and writes a manual script from the questions people really asked live, with Admin replies that match the answers they gave. Every session plays the same tested conversation.

Small businesses and solo operators

A solo operator without time to script uses AI Simulation, builds a cast with a mix of beginners, a sceptic and quieter observers, and reviews the cast before turning the simulation on.

SaaS and product teams

A product team runs an on-demand demo. A manual script places a short exchange about integrations at the point in the video where integrations come up, so the answer appears just as viewers are wondering.

Agencies

An agency building evergreen funnels for several clients uses AI Simulation to set up each room quickly, then switches individual webinars to Manual Simulation once a client wants a fixed, approved script.

Limits and things to know

  • Recorded webinars only. Live webinars have real chat.
  • One mode at a time. Turning on AI Simulation disables Manual Simulation, and the reverse.
  • It needs the built-in chat. Third-party chat replaces EasyWebinar's panel.
  • AI chat varies between sessions; a manual script doesn't.
  • CSV files must be UTF-8, with commas inside messages wrapped in double quotes.
  • Timestamps run from the start of the video, not from the clock time of the session.
  • Changes apply when you click Save Changes.
  • It's labelled Beta in the app.

Common questions

Why would I simulate chat?

On an automated webinar, attendees arrive at different times, so a genuine shared chat isn't possible. An empty panel makes the room look deserted.

Does chat simulation work on a live webinar?

No. Live webinars have real chat.

Can I use AI and manual simulation together?

No. Turning one on disables the other.

What format does the CSV need?

Four columns, HH:MM:SS,Name,Role,Message, saved as UTF-8 CSV. Download sample CSV gives you the exact layout.

What roles can I use in the script?

Attendee and Admin. Admin messages appear as host replies.

When do scripted messages appear?

At the timestamp you set, measured from the start of the video.

Can I see the chat before attendees do?

Yes. Preview Simulated Chat shows the conversation exactly as it will appear in the room.

What can I set for each AI-simulated attendee?

Name and country, Engagement, Knowledge, Language and Behavior.

Will the AI chat be the same in every session?

No. AI Simulation varies per session. A manual script is identical every time.

Can simulated attendees appear in the attendee list?

Yes, with Show attendee list on event page.

Does it work with a third-party chat tool?

No. Simulated chat works with Easywebinar Chat only.

The three webinar types

You choose a type when you create a webinar. The type can't be changed afterwards, so it is worth deciding first.

Live

You present in real time at a specific date and time. Chat and Q&A happen as they happen. Choose Live when people come for the interaction: a launch, a workshop, a session where you answer real questions.

Simulive

A recording plays at times you set, like a scheduled broadcast. Everyone in a session sees the same moment, so the chat still works as a shared room, and you can answer questions live while the recording plays.

Evergreen

A recording runs on a repeating schedule, and each registrant picks a time that suits them. Use it when the webinar is part of a funnel that should work whether or not you are available.

An evergreen schedule can offer just-in-time sessions, On Demand viewing and a recurring daily or weekly schedule. All three can run at once, and the registrant picks.

Which to choose

If you wantChoose
To present in real time and answer questions as they comeLive
A polished recording, broadcast at set times, with you in the chatSimulive
A session running continuously, with each registrant picking their own timeEvergreen
To run the same session weekly without presenting it againSimulive or Evergreen
To record once and let it sell while you sleepEvergreen

Live and Simulive need you to pick dates. Evergreen takes care of scheduling for you. Simulive and Evergreen play a recording; Live does not need one.

Changing type

To turn a live webinar into an evergreen one, run it live first, then clone it and choose Evergreen on the copy. Cloning is the supported path because the type is fixed.

What every webinar is made of

All three types use the same pages, emails, polls, offers and analytics. Only the delivery differs. Each webinar has these tabs:

TabWhat you do there
OverviewSee registrants and revenue, share links, add presenters and moderators
ContentEdit the five webinar pages: registration, thank-you, waiting area, webinar and replay
CTAsCreate polls and offers
EngagementSet up chat, Q&A and moderation
NotificationsEmails, SMS and WhatsApp messages to registrants
IntegrationsConnect a CRM or email tool for this webinar
AnalyticsRegistrations, attendance, offers and revenue
ReplayHow and for how long the recording is available
SettingsWebinar-level options such as showing the attendee list

The webinar header also has Registration Page, Share and Enter Room.

The objects you work with

ObjectWhat it is
WebinarType, schedule, pricing, pages, emails and CTAs
SessionOne sitting of a webinar: upcoming, past or cancelled
RegistrantA person registered for a session
Offer and pollA CTA shown in the room
TransactionA paid registration or offer purchase, with a status

Webinars are archived, not deleted.

From registration to follow-up

  1. Someone registers on your registration page, paying first if the webinar is paid.
  2. They get confirmation and reminder emails from the Notifications tab.
  3. They join live, or watch the recording at their session time.
  4. You show polls and offers in the room.
  5. Attendance, offer clicks and revenue appear in Analytics, and can be sent to your CRM as tags.

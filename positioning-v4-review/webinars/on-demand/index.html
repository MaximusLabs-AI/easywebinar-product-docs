On Demand (instant replay)

How On Demand works on EasyWebinar's evergreen webinars, letting registrants start watching straight away, how to label its button, and how it differs from the replay page.

Evergreen webinars

On Demand is an evergreen schedule setting that lets a registrant start watching your webinar straight away, with no session to wait for. EasyWebinar also calls it instant replay: registrants click a button, labelled Watch instant replay unless you change it, and the recording starts. It is a different thing from the replay page on the Replay tab, which shows the recording of a session that has already happened.

What On Demand is

An evergreen webinar can offer three ways to watch. On Demand is the one with no wait at all: the registrant doesn't pick a session time, they start the recording when they're ready.

OptionWhen the registrant watches
On DemandImmediately
Just-in-time RegistrationIn a session starting a few minutes after they register
Recurring ScheduleAt a set daily or weekly time

Any combination can be switched on, and the registration page lists each enabled option as a choice.

On Demand and the replay page are different

The names overlap, so the two are easy to mix up.

On Demand (instant replay)Replay page
What it playsThe evergreen webinar's recording, whenever a registrant choosesThe recording of a session that has already happened
Who it's forPeople who want to watch now rather than wait for a sessionPeople who missed a session and want to catch up
Where you set itThe schedule step of an evergreen webinarThe Replay tab of a webinar
What you configureOn or off, and the Instant replay textWhether the page is on, how long it stays available, redirects and how the page looks

A simple rule: if you want people to watch the moment they register, use On Demand on an evergreen webinar. If you want people who missed a session to catch up, use the replay page.

Why On Demand matters

What it's for

On Demand is for evergreen webinars where the content is the point, not the occasion. Think of training, product explainers and onboarding material. People want to get through these on their own timetable.

How it helps

  • No waiting at all. A registrant who is ready now can watch now.
  • It suits people short on time. Someone with twenty free minutes between meetings doesn't have to fit a session around them.
  • It sits alongside scheduled options. Registrants who'd rather turn up for a session can still pick a just-in-time or recurring one.
  • The button speaks your language. Instant replay text lets you word the button to match your page.

What happens without it

Without On Demand, every registrant has to wait for a session, even if only for a few minutes. Some content is meant to be watched the moment people find it. For that content, any wait is a chance for them to leave and not return.

Why buyers care

On Demand matters to teams using evergreen webinars for education and onboarding. It matters most when the audience is busy and the content is the reason they came. Those registrants get immediate access from the same page, recording and webinar setup.

The EasyWebinar approach to On Demand

Take a typical case. An HR software vendor sells leave and payroll tools to companies with 50 to 200 staff. Its marketing team of three records a 15-minute explainer on a change to holiday pay rules and links it from a monthly newsletter. The aim is to get HR managers to book a product demo afterwards.

A common approach is to run the explainer at set times, two or three a week. HR managers tend to read newsletters between interviews and payroll deadlines, though. A Thursday afternoon slot may not fit, and some may decide the topic can wait. For them, the newsletter click is probably wasted.

The explainer has no live element that needs everyone together. Its value is the content, so the timing matters only to the reader. When a manager has fifteen spare minutes now, that is the moment to watch.

EasyWebinar handles this on the schedule step of an evergreen webinar. The team switches on On Demand, and the registration page offers an instant start beside any other options. They change Instant replay text to wording such as "Start the explainer now". A Recurring Schedule can stay on for managers who prefer to book a time.

Set it up

On a new evergreen webinar

  1. On My Webinars, click Create a Webinar, then Create Manually.
  2. Name the webinar, choose Evergreen Webinar, and click Continue to Schedule.
  3. Switch on On Demand.
  4. In Instant replay text, keep Watch instant replay or type your own wording.
  5. Set any other options you want alongside it: Just-in-time Registration, Recurring Schedule, Availability and Schedule Preferences.
  6. Click Continue to Pricing and complete the remaining steps.

On an existing evergreen webinar

On the webinar's Overview tab, the options menu on the Scheduling Options panel changes the schedule for the whole webinar. The same panel shows the frequency, start date, timezone and the preference you chose.

Wording the button

The default Watch instant replay works, but the word "replay" can suggest a recording of something people missed. If your page sells the webinar as fresh content, wording that says what happens next, such as starting the training now, can read more naturally. Whatever you choose, keep it short enough to fit a button.

When to use On Demand, and when to leave it off

The deciding question is whether the webinar is valued for its content or for the occasion.

Use On Demand whenLeave it off when
The content matters more than the sense of an eventYou want the scheduled session to feel like something people turn up for

With On Demand off, registrants still have just-in-time or recurring sessions if you've enabled them.

How On Demand works with other features

Just-in-time Registration

Just-in-time offers a session a few minutes away, in each attendee's local time. With both on, registrants choose between watching now and joining a session shortly. Just-in-time webinars covers the interval and timezone rules.

Recurring Schedule

A daily or weekly schedule can run alongside On Demand for registrants who want to book a time. Recurring sessions run in the webinar's timezone.

The replay page

The Replay tab controls a separate page for people who missed a session, with availability options of Always Available, Expire after days or Specific date, and an Experience of Webinar Replica or Custom Build. Those settings belong to the replay page, not to On Demand.

Polls, offers and chat

Chat, Q&A, polls, offers, emails and analytics work on evergreen webinars as they do everywhere else. Where viewers watch at different times, Chat Simulation on the Engagement tab keeps the chat from looking empty.

Emails

Confirmation, reminder and follow-up emails are set on the Notifications tab. Replay emails are set up there too, not on the Replay tab. The confirmation email is the first one registrants receive.

Plan usage

People watching evergreen sessions count towards the Automated attendees meter on the Plan tab.

Customer success teams

SaaS and product teams

A product team offers a recorded feature walkthrough with On Demand alongside just-in-time. Evaluators in a hurry watch straight away; others join a session a few minutes later.

Course creators

Limits and things to know

  • Evergreen only. On Demand is set on the evergreen schedule step. Live and simulive webinars don't have it.
  • It isn't the replay page. Replay availability, deadlines, redirects and the replay page's look are set on the Replay tab for the replay page.
  • The recording is the webinar's. An evergreen webinar plays the recording chosen on its video step; change it from the Webinar Video panel on the Content tab.
  • The type is fixed. To offer On Demand for a live or simulive webinar, clone it as Evergreen.

Common questions

What does On Demand do?

It lets someone watch immediately, with no session to wait for.

Is On Demand the same as instant replay?

Yes. They're two names for the same evergreen setting.

What's the difference between instant replay and a replay?

A replay is the recording of a past session, on the Replay tab. Instant replay, or On Demand, lets someone watch an evergreen webinar straight away without waiting for a session.

Can I change the button text?

Yes. Edit Instant replay text on the schedule step. It reads Watch instant replay by default.

Can I offer On Demand and scheduled sessions together?

Yes. Turn on Just-in-time Registration, Recurring Schedule or both alongside it, and the registration page offers each as a choice.

Where do I turn On Demand on?

On the schedule step when you set up an evergreen webinar, not on the Replay tab.

Can a live or simulive webinar have On Demand?

No. It's an evergreen setting. Clone the webinar as Evergreen to use it.

I want people who missed a session to catch up. Is On Demand the right tool?

No. Use the replay page on the Replay tab for that.

Should every evergreen webinar have On Demand on?

Not necessarily. Turn it on when the content matters more than the event feeling, and leave it off when you want a scheduled session people turn up for.

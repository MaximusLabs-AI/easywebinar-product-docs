Create a live webinar, set the date, time and timezone your attendees register for, and get a registration page ready to publish. The builder has four steps and takes about two minutes.

Expected result: a webinar with its schedule applied, open in the registration page builder, ready for you to publish.

01 · Before you begin

Before you begin

  • Sign in to EasyWebinar. Any team member can create a webinar; you don't need to be the account owner.
  • Decide the date, time and timezone you will broadcast in.
  • If you will charge for the webinar, connect a Stripe account. You can complete everything without one by leaving pricing on Free.
  • Decide the webinar type first. The type can't be changed after the webinar is created.

Live, simulive or evergreen?

You wantChoose
To broadcast live, in real timeLive Webinar
Pre-recorded content that plays at scheduled times, with attendees registering for specific datesSimulive Webinar
Pre-recorded content available around the clock, watched on each viewer's scheduleEvergreen Webinar

The three webinar types use the same pages, emails, polls, offers and analytics. Only the delivery differs.

If you would rather have EasyWebinar write the script, pages, emails, offers and polls for you, choose Create with AI instead of Create Manually in step 1.

02 · Set it up

Step 1: Open the create dialog

  1. In the left sidebar, click My Webinars.
  2. Click Create a Webinar at the top right.
  3. Choose Create Manually.

Two ways to create a webinar. Create with AI generates your pages, emails, offers and polls; Create Manually opens the four-step builder.

03 · Set it up

Step 2: Name your webinar and choose its type

The builder opens on Step 1 of 4: Build.

  1. In Webinar Name, type the title attendees will see.
  2. Under Webinar Type, choose a card. Live Webinar is selected by default.
  3. Optionally add Tags: type one and press Enter, or click a suggested tag (Business, Education, Webinar, Sales, Coaching).
  4. Click Continue to Schedule.

1 Webinar Name · 2 Webinar Type, Live selected by default · 3 Tags · 4 Continue.

!
Nothing happens when you click Continue?

The webinar name is empty. The builder won't move on until you enter one.

04 · Set it up

Step 3: Set your schedule

On Step 2 of 4: Schedule:

  1. Choose a Schedule Frequency: Specific Date, Daily or Weekly.
  2. Check the Timezone. It defaults to your account timezone. Your live session runs at the time you set, in this timezone.
  3. Under Available Sessions, set the date and time.
  4. Optional: click Add Session to offer the same webinar at more than one date or time.
  5. Under Schedule Preferences, choose how many upcoming sessions a registrant can pick from.
  6. Click Continue to Pricing.

1 Frequency · 2 Timezone · 3 Session date and time · 4 Add Session · 5 Schedule Preferences · 6 Continue.

Timezone

A live webinar runs at one fixed time for everyone, in the timezone you set. Your registration page shows that time with its timezone. Choose the timezone you will broadcast in.

i
Local times

Only just-in-time sessions on evergreen webinars follow each attendee's own local time. Live and scheduled sessions always run in the webinar's timezone.

Past dates

You can't schedule a session in the past. Earlier dates are greyed out in the picker.

Run the same webinar every week

Choosing Weekly turns the single session into a recurring schedule and adds four controls:

ControlWhat it does
Days of weekWhich days the webinar runs, Monday to Sunday
Start Date and End Date (Optional)When the recurrence begins and ends. Leave the end date as No end date to run it indefinitely
Session Time and Add TimeRun it more than once on the same day
Blackout DatesStop the webinar running on specific dates, such as holidays or time off

Blackout dates

A weekly webinar runs until you stop it. Use Blackout Dates to skip individual days without ending the series, or set an end date to stop it.

Which sessions registrants see

By default registrants see only the next available session. Change Schedule Preferences to let them choose from more upcoming sessions.

05 · Set it up

Step 4: Choose free or paid

On Step 3 of 4: Pricing, choose one:

  • Free (selected by default): attendees register without paying.
  • Paid: attendees pay to register. Requires a connected Stripe account.

Click Continue to Registration Template.

Charging for the webinar

Selecting Paid shows:

  • Pricing Title and an optional Description, shown to attendees at checkout
  • Live webinar price, the amount each registrant pays
  • Live + Replay bundle, to sell access to the webinar and its recording together

Attendees pay through a Stripe checkout, and the money goes straight to your Stripe account. If no account is connected, a Payments · Action required panel offers Connect with Stripe. You can switch back to Free any time before creating the webinar.

Once people pay, the webinar's Analytics tab shows what you've earned under Revenue, with a list of every transaction and its status.

06 · Set it up

Step 5: Choose a registration template

On Step 4 of 4: Registration Template:

  1. Filter by category: All Templates, Agency, Coach, Education, Health & Wellness, Lead Magnet, Marketing, or My Templates for ones you have saved.
  2. Click a template. Default Register Page is the simplest one-column option; Build Your Own starts from a blank page.
  3. Click Create Webinar.

1 Category filters · 2 Template cards · 3 Create button.

You can skip this step. The button reads Skip & Create Webinar until you select a template, and you can choose one later.

07 · Check

Verify the result

The webinar is created and opens in the registration page builder, with your schedule applied and a live countdown to the session.

  • The default form collects Full name and Email, both required. Use Add Custom Field to collect more on the form.
  • Use the desktop and mobile toggle to check both layouts.
!
Not public yet

Creating the webinar does not publish it. Your registration page goes live only when you click Publish. Use Preview to check it and Save to keep a draft.

08 · How it works

What a live webinar is

A live webinar is a session you present in real time. Attendees join at the scheduled moment, and chat and questions happen as they happen. Live is one of three webinar types; simulive and evergreen play a recording instead. The schedule you set here decides when registrants can attend, and the registration page is where they sign up for it.

09 · Why it matters

Why scheduling a live webinar matters

What it's for

A live webinar suits sessions where your presence is the point: a launch, a workshop or a class with real questions. Creating it fixes the date, time and timezone that people sign up for.

How it helps

  • Many sittings, one page. Add Session, Daily and Weekly keep repeat sessions under a single registration page.
  • Holidays stay free. Blackout Dates skip chosen days without ending a weekly run.
  • Payment at sign-up. With Paid selected, people complete a Stripe checkout before they are registered.
  • No accidental launch. The registration page stays private until you click Publish.

What happens without it

Without a scheduled live session, your audience has no fixed moment to turn up. Promotion has no date to point at, so interest fades before anyone joins. You also lose the chance to answer questions while people are still deciding.

Why buyers care

It matters most to trainers, educators and sales teams whose value comes from talking with people in real time. The key moment is the week before a session, when people decide whether to register and keep the date free.

10 · Our approach

The EasyWebinar approach to live webinar scheduling

Take a typical case. A real-estate training company with three instructors runs a live exam review for about 80 trainee agents. It meets on Tuesday and Thursday evenings for the eight weeks before each licensing exam, and it pauses on public holidays.

A common approach is to set up a separate event for every evening, which can mean sixteen events per course. Each one may need its own sign-up link, so trainees often juggle a list of links. When a holiday moves a class, someone usually has to rebuild that night by hand.

The review is the same course with the same instructors every evening. What changes is only the calendar. So one webinar with a repeating schedule fits the course far better than sixteen separate ones, as long as the gaps can be marked.

EasyWebinar handles this as a single live webinar. The team picks Weekly, ticks Tuesday and Thursday, and sets the course dates as the Start Date and End Date (Optional). Holidays go in as Blackout Dates, and every trainee signs up on the same registration page.

The weekly controls are easy to spot. With Weekly selected, the schedule step shows Days of week, Session Time with Add Time, and Blackout Dates. How this scheduling stacks up against other live webinar tools is covered in How live webinars compare, below.

11 · Works with

How live webinars work with other features

Registration page

The webinar opens straight into the registration page builder with your schedule applied. Edit the page, add form fields and publish it there. The registration embeds put the same form on your own site.

Reminder emails

Reminder timing is relative to the webinar, not a fixed clock time, so it follows a recurring schedule. Set reminders on the Notifications tab; see reminder emails for each session.

The Overview tab

After the webinar exists, its Overview tab holds the schedule. The options menu on Scheduling Options changes the whole schedule, and the menu on a session row acts on one sitting. Click Share for the Registration Page Link you promote, or use the sharing links guide.

Cloning

The type is fixed once the webinar exists. To reuse a live webinar as simulive or evergreen, clone it and choose the new type on the copy.

Engagement in the room

Live chat, polls and questions from attendees are set up on the webinar's tabs after you create it, ready for the session itself.

12 · Who it’s for

Who uses live webinars, and how

These are typical patterns, shown to illustrate how live scheduling fits different teams.

Training and education providers

A training provider runs the same class on set weekdays through a term. A Weekly schedule with an end date covers the term, and Blackout Dates cover the breaks.

B2B marketing teams

A marketing team presents a quarterly product briefing at one fixed time. It offers a second sitting with Add Session for prospects in other regions.

Consultants selling a masterclass

A consultant charges for a live masterclass by choosing Paid on the pricing step. Buyers complete a Stripe checkout before their registration goes through.

Coaches running open office hours

A coach holds free weekly office hours with No end date. Registrants see only the next session unless the coach changes Schedule Preferences.

14 · Limits

Limits and things to know

  • The type is fixed. Clone the webinar and choose another type on the copy.
  • Choose Paid when you create the webinar. If you switch an existing free webinar to paid, its registration page keeps the free form.
  • You can't schedule a session in the past.
  • Registrant numbers depend on your plan. Attendee and registration limits differ between EasyWebinar's plans; the features on this page are available on all plans.

Schedule variations

  • A live webinar runs at one fixed time in the timezone you set.
  • A weekly webinar runs until you stop it, unless you set an end date.
  • Registrants see the next session only unless you change the schedule preference.
15 · Questions

Common questions

Can I run the same webinar at more than one time?

Yes. Click Add Session to add dates and times, or switch the frequency to Daily or Weekly.

Can I change the webinar type after creating it?

No. Clone the webinar and choose the type you want on the copy. This is also how you turn a live webinar into an evergreen one.

Do I have to be the account owner?

No. Any team member can create and schedule webinars.

Is my registration page live as soon as I create the webinar?

No. It becomes public only when you click Publish.

What's the difference between live, simulive and evergreen?

Live broadcasts in real time. Simulive plays pre-recorded content at scheduled times, with attendees registering for specific dates. Evergreen makes pre-recorded content available 24/7, with viewers watching on their own schedule.

How do I stop a recurring webinar from running on a holiday?

Use Blackout Dates on the schedule step to list the dates the webinar should skip.

What timezone do my attendees see?

The one you set for the webinar. A live session runs at one fixed time for everyone, and the registration page shows that time with its timezone.

Can I schedule a webinar in the past?

No. Past dates are disabled in the date picker.

Do I have to pick a registration template now?

No. The button reads Skip & Create Webinar when nothing is selected, and you can choose a template later.

How do I charge for a live webinar?

Choose Paid on the pricing step and connect a Stripe account. You can also sell a Live + Replay bundle that includes the recording.

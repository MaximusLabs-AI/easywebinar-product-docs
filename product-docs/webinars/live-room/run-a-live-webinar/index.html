Run a live webinar

Enter the EasyWebinar webinar room, prepare in the green room, go live, present with chat, polls, offers and Q&A, then end the session and find your recording.

Live webinars

Markdown

To run a live webinar in EasyWebinar, you open the webinar room from the webinar's Overview, pass a quick camera and microphone check, and arrive in the green room, where nothing reaches your audience. When you're ready you click Go Live, present using the panels on the right-hand rail, and finish with End Session. The result is a broadcast to everyone registered for the session, with the recording saved to Library → Recordings if recording was on.

Before you begin

  • Be signed in as a team member. Any team member signed in to EasyWebinar can open the room. To bring in a co-presenter or moderator, see The green room and backstage.
  • Check your camera and microphone. The room won't let you past the device check without both.
  • Set chat, Q&A and the attendee list in advance. Who can see chat messages and how Q&A works are chosen on the webinar's Engagement tab. Whether attendees can see each other is Show Attendees on the Settings tab. These belong to the webinar's setup, not the live room, so decide them before the session.
  • Build your polls and offers. Create polls and offers on the CTAs tab. You can keep an offer switched off in the list until you want it.
  • Fill in End of Session. On the Settings tab, End of Session is where attendees go when the stream ends. It's empty by default, which leaves attendees on a finished webinar.
  • Connect any streaming destinations. If you plan to stream to YouTube or LinkedIn as well, connect them once under Integrations → EasyCast in the sidebar.

Run the session

1. Open the webinar room

  1. Open the webinar. It opens on Overview.
  2. Click Enter Room at the top right.

You can also click ⋮ on the webinar's card in My Webinars and choose Go Live, which takes you into the same room.

2. Pass the device check

You're asked to choose your camera, microphone and speakers. Pick each one, then click Continue to Green Room.

The button stays greyed out until the room can see a camera and a microphone. If it can't open your camera, you'll see "Not able to access selected media device". Allow camera access in your browser and close any other app that's using the camera. Camera not detected walks through the fixes.

If another of your webinars is already live, Enter Room shows "You can only have one event live at a time." Join the session that's running, or go live with the new one.

3. Get ready in the green room

The green room is the room as it will look, with nothing broadcast. Check your framing, test Share, and get your co-hosts in place.

The green room: 1 Status · 2 Settings, Reactions, EasyCast and Full Screen · 3 Camera, Mic and Share · 4 Go Live and Exit · 5 Backstage · 6 Side rail · 7 Layout.

Anyone who joins as a co-host or moderator lands in Backstage, not on stage. The green room page covers backstage, the People panel and presenter links in detail.

4. Check recording

Look at the badge in the header. Auto Recording Enabled means the session will be recorded. To change it, click Settings in the top bar, then Recording Settings.

SectionWhat it covers
1Device settingsCamera, audio and recording
2Recording SettingsWhether the session is recorded
3PreferencesChat moderation, Q&A moderation and appearance

Keep recording on unless you have a specific reason to switch it off. The replay usually reaches more people than the live session does, and a recording is what you'd use to turn this webinar into an evergreen one later.

5. Style the room

Click Design on the right-hand rail. Do this now: design changes apply immediately, so anything you change once you're live happens in front of your audience.

ControlWhat to do with it
1Stage / FrameStage styles what sits behind the presenters; Frame styles the area around the stage
2Background presetsPick a supplied background, or + to upload your own image
3Blur IntensityStarts at 0. Raise it to blur whatever is behind you
4ColoursUse a plain colour instead of an image
5Frame OverlaysA border, logo bar or lower third over the stage, or + for your own
6LayoutOpen Choose Layout at the bottom of the stage to arrange presenters
7ThemeLight, Dark or System, for your own view only

Blur is the quickest fix for an untidy room. A frame overlay is where your branding goes, and it carries through to the recording and anything EasyCast streams out. Upload overlays with a transparent background, otherwise they cover the video instead of framing it. Layout matters most with several presenters or a screen share, because it decides whether your slides or your faces take the space.

6. Switch on streaming destinations (optional)

If you connected destinations in Integrations → EasyCast, click EasyCast in the top bar of the room. Switch Stream on for each destination you want this session to reach. The pencil beside a destination sets the title and description shown on that platform, so your public stream doesn't carry your internal webinar name. Connect More Channels takes you to the Integrations page to add another.

EasyCast is set up per account, so a destination connected once is available on every webinar. It covers YouTube, LinkedIn, LinkedIn Organisation and Custom RTMP.

7. Go live

Click Go Live. A confirmation asks you to check two things: people in the waiting area will be moved into the webinar automatically, and your offers and polls should already be created. Click Go Live in the dialog to start.

Live: 1 LIVE with a timer, and Recording · 2 One icon per EasyCast destination · 3 End Session.

The header now shows LIVE with a timer of how long you've been broadcasting, the recording badge changes to Recording, and Go Live becomes End Session. If you're streaming with EasyCast, a platform icon appears for each destination. Click it to open the stream on that platform and see what viewers there are seeing.

You can switch your camera and microphone off with Camera and Mic and stay live, which suits a session built on slides or a video rather than your face.

8. Present

Everything you use while presenting sits on the right-hand rail:

PanelWhat you use it for
ChatAttendee messages, with Global Chat and Admin Chats
PeoplePresenters and attendees, and moving people on and off stage
OffersPushing an offer to the room
PollsLaunching a poll. On a live webinar you launch each one yourself
QuestionThe Q&A queue, split into Active and Answered
DesignBackgrounds, overlays and layout
ThemeLight, dark or system

In the top bar, Share shares your screen, Full Screen expands the stage, and Reactions enables emoji reactions from the room.

Polls don't fire on their own in a live session, so plan when each poll goes out. Offers reach the room when you push them from the Offers panel. The Question panel keeps Active and Answered apart, so you can see at a glance what's still waiting.

9. End the session

Click End Session and confirm. Ending disconnects everyone in the room, and nobody can rejoin, which is why EasyWebinar asks first. If you were streaming with EasyCast, the broadcast stops on every destination at the same moment.

Attendees then go to the URL you set in End of Session, if you set one.

Verify the result

  • While live: the header shows LIVE with a running timer and the Recording badge. With EasyCast on, there's one platform icon per destination.
  • Recording: open Library → Recordings. The recording is named after its webinar, with its duration and the date it was recorded.
  • Session history: on the webinar's Overview, the session moves to the Past tab of the Sessions panel.
  • Attendance: the webinar's Analytics tab shows Live Attended for people who joined the broadcast. See Webinar analytics.

What the live room is

The live room is where you present a live webinar. You arrive in a green room where nothing is broadcast, then go live when you're set. The same room holds backstage, chat, polls, offers, questions and the recording controls.

QuestionLive room answer
Is anything broadcast before you start?No. The header reads Not Live until you confirm Go Live
Where do co-hosts wait?Backstage, out of sight until you move them on stage
Where does the recording go?Library → Recordings
Which webinar types use it?Live webinars. Simulive and evergreen webinars play a recording instead

To run a recording at set times without presenting, see Create a simulive webinar.

Why running the room well matters

What it's for

The live room is where a live webinar actually happens. Everything you set up beforehand leads to this hour. The room gives you a private place to prepare, a clear start, the tools to engage and sell, and a definite end.

How it helps

  • You can prepare in private. The green room lets you fix audio, lighting and screen sharing with nobody watching.
  • The start is controlled. Nothing is broadcast until you confirm Go Live, and people in the waiting area are moved in automatically when you do.
  • Selling happens in the room. Offers and polls are pushed from the same rail you use for chat and questions.
  • The recording is automatic. With recording on, the session lands in your Library without an extra step.
  • One session can reach more places. EasyCast sends the same broadcast to YouTube and LinkedIn.

What happens without it

Without a staging area, presenters test their microphones in front of the audience, and a co-host's first words are an apology about echo. Without a confirmation step, a webinar can start before the offers exist. Without automatic recording, the replay you planned to send to no-shows may not exist at all.

Why buyers care

It matters most to teams whose live session is a public moment: a launch, a recruitment event or a sales presentation. Those teams are choosing a live webinar platform, and the test comes in the first five minutes of the first session.

The EasyWebinar approach to the live room

Take a typical case. A university admissions office runs a live open evening each term for prospective postgraduate students. Two admissions officers present, a current student joins for the last twenty minutes, and around 200 applicants from several countries register.

The usual workaround is a general video meeting tool, set up by whoever is free that week. It may work, but the student often joins straight into the broadcast and tests their microphone in front of everyone. Questions can get lost in a busy chat, and the recording may end up in one person's account.

What changed is the stakes. An open evening is a recruitment event, so it has to look composed from the first minute. Applicants who can't attend live still need to see it, so the recording matters as much as the session.

EasyWebinar handles this inside the live room. EasyWebinar puts co-hosts and moderators backstage when they join, so the student can sort out their audio unseen and come on stage when called. EasyWebinar keeps questions in their own Question panel, split into Active and Answered, and with recording on the session lands in Library → Recordings.

One fact is easy to check before you start. In the green room the header reads Not Live, and nothing reaches attendees until you confirm Go Live. That private room is one of the capabilities covered in How the live room compares, below.

How the live room works with other features

Chat and Q&A settings

How chat and questions behave in the room is set on the webinar's Engagement tab. Hosts only makes each attendee's messages private to your team, No Chat removes the panel, and Q&A can be public, private or disabled. See Live chat and Questions and answers.

Polls and offers

Polls and offers are created on the CTAs tab and shown from the room. A row's toggle in the CTAs list keeps an offer ready without showing it. Switch on Countdown timer in the offer editor to put a countdown on an offer.

Replays and the Library

Your recording appears in Library → Recordings. With the replay page set to Webinar Replica, the replay shows the same video, offers, polls and chat replay as the live session. See Replay pages and The Library.

Evergreen from a live session

A webinar's type can't be changed, so the way to automate a successful live session is to clone it as Evergreen and pick its recording. See Clone a webinar.

EasyCast

EasyCast multistreaming sends the live session to YouTube, LinkedIn, LinkedIn Organisation pages and custom RTMP destinations, connected once under Integrations → EasyCast and switched on per session in the room. Facebook isn't offered as a destination; its RTMP details may work under Custom RTMP.

Who runs live webinars, and how

These are typical patterns, shown to illustrate how the live room fits different teams.

Coaches and consultants

A coach runs a live masterclass alone. They blur the background in the green room, keep recording on, launch a poll about the audience's situation halfway through, and push a booking offer near the end. End of Session sends everyone to the booking page.

SaaS and product teams

A product manager presents a release walkthrough or product demo while a colleague moderates. The colleague waits backstage, handles the Question queue during the demo, and comes on stage for the last ten minutes to answer the top questions. Share carries the product demo, and the layout favours the screen.

B2B marketing teams

A marketing team runs a panel webinar with branded frame overlays and streams it to the company's LinkedIn page through EasyCast. Registered attendees watch in the webinar room, and the overlay carries the brand into the recording too.

Course creators

A creator runs a training live a few times with recording on, then clones the strongest session as an evergreen webinar using the recording from the Library. The course creator's guide follows that route end to end.

Limits and things to know

  • A camera and microphone are required to enter, even if you switch them off afterwards.
  • Only one event can be live at a time.
  • Polls are launched manually on a live webinar.
  • Design changes apply immediately, including mid-broadcast.
  • Theme is personal. Light, dark or system changes your view, not the attendees'.
  • Ending is final. End Session disconnects everyone, and nobody can rejoin.
  • Chat, Q&A and Show Attendees are setup controls. Set them on the webinar's tabs before you go live.
  • EasyCast is for live webinars. Simulive and evergreen webinars play a recording rather than broadcasting.

Common questions

Is anything broadcast while I'm in the green room?

No. The header reads Not Live until you click Go Live and confirm.

Why is Continue to Green Room greyed out?

The room needs to see a working camera and microphone before it lets you in. See Camera not detected.

Can I go live with my camera off?

Yes. You need a camera to enter, but you can switch Camera and Mic off in the green room and still go live.

Is my webinar recorded automatically?

When the header shows Auto Recording Enabled, yes. Change it in Settings → Recording Settings.

Where does the recording go?

To Library → Recordings, named after the webinar with its duration and recording date.

Do polls appear on their own during a live webinar?

No. On a live webinar you launch each poll yourself from the Polls panel.

Can I share my screen?

Yes, with Share in the top bar.

Can I stop attendees chatting with each other?

Yes. Before the session, set chat to Hosts only on the Engagement tab, or choose No Chat.

What happens to people waiting when I go live?

People in the waiting area are moved into the webinar automatically when you go live.

Can I restart a session after ending it?

No. Ending disconnects everyone and nobody can rejoin, so make sure you're finished before you confirm.

Why can't I enter the room for a second webinar?

Another of your events is live. EasyWebinar allows one live event at a time.

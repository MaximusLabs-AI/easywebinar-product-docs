Sharing links

The seven links in an EasyWebinar webinar's Share dialog, from the registration page link you promote to the one-click registration, attendee, co-presenter, moderator and replay links, and who each one is for.

Applies to
Live, simulive and evergreen webinars
Status
Live
Verified

Page type
Feature
Markdown

Every EasyWebinar webinar generates seven links, and the Share button on the webinar's header shows them all in one dialog. One is the public registration link you promote; others let a known contact register in one click, let attendees join, let a co-presenter or moderator into the room, and send people to the replay. The dialog also holds the Registration Widget embed code, a Share on social media section, and a Shorten URL's toggle for shorter versions of every link.

What the Share dialog is

The Share dialog is the single place to copy any link a webinar produces. Click Share on the webinar's header and the dialog opens with three groups:

GroupWhat's in it
Share LinksThe seven links in the table above
Registration WidgetThe embed code that puts your registration form on your own site
Share on social mediaSharing the webinar on social networks

A Shorten URL's toggle gives you shorter versions.

The links fall into three kinds. Audience links (registration, one-click registration, attendee join, attendee replay) are meant for the people watching. Team links (co-presenter and moderator) let someone into the room in a role, so they're private. The Thank You Page Link is where registrants arrive on their own, so you rarely need it.

Why sharing links matter

What it's for

A webinar has several audiences at once. Some people still need to register, some are already registered, and some missed the session. Others are helping you run it. The Share dialog gives each group its own way in, from one screen.

How it helps

  • No hunting for links. Every link for the webinar sits in one dialog, a click from the Overview.
  • Guests don't need accounts. A co-presenter or moderator joins with a link, without an EasyWebinar login.
  • Known contacts skip the form. The One-Click Registration Link registers someone whose details you already hold.
  • Shorter links when you need them. Shorten URL's makes links easier to fit in a post or a text.

What happens without it

Without role links, the only way to bring a guest speaker into the room is to give them access to your account. Without a one-click link, every contact you invite from your own list retypes details you already hold. Some of them won't bother, and those registrations are lost.

Why buyers care

This matters most to teams that bring outside speakers onto their webinars and promote to lists they already own. It shows up in the week before a session, when links go out to speakers, moderators, customers and the wider audience at once.

The EasyWebinar approach to sharing links

Take a typical case. A podcast network with six shows hosts a monthly live webinar where a guest from one show gives a talk. The network's producer runs the session and a show host interviews the guest. Promotion goes out on every show's feed and newsletter, and the guest has never used the network's webinar account.

A common workaround is to send the guest a shared login, or a link that drops them straight in front of the audience. That can mean passwords passed around and a guest testing their microphone in public. Long registration URLs in show notes are also awkward to read aloud or retype.

The guest doesn't need an account; they need a way into the room for one evening. Listeners don't need a different page; they need a link short enough for show notes. Newsletter subscribers the network already knows shouldn't have to fill in a form again.

EasyWebinar puts each of those links in the webinar's Share dialog. The producer sends the guest the Co-presenter Join Link and a colleague the Moderator Join Link for Live room. Both join without a login and wait backstage until they're brought on. The producer turns on Shorten URL's for the show notes and emails the One-Click Registration Link to subscribers.

Open the Share dialog

  1. Click My Webinars in the sidebar and open the webinar.
  2. On the Overview tab, click Share on the webinar's header, next to Registration Page.
  3. Turn on Shorten URL's if you want shorter versions.
  4. Copy the link you need.

The seven links

Registration Page Link

The public link to your registration page, and the one you promote in ads, emails and posts. It only works once the registration page has been published in the page builder; saving alone isn't enough.

One-Click Registration Link

A link for an existing list. It carries the person's name and email in the URL, so a known contact registers without filling in the form. Because it depends on details you already hold, it only works for contacts you already have. Use it in emails to your own list, where you know who each recipient is.

Attendee Join Link

For registered attendees, to enter the live session. Registrants also receive a join link in the Registration Confirmation email, which is sent the moment someone registers. That's why it's the one email you shouldn't turn off.

Attendee Replay link

For people watching the recording afterwards. It's the same link as the Replay Page Link on the webinar's Replay tab, where you also turn the replay page on with Enable replay page.

1 Replay Settings · 2 Replay Page Link · 3 Availability · 4 Redirects · 5 Experience.

Once the page is enabled it reads Replay page is live, anyone with the link can watch, so treat the replay link as public. Replay emails aren't set up here: add a Webinar Follow-up on the Notifications tab and include the replay link.

Co-presenter Join Link

For a presenter, so they can join the room. They don't need an EasyWebinar account to use it. When a co-host or moderator joins a live session, they land backstage, not on stage, and your audience doesn't see or hear them until you move them to the stage.

Moderator Join Link for Live room

For a moderator, to handle chat and questions. Like the co-presenter link, it works without an account. It's also a handy way to check your own webinar from the inside: on a recorded webinar with an embedded third-party chat, joining with the Moderator Join Link lets you look at the side panel.

Treat presenter and moderator links as private

Anyone holding the Co-presenter Join Link or the Moderator Join Link for Live room can join your room in that role. Send them only to the people who should have them. The registration, attendee and replay links are the ones meant for your audience.

Thank You Page Link

Where registrants land after signing up. You'll rarely send it to anyone, but it's useful for checking the page yourself.

Shorten the links

Turn on Shorten URL's in the Share dialog to get shorter versions of the links. If you send join and replay links from a connected CRM, the integration's Custom fields tab offers short versions there too: Webinar Join Short Link and Webinar Replay Short Link, alongside the full Webinar Join Link and Webinar Replay Link.

How sharing links work with other features

Team & Moderators

The Team & Moderators panel on the Overview is the other way to bring people into a webinar: Add presenter adds someone who appears on camera and presents, and Invite sends an invitation to join the webinar team. A join link suits a guest on one session; adding someone to your account team suits people who work in EasyWebinar regularly.

The live room

Inside the room, the People panel's Invite People shares join links for presenters and moderators, so you can bring someone in after you've entered the green room. See Run a live webinar.

Registration Widget

The Share dialog's Registration Widget holds the same embed snippet as the Content tab. Use it to put the form on your own site instead of sharing a link.

Your CRM

A connected CRM can receive each attendee's personal join link and replay link through the Custom fields tab. Mapping the join link is what lets you send reminders from your CRM instead of EasyWebinar, with each contact getting their own working link.

How the links look when shared

Social Sharing on the webinar's Settings tab, and Social Sharing (Open Graph) in the page builder, control how your link looks when someone shares it.

Your domain

Every public link includes your subdomain, ending in .easywebinar.live, including the links in your emails. The C-Name add-on lets links use your own domain instead. Changing your subdomain changes your existing links, so anything already shared will break; set it before you promote.

Expired links

Expired Session on the webinar's Settings → General tab sets where people go if their link no longer works. It's empty by default, so fill it in with a page that tells people what to do next.

Who uses sharing links, and how

These are typical patterns, shown to illustrate how each link fits a different job.

Agencies

An agency runs a webinar for a client whose founder is the speaker. The agency sends the founder the Co-presenter Join Link and its own team member the Moderator Join Link for Live room. Neither needs a login to the agency's account, and the founder waits backstage until the host brings them on.

B2B marketing teams

A marketing team promotes the Registration Page Link in ads and social posts with Shorten URL's on. For an email to existing customers, it uses the One-Click Registration Link, so customers register without retyping details the team already holds.

Customer success teams

A customer success team runs a training webinar for existing customers. It emails the One-Click Registration Link to the customer list, then sends the Attendee Replay link in a follow-up to everyone who couldn't attend live.

Course creators

A course creator sends no-shows the replay link in a Webinar Follow-up after the session, and maps the join link into their email tool so reminders come from their own system.

Why EasyWebinar is the stronger choice for sharing links

  • Shorter links from the Share dialog. EasyWebinar's Shorten URL's toggle gives you shorter versions of the webinar's links. WebinarJam's help centre doesn't document a link shortener, as the EasyWebinar vs WebinarJam and EverWebinar comparison shows.

Limits and things to know

  • The registration link needs a published page. Publish the registration page before you promote the link.
  • The one-click link only works for known contacts. It carries name and email in the URL.
  • Presenter and moderator links grant access. Anyone holding one can join your room in that role.
  • The replay link is public while the replay page is on. Anyone with it can watch.
  • Your subdomain is in every link. Changing it breaks links you've already sent.
  • Integrations are per webinar. Mapping join and replay links into a CRM is set up on each webinar.

Common questions

Where do I get the link to promote my webinar?

Click Share and copy the Registration Page Link. That's the public one.

Why doesn't my registration link work?

The registration page needs to be published. Open it in the page builder and click Publish.

Can someone register without filling in the form?

Yes, with the One-Click Registration Link. It carries their name and email in the URL, so it only works for contacts you already have.

How do I let a guest speaker into the room?

Send them the Co-presenter Join Link from the Share dialog, or add them under Team & Moderators on the Overview.

Does a co-presenter need an EasyWebinar account?

No. Presenters and moderators can join with a link without having an account.

Will my co-presenter appear on screen as soon as they join?

No. A co-host or moderator who joins lands backstage. Your audience doesn't see or hear them until you move them to the stage.

Can I shorten these links?

Yes. Turn on Shorten URL's in the Share dialog.

Where's the replay link?

In the Share dialog, as Attendee Replay link, and on the Replay tab as Replay Page Link. Turn on Enable replay page first.

Who can watch my replay?

Anyone with the link, while the replay page is enabled.

Can I send each attendee their own join link from my CRM?

Yes. Map Webinar Join Link to a custom field in your CRM on the integration's Custom fields tab.

Can my links use my own domain?

Yes, with the C-Name add-on and a CNAME record at your DNS provider.

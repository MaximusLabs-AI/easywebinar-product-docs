# Webinar reminder emails

How EasyWebinar's Webinar Reminders work, how to time them relative to the session, how many to send, and how to tell from Analytics whether they are getting people to attend.

- Applies to: Every webinar, including recurring and evergreen schedules · set per webinar on the Notifications tab

**Webinar Reminders** are the emails EasyWebinar sends to registrants before the session starts. Each reminder has its own timing, set as a length of time before the webinar, so the same reminders work for a one-off session, a [recurring schedule](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/getting-started/create-a-live-webinar/#recurring-schedules) or an evergreen webinar. You can add as many as you need from the **Notifications** tab, and Analytics shows whether they are working: the gap between people who registered and people who attended is the reminders' job.

## What webinar reminders are {#what-it-is}

A reminder is an email in the **Webinar Reminders** group on the **Notifications** tab. It goes to people who registered, before the webinar starts, and its purpose is to [turn a registration into an attendance](https://easywebinar.com/blog/email-marketing-strategies-to-boost-webinar-attendance/).

What makes EasyWebinar's reminders work across webinar types is how their timing is set. A reminder isn't scheduled for a clock time like "Tuesday at 9am". It is set relative to the session, expressed as hours, minutes and seconds before the webinar, such as *0h 15m 0s before webinar*. A daily recurring webinar or an evergreen webinar has many sessions, and relative timing is why the same reminders work across all of them.

Reminders sit between two other email groups. The **Registration Confirmation** goes out the moment someone signs up, and [**Webinar Follow-ups**](https://easywebinar.com/features/webinar-follow-up/) go out after the session. Reminders cover the time in between, which is when most registrants forget.

## Why reminders matter {#why-it-matters}

### What it's for {#what-its-for}

Getting people to register is the easy part; [getting them to turn up](https://easywebinar.com/blog/how-to-drive-webinar-attendance/) is what notifications are for. Reminders bring the date, the time and the way in back to each registrant. They land the day before, when people can still plan, and in the final minutes, when they need a nudge to click.

### How it helps {#how-it-helps}

- **It brings the session back into view.** A sign-up from days ago is easy to forget. A reminder an hour before puts it in front of the person again.
- **The last reminder does most of the work.** In EasyWebinar's guidance, the one fifteen minutes before matters most.
- **It runs on every session by itself.** Timing is relative, so nothing needs rescheduling when new sessions appear.
- **It is measurable.** **Registered** to attended in Analytics shows whether the reminders are working.

### What happens without it {#without-it}

Without reminders, attendance depends on people remembering a session they booked days or weeks ago. Analytics shows the cost as a large **Missed** count: people who registered and never came. EasyWebinar reads a low registered-to-attended rate as a notification problem, not a content one.

### Why buyers care {#why-buyers-care}

Every result that matters to the business, from offer clicks to booked calls, depends on people actually watching. That makes reminders matter most to teams that sell or book calls at the end of a session. It matters even more on [automated webinars](https://easywebinar.com/automated-webinars/), where sessions run without anyone checking the emails.

## The EasyWebinar approach to reminder emails {#approach}

{/* 5P */}

Take a typical case. A yoga teacher-training school runs a free evergreen info session for people thinking about its 200-hour course. The session plays several times a week, and people register from the school's Instagram. Most sign up on their phones, often days before the session they pick.

A common approach is to schedule reminder emails by hand for each session date. That may work for one session a month, but it gets hard with several a week. Gaps creep in, and some people may hear nothing between signing up and the start.

The reminders themselves don't change from one session to the next. What changes is the start time each one counts back from. A reminder set against the session, rather than the calendar, fits every session the schedule creates.

EasyWebinar sets each reminder as time before the webinar, on the **Notifications** tab. The school keeps three: a day, an hour and fifteen minutes before. Each one follows every session on the evergreen schedule. For the final nudge, the school adds a **Reminder SMS**, in Beta, because its audience reads texts quickly.

The screenshot under Find your reminders shows the **Webinar Reminders** group with reminders timed 15 minutes, 1 hour and 24 hours before the webinar. How other platforms set reminder timing and channels is covered in How reminder emails compare, below.

## Set it up {#set-it-up}

### Find your reminders {#find-reminders}

1. Open your webinar and click the **Notifications** tab.
2. Stay on the **Emails** channel and find the **Webinar Reminders** group.

![The Notifications tab showing the Webinar Reminders group with three reminders timed 15 minutes, 1 hour and 24 hours before the webinar](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/easywebinar-product-docs/images/notifications/step-01-emails.png)

*1 Channel tabs · 2 Email Settings · 3 An email group · 4 Create New.*

Each entry shows its **trigger**, the audience it goes **to**, and its **subject**, so the group reads as a timeline of what registrants will receive.

### Edit a reminder {#edit-reminder}

1. Open the reminder you want to change.
2. Change its subject, content and timing.
3. Set the timing as time before the webinar, in hours, minutes and seconds.
4. Save your changes.

### Add another reminder {#add-reminder}

1. Click **Create New** in the **Webinar Reminders** group.
2. Set its timing before the webinar.
3. Write the subject and content.

### A sensible schedule {#sensible-schedule}

| Reminder | Timing before the webinar | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| A day before | One day before the session | Lets people plan their day around the session |
| An hour before | *1h 0m 0s before webinar* | Brings the session back to mind while there's still time to prepare |
| Fifteen minutes before | *0h 15m 0s before webinar* | The prompt to join now; the one that does most of the work |

Three is a sensible default. Webinars built with [**Create with AI**](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/ai/create-with-ai/#generate-emails) arrive with exactly this pattern already drafted: **Reminder — 1d**, **Reminder — 1h** and **Reminder — 15m**, with merge tags already in place so dates, times and join links fill themselves in.

### Check who the reminders come from {#check-sender}

Reminders use the webinar's [**Email Settings**](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/notifications/confirmation-emails/#email-settings): the **Sender name** registrants see and the **Reply-to email** where replies go. Set the sender to a name registrants will recognise from their confirmation.

## Text and WhatsApp reminders (Beta) {#sms-whatsapp}

Both Beta channels have a reminder of their own on the **Notifications** tab: **Reminder SMS** on the **SMS** tab and **Reminder Message** on the **Whatsapp** tab, each sent before the webinar starts. They work alongside email rather than replacing it, and suit the final reminder before a session, when an email may sit unread.

In short:

- **They need a phone number.** Add the **Phone** field to your [registration form](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/registration/custom-fields/#set-up-the-form), or there is nobody to message.
- **They come from an EasyWebinar number**, not your own.
- **You build them from templates** grouped by tone, then edit the words.
- **You can test first.** **Send Test SMS** and **Send Test Message** send the selected template to your own number. Pick your country from the flag dropdown rather than typing a dialling code.
- **Keep them short.** A reminder needs the time and the link.

Pick whichever channel your audience actually uses. Sending both tends to irritate people.

## How it works with other features {#works-with}

### Registration confirmation {#with-confirmation}

The confirmation starts the sequence and carries the join link; reminders bring the registrant back to it. Both are on the same tab and share the same sender settings.

### Evergreen and just-in-time sessions {#with-evergreen}

An [evergreen webinar](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/getting-started/create-an-evergreen-webinar/) generates a long run of sessions from one schedule, and can add [just-in-time sessions](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/automated/just-in-time/) that start shortly after someone registers. Reminders are timed relative to the webinar rather than to a clock, which is why they work for recurring and evergreen schedules without being set session by session.

### Analytics {#with-analytics}

The webinar's [**Analytics** tab](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/analytics/webinar-analytics/#read-the-funnel) shows the funnel from **Visitors** to **Registered**, **Live Attended**, **Total Attended** and **Missed**. Two gaps tell you different things:

| Gap | What a low number means |
|---|---|
| **Visitors** to **Registered** | A registration page problem: the headline, the form, the promise |
| **Registered** to attended | A notification problem, not a content one |

Change your reminders, run the next session, and compare.

### Follow-ups for people the reminders didn't reach {#with-follow-ups}

**Missed** counts people who registered and never showed up. Add a **Webinar Follow-up** with its audience set to no-shows and include the [replay link](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/media/replay-pages/#set-it-up). In [**People**](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/analytics/people/#filter-the-list), filter by **Behavior** for no-shows to see exactly who they are, then **Export** the list if you need it elsewhere.

### Reminders from your own CRM {#with-crm}

If you would rather send reminders from your [CRM or email tool](https://easywebinar.com/integrations/), [map the **Webinar Join Link**](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/integrations/map-webinar-data/#map-custom-fields) into one of its custom fields. Each contact then holds their own working link, which is what lets your system send the reminder instead of EasyWebinar.

### Cloning {#with-cloning}

**Email notifications** can come across when you [clone a webinar](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/getting-started/clone-a-webinar/#choose-what-to-copy), so a reminder schedule you have tuned carries into the copy.

## Limits and things to know {#limits}

- **Timing is relative to the webinar**, not a fixed clock time. Set it in hours, minutes and seconds before the session.
- **Reminders are set per webinar.** Use cloning to carry a schedule to a new webinar.
- **Reminders go to registrants.** They are written once per webinar and sent to the people who registered for it.
- **SMS and WhatsApp reminders are in Beta** and only reach people who gave a phone number on your registration form.
- **SMS and WhatsApp come from an EasyWebinar number**, so replies don't reach your phone.
- **Email stays the main channel.** Text and WhatsApp reminders work alongside email, not instead of it.

## Common questions {#common-questions}

#### How many reminders should I send? {#faq-how-many}

Three is a sensible default: a day before, an hour before, and fifteen minutes before. The last one does most of the work.

#### Can I add more than one reminder? {#faq-add-more}

Yes. Click **Create New** in the **Webinar Reminders** group, and set the timing for each.

#### How is a reminder's timing set? {#faq-timing}

As time before the webinar, in hours, minutes and seconds, for example *1h 0m 0s before webinar*.

#### Do reminders work on recurring and evergreen webinars? {#faq-evergreen}

Yes. Timing is relative to the webinar, not a fixed clock time, so it works for recurring and evergreen schedules.

#### Can I change what a reminder says? {#faq-edit}

Yes. Open the reminder to change its subject, content and timing.

#### How do I know if my reminders are working? {#faq-measure}

Compare **Registered** with attendance in the webinar's Analytics. A low registered-to-attended rate points to your notifications, not your content.

#### Can I send reminders by text or WhatsApp? {#faq-sms-whatsapp}

Both are in Beta. **Reminder SMS** and the WhatsApp **Reminder Message** send before the webinar starts, and both need the **Phone** field on your registration form.

#### Should I test a text or WhatsApp reminder first? {#faq-test}

Yes. **Send Test SMS** and **Send Test Message** send the selected template to your own number so you see exactly what registrants receive.

#### Does a webinar built with AI include reminders? {#faq-ai}

Yes. **Create with AI** drafts reminders for a day, an hour and fifteen minutes before, with merge tags for dates, times and join links.

#### What about people who still didn't turn up? {#faq-no-shows}

Add a **Webinar Follow-up** with its audience set to no-shows and include the replay link.

#### Can I send reminders from my CRM instead? {#faq-crm}

Yes. Map the **Webinar Join Link** into a custom field in your CRM so each contact has their own working link.

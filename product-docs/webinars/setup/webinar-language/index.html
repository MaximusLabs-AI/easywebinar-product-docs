01 · Before you begin

Before you begin

  • Open a live webinar. This page covers live webinars. If you don't have one yet, start with Create and schedule a live webinar.
  • Stay out of the room. You set Webinar Language from the webinar's settings, without entering the live room.
  • Write your own text in the language you want. Webinar Language sets the interface default, not your content. Your titles and page text stay as you wrote them.
  • Know which setting you need. Your dashboard language is a separate setting that Applies to the dashboard interface. Change your dashboard language covers it.
02 · Set it up

Step 1: Open the webinar's General settings

  1. Click My Webinars in the sidebar, then click the live webinar.
  2. Click the Settings tab, the last of the webinar's tabs.
  3. Stay on the General sub-tab. Webinar Language has its own section, and shows English by default.

03 · Set it up

Step 2: Choose the language

  1. Click the Webinar Language field. A list opens under the heading AVAILABLE LANGUAGES · 11, with a tick on the current language.

  3. Click the language attendees should get by default. The field then shows it, such as Spanish · Español.

Verify the result

  • The field shows your choice. Webinar Language reads the language you picked, such as Spanish · Español.

  • The list ticks it. Open the field again and your language has the tick.
06 · How it works

What Webinar Language is

Webinar Language is the EasyWebinar setting for the default language attendees get when they join a webinar. The host chooses it from the webinar's settings, without entering the live room. The list holds 11 languages.

The info icon beside Webinar Language opens a note headed What does this change? It says the setting picks "the language attendees see in supported webinar pages and interface text". It adds that it "does not translate spoken audio or uploaded videos". It also says "Exact coverage depends on your webinar configuration."

1 What does this change?

Webinar Language is separate from your dashboard language, whose helper text reads Applies to the dashboard interface. Date Display Language is another separate field, in the Date & Time Display section. Each one is set on its own. 07 · Why it matters

Why Webinar Language matters

What it's for

Webinar Language gives a webinar its own default language for attendees. You set it once, from the webinar's settings, before the session.

How it helps

  • Set on each webinar. Webinar Language sits on each webinar's own Settings tab.
  • No trip into the room. You choose Webinar Language from the webinar's settings, without entering the live room.
  • Your dashboard stays as it is. Your dashboard language is a separate setting under My Profile.
  • Languages are easy to spot. The Webinar Language list shows each language's own name underneath its English name.

What happens without it

Without a setting on the webinar, the attendee default would need sorting out somewhere else. Setting it close to the session is easy to forget.

Why buyers care

Webinar Language matters to teams that run webinars for audiences outside their own language. A webinar for coaches in Brazil, run in Portuguese, is one example. Both language settings offer the same 11 languages, so a host can work in the language the audience gets. Buyers comparing platforms should also read the Compare section below, which notes where rivals document more.

08 · Our approach

The EasyWebinar approach to Webinar Language

Take a typical case. A small team runs webinars for coaches. Its next webinar is for coaches in Brazil, so it runs in Portuguese. The team works in English and keeps its own dashboard in English.

A common approach is to leave the language until the day of the session. Someone then has to remember it while also checking the camera, the slides and the guest speaker.

The problem sits in timing. A setting left to the last minute is easy to miss, and the team's own dashboard language is a separate question.

EasyWebinar puts the setting on the webinar's Settings tab. The host opens General, picks Portuguese (Brazil) under Webinar Language and clicks Save Changes. That happens without entering the live room. The team's dashboard stays in English, under Settings → My Profile → General Settings → Language. The team writes its titles and page text in Portuguese, because Webinar Language sets the interface default, not the content.

The screenshot under Step 1 numbers Settings, General, Webinar Language, Date Display Language and Save Changes. How other platforms handle attendee languages is covered in How Webinar Language compares, below.

09 · Works with

How Webinar Language works with other features

Your dashboard language

Your dashboard language is separate from Webinar Language. It sits under Settings → My Profile → General Settings → Language, with the helper text Applies to the dashboard interface. Change your dashboard language covers it.

Date Display Language

Date Display Language is a separate field, set on its own. It sits with Date Format and Time Format in Date & Time Display, a section for how dates and times appear on your webinar pages. All of them are on the Settings tab, one of the webinar tabs the Overview tab page lists.

The Settings tab's other fields

The Attendee Experience & Redirects section holds Show Attendees, End of Session and Expired Session. End of Session redirects attendees after the live stream ends. Run a live webinar explains why to fill it in.

Registration page

Your registration page says whatever you write in the registration page builder. Webinar Language sets the interface default, not your content.

Emails

You write your registration confirmation email and your reminders yourself. Webinar Language sets the interface default, not your content.

Creating a live webinar

Webinar Language sits on a webinar that already exists. Create and schedule a live webinar first, then open its Settings tab.

11 · Compare

How Webinar Language compares

EasyWebinar sets a default language for attendees on each webinar, from its settings, without entering the live room. Livestorm, Demio and ON24 state more attendee languages than EasyWebinar's 11. Livestorm, Demio and Zoom also document email language, which this page doesn't cover for EasyWebinar. Livestorm's article says changing the event language translates its landing and registration pages, live event room and automated emails. Demio's account default language sets registration page buttons, dates and times, and system emails. ON24 adds a language dropdown to registration and lobby pages through an AI Translation Package from its account team.

Why EasyWebinar is the stronger choice for Webinar Language

  • Host and attendees share one list. EasyWebinar offers the same 11 languages for the dashboard and for Webinar Language. Demio's help centre says its host dashboard is available only in English. Compare EasyWebinar vs Demio.
  • More dashboard languages than Livestorm. EasyWebinar's dashboard comes in the same 11 languages as Webinar Language. Livestorm's article lists its dashboard in English, French, German and Spanish. Compare EasyWebinar vs Livestorm.
  • You choose the default for each webinar. In EasyWebinar, the host sets the attendee default on the webinar. Zoom says its registration page is shown in the language of the registrant's Zoom profile, if available. Compare EasyWebinar vs Zoom.

12 · Limits

Limits and things to know

  • This page covers live webinars. It describes Webinar Language as it appears on a live webinar's Settings tab.
  • There are 11 languages. Italian isn't one of them.
  • Your own content isn't translated. Webinar Language sets the interface default, not your titles, page text or the emails you write.
  • Date Display Language is a separate field. It sits in Date & Time Display and is set on its own.
  • It doesn't translate audio or video. The app's own note says Webinar Language "does not translate spoken audio or uploaded videos".
  • Coverage depends on the webinar. The same note says exact coverage depends on your webinar configuration.
  • Nothing is kept until you save. Click Save Changes before you leave the page.
  • It isn't your dashboard language. That's a separate setting under My Profile.
  • The default is English. A new live webinar's Webinar Language shows English.
13 · Questions

Common questions

Where do I set the webinar language in EasyWebinar?

On the webinar's Settings tab → General → Webinar Language. Open the webinar from My Webinars first.

Do I need to enter the live room to set the webinar language?

No. In EasyWebinar, the host sets Webinar Language from the webinar's settings, without entering the live room.

What does Webinar Language set?

EasyWebinar's Webinar Language sets the default language attendees get when they join. It sets the interface default, not your own content.

How many languages does Webinar Language offer?

EasyWebinar's Webinar Language list is headed AVAILABLE LANGUAGES · 11. The 11 are English, Spanish, Japanese, French, German, Portuguese (Brazil), Chinese (Simplified), Korean, Hindi, Danish and Arabic.

Is Italian available as a webinar language?

No. Italian isn't one of the 11 languages in EasyWebinar's Webinar Language list.

What is the default webinar language?

English. A live webinar's Webinar Language field in EasyWebinar shows English by default.

Is Webinar Language the same as my dashboard language?

No. Your EasyWebinar dashboard language Applies to the dashboard interface and lives under My Profile. Webinar Language is set on each webinar.

Is Date Display Language the same as Webinar Language?

No. Date Display Language is a separate field next to Webinar Language on EasyWebinar's General settings, and you set it on its own.

Will Webinar Language translate my webinar title and page text?

No. EasyWebinar's Webinar Language sets the interface default for attendees, not your content. Your titles and page text stay as you wrote them.

Does each webinar have its own language?

Yes. EasyWebinar's Webinar Language sits on each webinar's own Settings tab, so you set it per webinar.

Do I need to click Save Changes after choosing a webinar language?

Yes. EasyWebinar keeps Webinar Language only after you click Save Changes on the General sub-tab. The app then shows General settings saved successfully.

Which settings sit next to Webinar Language?

On EasyWebinar's General sub-tab, Webinar Language has its own section. Date & Time Display sits above it and Attendee Experience & Redirects below.

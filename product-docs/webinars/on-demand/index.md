# On Demand (instant replay)

How On Demand works on EasyWebinar's evergreen webinars, letting registrants start watching straight away, how to label its button, and how it differs from the replay page.

- Applies to: Evergreen webinars
- Status: Live
- Verified: 2026-10-16
- Canonical page: https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/on-demand/

**On Demand** is an evergreen schedule setting that lets a registrant start watching your webinar straight away, with no session to wait for. EasyWebinar also calls it **instant replay**: registrants click a button, labelled *Watch instant replay* unless you change it, and the recording starts. It is a different thing from the replay page on the **Replay** tab, which shows the recording of a session that has already happened.

## At a glance {#at-a-glance}

| | |
|---|---|
| **What it does** | Lets registrants watch immediately instead of waiting for a session |
| **Also called** | Instant replay |
| **Where it's set** | The schedule step of an evergreen webinar |
| **Webinar types** | Evergreen |
| **Button wording** | **Instant replay text**; reads *Watch instant replay* by default |
| **Combines with** | **Just-in-time Registration** and **Recurring Schedule** on the same webinar |
| **Not the same as** | The replay page on the **Replay** tab |

## What On Demand is {#what-it-is}

An evergreen webinar can offer three ways to watch. On Demand is the one with no wait at all: the registrant doesn't pick a session time, they start the recording when they're ready.

| Option | When the registrant watches |
|---|---|
| **On Demand** | Immediately |
| **Just-in-time Registration** | In a session starting a few minutes after they register |
| **Recurring Schedule** | At a set daily or weekly time |

Any combination can be switched on, and the registration page lists each enabled option as a choice. Just-in-time has its own page: [Just-in-time webinars](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/automated/just-in-time/).

### On Demand and the replay page are different {#on-demand-vs-replay}

The names overlap, so the two are easy to mix up.

| | On Demand (instant replay) | Replay page |
|---|---|---|
| **What it plays** | The evergreen webinar's recording, whenever a registrant chooses | The recording of a session that has already happened |
| **Who it's for** | People who want to watch now rather than wait for a session | People who missed a session and want to catch up |
| **Where you set it** | The schedule step of an evergreen webinar | The **Replay** tab of a webinar |
| **What you configure** | On or off, and the **Instant replay text** | Whether the page is on, how long it stays available, redirects and how the page looks |

A simple rule: if you want people to watch the moment they register, use On Demand on an evergreen webinar. If you want people who missed a session to catch up, use the replay page. Replay page settings are covered in [Replay pages](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/media/replay-pages/).

## Why On Demand matters {#why-it-matters}

### What it's for {#what-its-for}

On Demand is for evergreen webinars where the content is the point, not the occasion: training, product explainers, onboarding material and other sessions people want to get through on their own timetable.

### How it helps {#how-it-helps}

- **No waiting at all.** A registrant who is ready now can watch now.
- **It suits people short on time.** Someone who has twenty minutes free between meetings doesn't have to fit a session around them.
- **It sits alongside scheduled options.** Registrants who'd rather turn up for a session can still pick a just-in-time or recurring one.
- **The button speaks your language.** **Instant replay text** lets you word the button to match your page.

### What happens without it {#without-it}

Without On Demand, every registrant has to wait for a session, even if only a few minutes with just-in-time. For content people want to consume as soon as they find it, any wait is a chance for them to leave and not return.

### Why buyers care {#why-buyers-care}

Teams using evergreen webinars for education and onboarding want people to get the content when they ask for it. On Demand gives registrants immediate access from the same registration page, recording and webinar setup.

## Set it up {#set-it-up}

### On a new evergreen webinar {#set-up-new}

1. On **My Webinars**, click **Create a Webinar**, then **Create Manually**.
2. Name the webinar, choose **Evergreen Webinar**, and click **Continue to Schedule**.
3. Switch on **On Demand**.
4. In **Instant replay text**, keep *Watch instant replay* or type your own wording.
5. Set any other options you want alongside it: **Just-in-time Registration**, **Recurring Schedule**, **Availability** and **Schedule Preferences**.
6. Click **Continue to Pricing** and complete the remaining steps.

*1 Just-in-time Registration · 2 On Demand · 3 Recurring Schedule · 4 Availability · 5 Schedule Preferences.*

For pricing, video selection and templates, see [Create an evergreen webinar](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/getting-started/create-an-evergreen-webinar/).

### On an existing evergreen webinar {#set-up-existing}

On the webinar's **Overview** tab, the options menu on the **Scheduling Options** panel changes the schedule for the whole webinar. The same panel shows the frequency, start date, timezone and the preference you chose.

### Settings {#settings}

| Setting | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **On Demand** | Whether registrants can start watching straight away |
| **Instant replay text** | The wording on the button registrants click; *Watch instant replay* by default |
| **Schedule Preferences** | The choices registrants see; with just-in-time also on, it reads **Display Just-in-time & Instant-replay** |

### Wording the button {#button-wording}

The default *Watch instant replay* works, but the word "replay" can suggest a recording of something people missed. If your page sells the webinar as fresh content, wording that says what happens next, such as starting the training now, can read more naturally. Whatever you choose, keep it short enough to fit a button.

## When to use On Demand, and when to leave it off {#when-to-use}

The deciding question is whether the webinar is valued for its content or for the occasion.

| Use On Demand when | Leave it off when |
|---|---|
| The content matters more than the sense of an event | You want the scheduled session to feel like something people turn up for |

With On Demand off, registrants still have just-in-time or recurring sessions if you've enabled them.

## How On Demand works with other features {#works-with}

### Just-in-time Registration {#with-just-in-time}

Just-in-time offers a session a few minutes away, in each attendee's local time. With both on, registrants choose between watching now and joining a session shortly. See [Just-in-time webinars](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/automated/just-in-time/).

### Recurring Schedule {#with-recurring}

A daily or weekly schedule can run alongside On Demand for registrants who want to book a time. Recurring sessions run in the webinar's timezone.

### The replay page {#with-replay-page}

The **Replay** tab controls a separate page for people who missed a session, with availability options of **Always Available**, **Expire after days** or **Specific date**, and an **Experience** of **Webinar Replica** or **Custom Build**. Those settings belong to the replay page, not to On Demand. See [Replay pages](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/media/replay-pages/).

### Polls, offers and chat {#with-engagement}

Chat, Q&A, polls, offers, emails and analytics work on evergreen webinars as they do everywhere else. Where viewers watch at different times, **Chat Simulation** on the **Engagement** tab keeps the chat from looking empty. See [Chat simulation](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/engagement/chat-simulation/).

### Emails {#with-notifications}

Confirmation, reminder and follow-up emails are set on the **Notifications** tab. Replay emails are set up there too, not on the **Replay** tab. See [Confirmation emails](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/notifications/confirmation-emails/).

### Plan usage {#with-plan-usage}

People watching evergreen sessions count towards the **Automated attendees** meter on the **Plan** tab.

## Who uses On Demand, and how {#who-uses-it}

These are typical patterns, shown to illustrate how the setting is used.

### Customer success teams {#customer-success}

A customer success team runs its product onboarding as an evergreen webinar with On Demand on. New customers register and start the walkthrough immediately, and the button reads *Start onboarding* instead of the default.

### SaaS and product teams {#saas-teams}

A product team offers a recorded feature walkthrough with On Demand alongside just-in-time. Evaluators in a hurry watch straight away; others join a session a few minutes later.

### Course creators {#course-creators}

A creator offers a free training lesson on demand, so new subscribers can start it the moment they sign up. On a separate sales webinar, the creator leaves On Demand off so the session feels like an event people turn up for. See [Selling online courses with webinars](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/use-cases/sell-courses/).

### Coaches and consultants {#coaches}

A consultant puts an explainer webinar on demand for prospects to watch before a first call, so the call can start from where the webinar left off.

## Limits and things to know {#limits}

- **Evergreen only.** On Demand is set on the evergreen schedule step. Live and simulive webinars don't have it.
- **It isn't the replay page.** Replay availability, deadlines, redirects and the replay page's look are set on the **Replay** tab for the replay page.
- **The recording is the webinar's.** An evergreen webinar plays the recording chosen on its video step; change it from the **Webinar Video** panel on the **Content** tab.
- **The type is fixed.** To offer On Demand for a live or simulive webinar, clone it as **Evergreen**. See [Clone a webinar](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/getting-started/clone-a-webinar/).

## Common questions {#common-questions}

#### What does On Demand do? {#faq-what-it-does}

It lets someone watch immediately, with no session to wait for.

#### Is On Demand the same as instant replay? {#faq-instant-replay}

Yes. They're two names for the same evergreen setting.

#### What's the difference between instant replay and a replay? {#faq-vs-replay}

A replay is the recording of a past session, on the **Replay** tab. Instant replay, or On Demand, lets someone watch an evergreen webinar straight away without waiting for a session.

#### Can I change the button text? {#faq-button-text}

Yes. Edit **Instant replay text** on the schedule step. It reads *Watch instant replay* by default.

#### Can I offer On Demand and scheduled sessions together? {#faq-together}

Yes. Turn on **Just-in-time Registration**, **Recurring Schedule** or both alongside it, and the registration page offers each as a choice.

#### Where do I turn On Demand on? {#faq-where}

On the schedule step when you set up an evergreen webinar, not on the **Replay** tab.

#### Can a live or simulive webinar have On Demand? {#faq-other-types}

No. It's an evergreen setting. Clone the webinar as **Evergreen** to use it.

#### I want people who missed a session to catch up. Is On Demand the right tool? {#faq-catch-up}

No. Use the replay page on the **Replay** tab for that.

#### Should every evergreen webinar have On Demand on? {#faq-always-on}

Not necessarily. Turn it on when the content matters more than the event feeling, and leave it off when you want a scheduled session people turn up for.

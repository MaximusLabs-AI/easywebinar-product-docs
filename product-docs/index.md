## What EasyWebinar is {#what-easywebinar-is}

EasyWebinar is a webinar platform for running live and recorded webinars that collect leads and sell. You build a webinar with a [registration page](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/registration/registration-page-builder/), [emails](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/notifications/confirmation-emails/), [polls](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/engagement/polls/) and [offers](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/engagement/timed-offers/), run it live or from a recording, and follow up through [analytics](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/analytics/webinar-analytics/), integrations and the built-in [EasyCRM](https://easywebinar.com/easycrm/).

## How the parts connect {#how-it-connects}

A webinar's registration page collects registrants and can take payment through your own Stripe account. Registrants get [reminder emails](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/notifications/reminder-emails/) from the Notifications tab, and SMS or WhatsApp messages, both currently in Beta. They join the room live, or a recording plays at a scheduled time ([simulive](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/getting-started/create-a-simulive-webinar/)) or on demand ([evergreen](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/getting-started/create-an-evergreen-webinar/)). Attendance, offer clicks and revenue flow into the webinar's Analytics and the [People view](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/analytics/people/), and out to [connected CRMs and email tools](https://easywebinar.com/integrations/), or into EasyCRM.

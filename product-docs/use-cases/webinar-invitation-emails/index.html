Where webinar invitation emails fit

Say you're a leadership coach with 800 past clients and newsletter readers in a spreadsheet. Your free live session on giving hard feedback is three weeks away. The registration page is built, but nobody on that list knows it exists. A webinar invitation is the email that closes that gap: the first message that turns a contact into a registrant.

What they're for

An invitation moves people from your list onto your registration page. After they register, other emails take over: the registration confirmation goes out immediately, and reminders go out before the session starts. The invitation only has to earn the click.

How they help

  • They reach your warmest audience first. People who already know your work are the likeliest to register.
  • They fill seats before you pay for any other promotion. Your list costs nothing extra to email.
  • They give you a number to beat. Opens and clicks show whether the subject or the offer is the weak part.
  • They set up the rest of the sequence. Every registrant they bring in gets your confirmation and reminders automatically.

What happens without them

Without an invitation, the people most likely to come never hear about the session. The registration page waits for search or social traffic that may not arrive, and your list goes cold while you post into feeds.

Why coaches and consultants care

For a coach or consultant, a webinar is often the start of a booked call or a new client. A room of people who already trust you is worth more than a room of strangers. The invitation decides how full that room gets.

When to send a webinar invitation

There's no single rule for how far ahead to invite people. This plan is a sensible starting point, because it leaves time for people to plan and still lands close enough to feel current.

WhenWhat to sendWho gets it
Two to three weeks beforeThe first invitationYour whole list, or the segment the topic suits
About a week beforeA second invitation with a new angleThe same list, with a line for people who already registered
The day beforeNothing from you to the listRegistrants get your reminders from the webinar instead

Evergreen and just-in-time webinars change the timing. A recorded webinar that runs every day, like an evergreen webinar, doesn't need a countdown. Invite people when you launch it, then again when you change the topic or the offer.

What makes a webinar invitation work

Every line of an invitation has a job. If a line doesn't help someone decide to register, cut it.

A sender people recognise

People decide whether to open an email by who sent it. Nielsen Norman Group's research found that unrecognisable sender information was the top reason people gave for not opening email (2008). Send from your own name or a name your list knows, not a brand they've forgotten.

A subject line that names the outcome

The subject line carries most of the open. Nielsen Norman Group advises showing no more than about 40 characters and putting the useful words first, so nothing important is cut off (2014). For more subject lines to test, read our guide to webinar email subject lines that increase registrations.

The problem before the product

Open with the reader's problem in a sentence they'd say themselves. Then say what they'll be able to do after the session. Your name, your credentials and the webinar title can wait until the second paragraph.

Date, time and time zone in one line

Write the date, the start time, the time zone and the length together, so nobody has to hunt. If your list spans countries, give a second time zone in brackets.

One link, said twice

Use one registration link and repeat it: once as a button or plain link after the opening, and once at the end. Two different links split your clicks and make results harder to read.

A reason to register now

Give an honest reason to act this week: a live question-and-answer slot, a limited number of seats, or a handout for people who attend. Don't invent scarcity you won't keep.

Webinar invitation email template

Copy this template and replace every field in square brackets. It's a plain-text pattern for you to fill in, not a template stored inside EasyWebinar.

Subject line options (pick one):

  • [Outcome] in [length]: live on [day]
  • Can I show you how to [outcome]?
  • [Problem in four words]? Join me [day]

Preview text: A free live session on [topic], [date] at [time] [time zone].

Body:

Hi [first name],

[One sentence naming the problem in the reader's words, for example: "Most managers I work with put off hard feedback until it becomes a bigger problem."]

On [day, date] at [time] [time zone], I'm running a free [length]-minute live session on [topic]. You'll leave with [specific result 1] and [specific result 2].

[Registration link: Save your seat]

Here's what we'll cover:

  • [Point 1]
  • [Point 2]
  • [Point 3]

[One line on why you: "I've [relevant credential or experience]."]

I'll stay on for questions at the end, so bring the situation you're stuck on. [Or your honest reason to register now.]

[Registration link: Register for [day]]

[Your name] [Your business name and postal address] [Unsubscribe link]

The second invitation

Should you send a second invitation to people who didn't register? Yes, once, with a new angle rather than the same email again. Lead with one question the session answers, or with what a real attendee will be able to do.

Hi [first name],

Quick one: if [specific situation], this is the session for you.

[Day] at [time] [time zone], I'm covering [the one thing that matters most]. It's free and live, and there's time for your questions.

[Registration link: Save your seat]

Already registered? Thank you. You can ignore this, and your confirmation email has your join details.

[Your name] [Your business name and postal address] [Unsubscribe link]

Invitation mistakes that are easy to make

  • Sending before sending is set up. In EasyCRM nothing goes out until a provider is connected and your sender is verified, so do it days before, not on the morning.
  • Pasting a private link. The Co-presenter Join Link and Moderator Join Link for Live room let anyone join your room in that role. Only the registration, attendee and replay links are meant for your audience.
  • Forgetting the time zone. A time without a zone sends half a mixed list to the wrong hour.
  • Two different links. One link, repeated, gives you a click count you can trust.
  • Inviting the whole list to a narrow topic. A saved list for the segment the topic suits protects your open rate.

What the law and mailbox providers ask of an invitation

A webinar invitation to your list is usually a marketing email, so these rules apply to it. This is a summary, not legal advice.

United States: an opt-out and a postal address

The CAN-SPAM Act requires commercial email to carry a clear notice that people can decline further email, and "a valid physical postal address of the sender". It also bars a subject heading likely to "mislead a recipient". The template above leaves room for both lines.

United Kingdom: a stated purpose

UK data protection law says personal data must be "used for specified, explicit purposes" and handled fairly, lawfully and transparently. Invite people whose details you collected for this kind of contact.

Gmail: unsubscribe and spam rates

Google's email sender guidelines say marketing messages must support one-click unsubscribe and show a visible unsubscribe link. Every sender should keep the spam rate in Postmaster Tools below 0.3%. Extra rules apply above 5,000 messages a day to Gmail accounts.

How to send a webinar invitation by hand

If you're not using a CRM yet, this is the manual way to do the job.

  1. Copy the public registration link from your webinar platform.
  2. Export your contacts to a spreadsheet and remove anyone who has unsubscribed.
  3. Write the invitation and a second version with a new angle.
  4. Send a test to yourself and click every link.
  5. Send the first invitation from your email tool and note the date.
  6. A week later, check registrations against the list and send the second invitation.
  7. Count opens, clicks and registrations for each send in a spreadsheet.

The weak points are steps 2 and 7. The list goes out of date, and registrations live somewhere other than the opens and clicks.

See whether your invitation worked

Three numbers tell you how an invitation did: opens, clicks and registrations. Each one points at a different line of the email.

MeasureWhere you see itWhat a weak figure points to
Open rateAvg open rate on the Broadcasts screen, against a 21% benchmarkThe subject line or the sender
Click rateAvg click rate on the Broadcasts screen, against a 2.6% benchmarkThe body or the reason to register
RegistrationsThe webinar's analytics and its Overview tabThe registration page, if clicks are healthy

A low open rate is usually a subject line or sender problem. A good open rate with a low click rate means people opened the email and the offer didn't land.

How far to trust these numbers

The rates on the Broadcasts screen are averages across all your broadcasts, not the figures for one send. Write down the totals after each invitation so you can see how that send moved the average. Registrations also come from other places, such as social posts or an embedded form on your site, so not every registrant came from the email.

Who sends webinar invitations

Coaches and consultants

Small businesses and solo operators

B2B marketing teams

Agencies

Common questions about webinar invitations

What should a webinar invitation email include?

A webinar invitation should name the reader's problem, say what they'll leave with, and give the date, time, time zone and length. It needs one registration link, repeated, plus your name, postal address and an unsubscribe link.

How far ahead should you send a webinar invitation?

A sensible starting plan is a first invitation two to three weeks before a live webinar and a second about a week before. After people register, your confirmation and reminder emails take over.

Should you send a second invitation to people who didn't register?

Yes, once, with a new angle rather than the same email. Add a line telling people who already registered that they can ignore it, and point them to their confirmation email.

Is there a webinar invitation template I can copy?

Yes. This page has a fill-in webinar invitation template with subject line options, preview text and body, plus a second invitation. Replace every field in square brackets before you send.

Which link should go in a webinar invite email?

Use the Registration Page Link from the webinar's Share dialog for anyone. For contacts you already hold, the One-Click Registration Link registers them without the form. Never send the presenter or moderator links.

What is a one-click registration link?

It's a registration link that carries a contact's name and email in the URL. A known contact clicks it and is registered without filling in the form, so it only works for people already on your list.

Where do I send webinar invitations in EasyWebinar?

Send them as a broadcast from Marketing → Broadcasts in EasyCRM. Click New Broadcast, choose the channel, pick the list, write the message, then send or schedule it.

Why can't I send my broadcast?

Sending usually isn't set up yet. In EasyCRM, check Configurations: you need a connected SMTP provider and a verified domain or business email address before anything goes out.

What is a good open rate for a webinar invitation?

The Broadcasts screen sets your average open rate beside a 21% industry benchmark, and your average click rate beside 2.6%. Those figures are averages across all your broadcasts, so note each send's totals yourself.

Can I schedule a webinar invitation?

Yes. An EasyCRM broadcast can be sent straight away or scheduled for later, so you can write both invitations at once and set their send times.

Do I need EasyCRM to send invitations?

No. You can keep HubSpot, Salesforce Pardot (Account Engagement), ActiveCampaign, Mailchimp or Kit, or use EasyCRM natively. EasyCRM is built in from the Growth plan.

Do invitations count against my email allowance?

Yes. EasyCRM broadcasts draw on your Wallet, which includes 1,000 emails a month. The allowance resets on your signup date, not the calendar month.

How long should a webinar invitation subject line be?

Short enough that the useful words show. Nielsen Norman Group advises displaying no more than about 40 characters and putting the most important words first.

What's the difference between a webinar announcement and an invitation?

An announcement tells people a webinar exists, often on social media or your site. An invitation is a personal email to your list with a registration link, asking each person to save a seat.

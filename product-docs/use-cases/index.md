## Where each type of webinar fits {#where-it-fits}

Take a typical case. A small travel company sells guided walking holidays and wants more bookings from people who are still deciding. A live talk once a month works, but the team can't present every evening for every time zone. What they need is a set-up that matches that job, not a list of features.

A webinar type is the way your content reaches people. EasyWebinar offers three types of webinar, and you choose one when you create it. The choice sets your schedule and how attendees take part. All three use the same pages, emails, polls, offers and analytics, so only the delivery differs.

### Live {#live}

You present in real time, and attendees join at the scheduled moment. Chat and questions happen as they happen. A [live webinar](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/live/) is best for a launch, a workshop or any session where people come for the conversation. It is not the right fit when nobody can be there at the set time, every time.

### Simulive {#simulive}

You record the session in advance, and it plays at set times. Everyone in a session watches together, and the recording plays while you and your moderators answer in the chat live. Simulive suits a talk you give again and again, or a session in a time zone you'd rather sleep through. It is not for a session you haven't recorded yet.

### Evergreen {#evergreen}

The recording runs on a repeating schedule, with each registrant picking their own time. Evergreen suits a demo, an onboarding session or an offer you run all year. It is not the best fit for a one-off event where the live questions are the point. The [automated webinar overview](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/automated/) explains how the schedules work together.

### Which one to pick {#which-to-pick}

| If you want | Pick |
|---|---|
| To answer questions as they come, in real time | Live |
| A polished recording at set times, with you in the chat | Simulive |
| A session that runs all the time and lets each person choose | Evergreen |
| To run the same session weekly without presenting it again | Simulive or Evergreen |

Live and Simulive both need you to pick dates, while Evergreen handles the schedule for you. Our walking-holiday company would run the talk live first, then turn it into an evergreen webinar for the evenings nobody can present.

## Choose your starting point {#start}

Work through these steps in order. Most set-up problems come from skipping the first two.

1. **Name the job.** Write one sentence about what the webinar must achieve, such as "book ten discovery calls". Match it to a row in the table at the top.
2. **Pick the format.** Choose Live, Simulive or Evergreen from the table above. If the format needs a recording, have it ready first.
3. **Choose how to build it.** In **Create a Webinar**, pick **Create with AI** or **Create Manually**.
4. **Follow the guide for your format.** Use the steps to [create a live webinar](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/getting-started/create-a-live-webinar/), [create a simulive webinar](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/getting-started/create-a-simulive-webinar/) or [create an evergreen webinar](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/getting-started/create-an-evergreen-webinar/).
5. **Plan the follow-up before the session.** Decide what attendees and no-shows each receive, using the [follow-up email guide](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/use-cases/webinar-follow-up-emails/).

## What goes wrong, and what to check {#what-goes-wrong}

**You picked the wrong format.** You can't change the type later. Clone the webinar and choose the right type on the copy. Cloning is also how you turn a live webinar into an evergreen one, as the [guide to turning a live session into an evergreen one](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/automated/live-to-evergreen/) explains.

**There's no recording yet.** Simulive and Evergreen need a recording, but Live webinars don't. If you haven't recorded anything, run it live first and use that recording later.

**Most registrants don't turn up.** Reminders are where attendance is won or lost. Check your [reminder emails](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/notifications/reminder-emails/), and send no-shows a follow-up with the [replay page](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/media/replay-pages/).

**Your audience speaks another language.** Language is set per webinar, so each webinar can run in its own language. Language settings don't translate your content, though, so write your pages and emails in your audience's language.

**Your leads stay in the webinar tool.** Plan where contacts go before you run the session. You can bring registrants and attendees into [EasyCRM](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/easycrm/), or send them to the CRM you already use.

## Common questions {#common-questions}

#### What are webinars used for? {#what-are-webinars-used-for}

Webinars are used to sell courses and programmes, win clients, bring in sales leads, launch products and onboard customers. The job decides the format. A live session suits real-time questions, while a recording on a schedule suits sessions you repeat.

#### What are the main types of webinars? {#main-types}

EasyWebinar offers three: Live, Simulive and Evergreen. Live is presented in real time. Simulive plays a recording at set times, with you in the chat. Evergreen plays a recording on a repeating schedule, and each registrant picks their own time.

#### Which type of webinar is best for a sales funnel? {#best-for-a-funnel}

Evergreen, in most cases, because it runs without you being there. Many people run the session live a few times first to sharpen it. Then they clone it as Evergreen and use the recording.

#### Can I change a webinar from live to evergreen? {#change-type}

Not directly, because the type is fixed when you create the webinar. Clone the webinar and choose Evergreen on the copy. That is the supported way to turn a live session that works into an evergreen one.

#### Can I still talk to attendees on a recorded webinar? {#talk-on-recorded}

Yes, on Simulive. The recording plays while you and your moderators answer in the chat live. Attendees can also send questions through [questions and answers](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/engagement/questions-answers/), which keeps them apart from the chat.

#### Do I need to record something before I start? {#need-a-recording}

Only for Simulive and Evergreen, because both play a recording. For a Live webinar you supply the content by presenting, so you need nothing recorded in advance.

#### Do I have to give up my CRM? {#keep-your-crm}

No. EasyWebinar works with your CRM or ours. You can keep the CRM you use today and send webinar data to it, or use EasyCRM in the same product.

#### Can EasyWebinar host my course? {#host-my-course}

No. EasyWebinar is not a course platform. It runs the webinar that sells your course, and the course itself stays on the platform you already use.

#### Can AI write the webinar for me? {#ai-writes-it}

It can draft it. The [AI webinar overview](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/ai/) explains what AI drafts before the session and what you still review. You stay in charge of the final wording.

# Team members and roles

Give colleagues their own EasyWebinar login, choose Admin or User, and understand how team members differ from presenters, moderators and client workspaces.

- Applies to: All plans · seats depend on your plan · Owner or Admin
- Status: Live
- Verified: 2026-10-16
- Canonical page: https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/account/team-members/

Give each colleague who [builds webinars](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/getting-started/create-a-live-webinar/) or [reads your analytics](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/analytics/webinar-analytics/) their own EasyWebinar login, as an **Admin** or a **User**. Sharing one login means you can't tell who did what, and only one of you can be in the [live room](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/live-room/run-a-live-webinar/) as host. When you finish, the new member is listed under **Settings → Team** and can sign in with the details you give them.

## At a glance {#at-a-glance}

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | **Settings** → **Team** |
| **Roles** | **Owner** (one per account), **Admin** or **User** |
| **Seats** | Part of your plan; the **Settings** overview shows seats used |
| **Invitation** | None. You set the password and pass the login on yourself |
| **Changing a role** | The **Actions** column on the member's row |
| **Guests on one webinar** | Join with a Co-presenter or Moderator link, with no account |
| **Agency clients** | A client workspace, which doesn't use a team seat |

## Before you begin {#before-you-begin}

- Check your seat allowance on the **Settings** overview, for example *2 of 10 seats used*. Seats are [part of your plan](https://easywebinar.com/pricing/).
- Each member needs their own email address.
- Decide on a role for each person. The Roles section below explains the difference.

## Open the Team screen {#team-screen}

Click **Settings** in the sidebar, then **Team**.

![The Team screen listing members and their roles](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/images/team/step-01-team-list.png)

| | Area | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Add user** | Creates a new teammate |
| 2 | **Column headings** | Member, Role, Joined, Actions |
| 3 | **Role** | Owner, Admin or User |

## Add a team member {#add-a-team-member}

1. Click **Add user**.
2. Enter their **Name** and **Email**, and optionally a **Photo**.
3. Set a **Password** and **Confirm password**.
4. Choose a **Role**: **Admin** or **User**.
5. Click **Add user**.

![The Add user dialog with name, email, password and role](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/images/team/step-02-add-user.png)

> **No invitation email is sent**
> You set their password and nothing is emailed to them. There is no invitation to accept, so pass the login details on yourself and ask them to change the password once they are in.


## Roles {#roles}

| Role | Access |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | The account holder. One per account |
| **Admin** | "Full access to everything in the organization including all settings." |
| **User** | "Basic access to organization features." |

### Which role to give {#which-role}

Give **Admin** to people who need to change billing, team or account settings. **User** suits [presenters and moderators](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/live-room/green-room/#add-presenters) and anyone who works on webinars without touching the account.

### Changing roles and ownership {#change-role}

Change someone's role from the **Actions** column on their row. The **Owner** can't be changed from this screen; contact support if ownership needs to move.

## Team members, presenters and moderators {#presenters-and-moderators}

A **team member** has a login. A **presenter** or **moderator** runs a specific webinar and is added on that webinar's [Overview tab](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/setup/overview-tab/#team-and-moderators). Presenters and moderators can join with a link, without an account.

- Add someone to your team when they work in EasyWebinar regularly.
- Send a **Co-presenter** or **Moderator** join link from the [Share dialog](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/registration/sharing-links/) when they are a guest on one session.

## Verify the result {#verify-the-result}

The new member appears in the Team list with their role and joined date, and the seat count on the **Settings** overview goes up by one.

## What team members are {#what-it-is}

A team member is a person with their own login on your EasyWebinar account. Their role sets how much of the account they can change. **Admin** reaches every setting, billing included, while **User** gets basic access to features. Access is set by role alone, with no per-permission matrix.

Team members are one of three ways to bring people in:

| | What it is | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **Team member** | A login on your account | A colleague who works across your webinars |
| **Presenter or moderator** | Someone on one webinar | A guest speaker, who needs no account |
| **Client workspace** | A separate account with its own plan | An agency client whose work stays separate |

## Why team members matter {#why-it-matters}

### What it's for {#what-its-for}

Team members let colleagues work in your account under their own names. Each person signs in separately, so nobody passes a password around. The role you pick decides whether they can reach billing and settings.

### How it helps {#how-it-helps}

- **You know who did what.** Separate logins replace a shared one, which leaves you guessing.
- **More than one person can host.** With one shared login, only one of you can be in the live room as host.
- **Access fits the job.** **Admin** suits people who handle billing and settings. **User** suits people who build and run webinars.
- **Guests stay guests.** A speaker on one session joins by link and needs no account.

### What happens without it {#without-it}

Everyone works from one login. Nobody can tell who changed a webinar or a setting, and only one person can host the live room at a time. Anyone holding that password can also reach billing and every account setting, whether their job needs it or not.

### Why buyers care {#why-buyers-care}

This starts to matter when a second person joins the webinar work. That might be a producer building pages, a moderator in the chat, or a manager checking results. It matters most to agencies, whose own staff and each client's work need clear lines between them.

## The EasyWebinar approach to team members {#approach}

{/* 5P */}

Take a typical case. A six-person digital marketing agency runs monthly lead-generation webinars for four accounting firms. Two producers build the registration pages, an account manager hosts, and a junior marketer moderates the chat. Each firm expects its own leads and settings to stay private.

A common approach is to share one login and keep every client's webinars in the same account. That tends to hold until two people need the room at once, or someone edits the wrong firm's webinar. Billing sits one click away from everyone, and nobody can say who changed what. The cost is usually small at first, then grows with every new client.

The webinars themselves don't need to change. What changes is who signs in, and where each client's work lives. Staff need their own names on their own logins, and each firm needs a space of its own.

EasyWebinar handles both from **Settings**. The agency owner adds the producers and the moderator with **Add user** as **User**, and gives the account manager **Admin**. Each accounting firm then gets a client workspace: a separate account the agency manages, on its own plan. Workspaces don't use team seats, so adding a fifth firm leaves the staff logins as they are.

The Team screen pictured under Open the Team screen lists each member with a **Role** column reading **Owner**, **Admin** or **User**. How other platforms handle team logins, guest presenters and client accounts is covered in How team members compares, below.

## How team members work with other features {#works-with}

### The Overview tab {#with-overview-tab}

Each webinar's **Team & Moderators** panel shows everyone who can run that webinar. **Add presenter** adds someone who appears on camera and presents. **Invite** sends an invitation to join the webinar team. The Overview page explains [how presenters differ from team members](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/setup/overview-tab/#team-members-vs-presenters).

### Join links {#with-share-links}

The Share dialog holds a [Co-presenter Join Link](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/registration/sharing-links/#co-presenter-join-link) for a presenter and a **Moderator Join Link for Live room** for someone who handles chat and questions. Treat both as private. Anyone holding one can join your room in that role.

### The live room {#with-live-room}

Co-hosts and moderators who join land [backstage, not on stage](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/live-room/green-room/#manage-backstage). Your audience doesn't see or hear them until you bring them on. That lets a colleague join early and sort out their audio before they speak.

### Chat and Q&A moderation {#with-moderation}

Moderators work with the settings on the **Engagement** tab. With [Private Q&A](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/engagement/questions-answers/#public-or-private), questions are visible only to hosts and moderators. With chat set to [Hosts only](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/engagement/live-chat/#who-can-see-messages), each attendee talks privately to your team. [AI Moderation (Beta)](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/engagement/live-chat/#with-ai-moderation) doesn't replace a human moderator on a live session; someone still spots the question worth answering on air.

### Your plan {#with-plan}

Seats come with your plan. **Change plan**, on the **Plan** tab under **Settings → Manage Plan**, moves you up or down, so [compare plans and seats](https://easywebinar.com/pricing/) before you add people in bulk.

### Client workspaces {#with-client-workspaces}

If you run webinars for clients, a client workspace gives each one a separate EasyWebinar account that you manage, on its own plan. It is the setup for [marketing agencies running client webinars](https://easywebinar.com/solutions/marketing-agencies/). Unlike adding a team member, the client does get an email confirmation to set their password, so use an address they read.

## Who uses team members, and how {#who-uses-it}

These are typical patterns, showing how different teams set up their logins.

### Agencies {#agencies}

An [agency webinar team](https://easywebinar.com/solutions/marketing-agencies/) gives its staff **User** logins on the agency account. Each client gets a client workspace, so that client's webinars, registrants and settings stay apart from everyone else's.

### Marketing teams {#marketing-teams}

A [marketing team](https://easywebinar.com/solutions/marketing-teams/) gives **Admin** to the person who owns the plan and settings. Colleagues who build registration pages and [follow up on registrants](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/analytics/people/) work as **User**.

### Sales teams {#sales-teams}

A [sales team running demos](https://easywebinar.com/solutions/sales-teams/) gives a login to each sales engineer who presents every week. A partner presenting once joins with the Co-presenter link instead.

### Customer success teams {#customer-success}

A [customer success team](https://easywebinar.com/solutions/customer-success-teams/) adds a colleague who handles questions during onboarding sessions. On a one-off session, the **Moderator Join Link for Live room** does the same job without a login.

## Limits and things to know {#limits}

- **Seats depend on your plan.** Adding a member costs more only if you have used all the seats your plan includes. Seat and role rules are also listed under [team and account limits](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/reference/limits-and-defaults/#team-and-account).
- **There is no per-permission matrix.** Access is set by role alone; custom roles were not available at the time of checking.
- **No invitation email.** You set the password and pass the login on yourself.
- **The Owner can't be changed from the Team screen.** Contact support if ownership needs to move.
- **Client workspaces are separate** from team seats; adding a workspace doesn't use a seat.

## Common questions {#common-questions}

#### How do I invite someone to my account? {#faq-invite}

You don't invite; you create their account with **Add user**, including their password, then pass the details to them.

#### Does a new team member get an email? {#faq-email}

No. Nothing is emailed when you add a team member, so share the login yourself and ask them to change the password.

#### Can someone join a webinar as a presenter without an account? {#faq-presenter-no-account}

Yes. Use the Co-presenter or Moderator join link from the Share dialog.

#### What's the difference between Admin and User? {#faq-admin-user}

Admin can change everything, including settings and billing. User gets basic access to the features.

#### Can I set custom permissions for one person? {#faq-custom-permissions}

No. Access is set by role alone, and no per-permission matrix is shown. Pick **Admin** or **User** to match the job.

#### How many team members can I have? {#faq-how-many}

It depends on your plan. The Settings overview shows seats used out of your allowance.

#### Can I change someone's role later? {#faq-change-role}

Yes, from the **Actions** column on their row in the Team list.

#### Can I hand the Owner role to someone else? {#faq-ownership}

Not from the Team screen. Contact support if ownership of the account needs to move.

#### Where do co-hosts appear when they join the room? {#faq-co-hosts}

Backstage. Move them to the stage when you want them seen and heard, and back again when they're done.

#### Does a client workspace use one of my team seats? {#faq-workspace-seat}

No. Seats and workspaces are separate, and each workspace is billed on its own plan.

#### Does my client get an email when I create their workspace? {#faq-client-email}

Yes. The client gets an email confirmation to set their password and join your workspace, so use an address they read.

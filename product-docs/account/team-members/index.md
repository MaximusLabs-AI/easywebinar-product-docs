# Team members and roles

Give colleagues their own EasyWebinar login, choose Admin or User, and understand how team members differ from presenters and moderators.

- Applies to: All plans · seats depend on your plan · Owner or Admin
- Status: Live
- Verified: 2026-10-16
- Canonical page: https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/account/team-members/

Anyone who builds webinars, moderates sessions or reads your analytics regularly should have their own login. Sharing one login means you can't tell who did what, and only one of you can be in the live room as host.

**Expected result:** a new team member listed under **Settings → Team** with the right role, able to sign in with the details you give them.

## Before you begin {#before-you-begin}

- Check your seat allowance on the **Settings** overview, for example *2 of 10 seats used*. Seats are part of your plan.
- Each member needs their own email address.

## Open the Team screen {#team-screen}

Click **Settings** in the sidebar, then **Team**.

![The Team screen listing members and their roles](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/easywebinar-product-docs/images/team/step-01-team-list.png)

| | Area | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Add user** | Creates a new teammate |
| 2 | **Column headings** | Member, Role, Joined, Actions |
| 3 | **Role** | Owner, Admin or User |

## Add a team member {#add-a-team-member}

1. Click **Add user**.
2. Enter their **Name** and **Email**, and optionally a **Photo**.
3. Set a **Password** and **Confirm password**.
4. Choose a **Role**: **Admin** or **User**.
5. Click **Add user**.

![The Add user dialog with name, email, password and role](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/easywebinar-product-docs/images/team/step-02-add-user.png)

> **No invitation email is sent**
> You set their password and nothing is emailed to them. There is no invitation to accept, so pass the login details on yourself and ask them to change the password once they are in.


## Roles {#roles}

| Role | Access |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | The account holder. One per account |
| **Admin** | "Full access to everything in the organization including all settings." |
| **User** | "Basic access to organization features." |

### Which role to give {#which-role}

Give **Admin** to people who need to change billing, team or account settings. **User** suits presenters, moderators and anyone who works on webinars without touching the account.

### Changing roles and ownership {#change-role}

Change someone's role from the **Actions** column on their row. The **Owner** can't be changed from this screen; contact support if ownership needs to move.

## Team members, presenters and moderators {#presenters-and-moderators}

A **team member** has a login. A **presenter** or **moderator** runs a specific webinar and is added on that webinar's **Overview** tab. Presenters and moderators can join with a link, without an account.

- Add someone to your team when they work in EasyWebinar regularly.
- Send a **Co-presenter** or **Moderator** join link from the **Share** dialog when they are a guest on one session.

## Verify the result {#verify-the-result}

The new member appears in the Team list with their role and joined date, and the seat count on the Settings overview goes up by one.

## Limits and variations {#limits-and-variations}

- **Seats depend on your plan.** Adding a member costs more only if you have used all the seats your plan includes.
- **There is no per-permission matrix.** Access is set by role alone; custom roles were not available at the time of checking.
- **Client workspaces are separate** from team seats; adding a workspace doesn't use a seat.

## Common questions {#common-questions}

#### How do I invite someone to my account? {#faq-invite}

You don't invite; you create their account with **Add user**, including their password, then pass the details to them.

#### Can someone join a webinar as a presenter without an account? {#faq-presenter-no-account}

Yes. Use the Co-presenter or Moderator join link from the Share dialog.

# Change your EasyCRM language

Change the EasyCRM interface language from the flag button in the header, find one of 11 languages by search or its own name, and keep EasyCRM and EasyWebinar in the same language.

- Applies to: EasyCRM · the language is shared with your EasyWebinar dashboard
- Status: Live
- Verified: 2026-10-29
- Canonical page: https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/account/easycrm-language/

The EasyCRM language is the setting that decides which of 11 languages the [EasyCRM](https://easywebinar.com/easycrm/) screens use. Click the round flag button in the EasyCRM header and pick a language. It applies at once, with no **Save** button. EasyCRM and EasyWebinar share the setting, so the [EasyWebinar dashboard](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/account/dashboard-language/) follows your choice, and a change made there carries over to EasyCRM.

**Expected result:** the EasyCRM menu, header, dashboard, charts and AI assistant show in the language you picked, and stay that way after a reload.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | The flag button in the EasyCRM header, labelled *Change language* |
| **How you change it** | Click a language in the list. There is no **Save** button |
| **Languages** | 11, listed under **AVAILABLE LANGUAGES · 11** |
| **When it applies** | At once, and it stays after a reload |
| **Shared with** | The [EasyWebinar dashboard language](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/account/dashboard-language/#at-a-glance): change it in either app and the other follows |
| **What changes** | The EasyCRM interface: dashboard, leads, clients, deals, tasks, broadcasts, campaigns, configurations, wallet and the AI assistant. Dates follow the language too |
| **What stays as it is** | What you and your team type, such as lead names, notes and pipeline stage names, and the content of your emails, SMS and WhatsApp messages |
| **Related setting** | [Webinar Language](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/setup/webinar-language/#at-a-glance), the default language attendees get, set on each webinar |

## Before you begin {#before-you-begin}

- **Open EasyCRM.** From EasyWebinar, open **EasyCRM** under **Other products** in the sidebar, or go to EasyCRM directly.
- **Know which setting you need.** The EasyCRM language changes the screens you and your team work in. To set the language attendees see in a webinar, use [Webinar Language](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/setup/webinar-language/) on that webinar instead.
- **Expect EasyWebinar to change too.** The language is shared, so switching EasyCRM to German also switches your EasyWebinar dashboard to German.

## Step 1: Open the language list {#open-language-list}

1. Find the round flag button in the EasyCRM header, between **Ask AI** and the notifications bell. Its flag shows the current language.
2. Click it. The language list opens under the button.

![The EasyCRM dashboard in English with the round flag button in the header marked](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/images/easycrm-language/step-01-flag-button.png)

*You will find the flag button in the EasyCRM header, next to Ask AI.*

## Step 2: Find your language {#find-your-language}

The list shows the current language at the top, a **Search languages…** box, and **AVAILABLE LANGUAGES · 11**. Each language appears with its English name and, underneath, its own name, such as *Español* or *日本語*, so you can find yours even if the screen is in a language you don't read.

![The EasyCRM language list open, with the flag button, the search box, the AVAILABLE LANGUAGES 11 label and the list of languages marked](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/images/easycrm-language/step-02-language-list.png)

| | Area | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Flag button** | Opens and closes the list |
| 2 | **Search languages…** | Filters the list as you type |
| 3 | **AVAILABLE LANGUAGES · 11** | The number of languages you can pick |
| 4 | **Language list** | Each language, with a tick on the current one |

To find a language quickly, type part of its name. Typing *Ja* leaves only **Japanese**.

![The EasyCRM language search with Ja typed and Japanese as the only result](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/images/easycrm-language/step-02b-search.png)

The 11 languages are English, Spanish, Japanese, French, German, Portuguese (Brazil), Chinese (Simplified), Korean, Hindi, Danish and Arabic.

## Step 3: Pick the language {#pick-the-language}

1. Click the language you want.
2. EasyCRM switches straight away. You don't need to click **Save** or reload.

The flag on the button changes to match, and the button's own label follows the language, so in Spanish it reads *Cambiar idioma*.

## Step 4: Check EasyCRM in the new language {#check-the-result}

Look at the sidebar, the header and the dashboard. In Spanish, for example, **Dashboard** becomes **Panel**, **Clients** becomes **Clientes**, **Deals** becomes **Negocios** and **Wallet** becomes **Billetera**. The chart months change too, from *May, Jun, Jul* to *may, jun, jul*.

![EasyCRM in Spanish, with the translated sidebar menu, the translated header buttons and the chart months marked](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/images/easycrm-language/step-03-easycrm-in-spanish.png)

*Menu, header, cards and chart months follow the language. Your own data does not change.*

The AI assistant follows the same language. Open **Ask AI** and its welcome text, suggested actions and input box appear in the language you picked.

![The EasyCRM AI assistant panel in Spanish, with its suggested actions and input box](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/images/easycrm-language/step-03b-ai-assistant-spanish.png)

## Verify the result {#verify-the-result}

- The EasyCRM sidebar, header and dashboard show in the language you picked.
- Reload the page. EasyCRM opens in the same language.
- Open the language list again. Your language has the tick, and every language name is now shown in that language, with its own name underneath.
- Open your EasyWebinar dashboard. It shows in the same language, because the setting is shared.

## What the EasyCRM language is {#what-it-is}

The EasyCRM language is the interface language of EasyCRM, EasyWebinar's built-in CRM. It is one setting with 11 choices, shared with your EasyWebinar dashboard, so the webinar side and the CRM side of your account read the same way. It translates the screens, labels, dates and AI assistant. It does not translate what you write: lead names, notes, pipeline stage names and the messages you send stay exactly as typed.

## Why the EasyCRM language matters {#why-it-matters}

### What it's for {#what-its-for}

It lets each person work with their webinar leads in a language they read easily. A coach who follows up in Spanish, or a team in Japan working its pipeline, sees EasyCRM in that language instead of translating labels in their head.

### How it helps {#how-it-helps}

- **Faster follow-up.** People act on leads quicker when the deal stages, tasks and buttons are in their language.
- **One setting, two apps.** Webinar work and CRM work stay in the same language without changing two settings.
- **Fewer mistakes.** Translated labels make it harder to send the wrong broadcast or move a deal to the wrong stage.

### What happens without it {#without-it}

Working a CRM in a second language slows every task: reading a lead record, scheduling a follow-up, checking what a broadcast will do. Teams that run webinars in one tool and a CRM in another also end up with two language settings to keep in step, and often two different sets of supported languages.

### Why buyers care {#why-buyers-care}

The webinar is only half the job. The leads it brings in have to be worked, and that happens in the CRM. When the people doing that work read the CRM easily, more leads get a timely follow-up. That matters most to teams outside English-speaking markets and to agencies running clients in several languages.

## The EasyWebinar approach to the EasyCRM language {#approach}

Take a typical case. A course creator in Brazil runs weekly webinars in Portuguese and follows up every registrant in EasyCRM. Her assistant, who reads Portuguese far more easily than English, handles the follow-up tasks.

A common setup pairs a webinar tool with a separate CRM. Each has its own language setting, and the two rarely offer the same languages, so one of them often stays in English. The assistant then works the pipeline in a language she reads slowly.

The webinars don't need to change. What needs to change is where the leads land and how the screens read.

In EasyWebinar, she picks **Portuguese (Brazil)** once, from the flag button in either app. EasyWebinar and EasyCRM both switch, the chart dates read in Portuguese, and the AI assistant answers in the same language. Lead names and the messages she writes stay as she typed them.

## How the EasyCRM language works with other features {#works-with}

### Your EasyWebinar dashboard language {#with-dashboard-language}

The two apps share one language. [Change your dashboard language](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/account/dashboard-language/) explains the EasyWebinar side: the same flag button, the same 11 languages, and the same route under **Settings → My Profile → General Settings → Language**.

### Webinar Language for attendees {#with-webinar-language}

**Webinar Language** is separate. It sets the default language attendees get in a live webinar and is set on each webinar's **Settings** tab. See [Set the webinar language for attendees](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/webinars/setup/webinar-language/).

### Emails, SMS and WhatsApp messages {#with-messages}

EasyCRM sends your broadcasts and workflow messages exactly as you write them. Changing the EasyCRM language changes the editor around your message, not the message. Sending allowances are listed under [EasyCRM sending allowance](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/reference/limits-and-defaults/#easycrm-allowance).

### The AI assistant {#with-ai-assistant}

The **Ask AI** assistant shows its welcome text, suggested actions and input box in the chosen language. It is marked **Beta** in the app.

### Leads, deals and pipelines {#with-your-data}

Everything your team types stays as typed, whatever the language: lead names, notes, pipeline stage names and custom labels. Leads imported from your webinars keep their names and details. Moving webinar leads into EasyCRM is covered in [Connect a CRM and map your webinar data](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/product-docs/integrations/map-webinar-data/).

## How the EasyCRM language compares {#compare}

EasyCRM comes in 11 languages and shares one language setting with EasyWebinar. Teams that pair a webinar tool with a separate CRM manage two settings, each with its own list of languages.

Webinar and CRM language, from each vendor's public documentation:

| Setup | Webinar dashboard language | CRM interface language | One setting for both |
|---|---|---|---|
| **EasyWebinar with EasyCRM** | Yes (11 languages) | Yes (11 languages) | Yes |
| **Demio with ActiveCampaign** | Partly ([English only](https://help.demio.com/en/articles/3524482-change-the-default-language)) | Yes ([14 languages](https://help.activecampaign.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000754010)) | No, two separate products |
| **Livestorm with HubSpot** | Partly ([4 languages](https://support.livestorm.co/article/89-available-languages)) | Yes ([set per user](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/account-management/change-your-language-and-region-settings)) | No, two separate products |

*Yes: documented and built in. Partly: documented with a limit, shown in brackets.*

### Why EasyWebinar is the stronger choice for the CRM language {#compare-stronger}

- **One language for webinars and leads.** EasyWebinar and EasyCRM share one setting, so a team switches both with one click. A webinar tool and a separate CRM keep two settings in step.
- **Languages your CRM may not offer.** EasyCRM includes Japanese, Korean, Hindi and Arabic. [ActiveCampaign's help centre lists 14 fully supported languages](https://help.activecampaign.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000754010), none of them Japanese, Korean, Hindi or Arabic, and says Danish and Simplified Chinese are no longer supported. Compare [EasyWebinar vs Demio](https://easywebinar.com/compare/easywebinar-vs-demio/).
- **A webinar dashboard beyond English.** [Demio's help centre says its host dashboard is available only in English](https://help.demio.com/en/articles/3524482-change-the-default-language), and [Livestorm's lists four dashboard languages](https://support.livestorm.co/article/89-available-languages). Compare [EasyWebinar vs Livestorm](https://easywebinar.com/compare/easywebinar-vs-livestorm/).

*Competitor details checked against each vendor's public documentation on 29 October 2026. Features and plans change, so confirm with the vendor before you decide.*

## Limits and things to know {#limits}

- **It translates the interface, not your content.** Lead names, notes, pipeline stage names and anything else your team types stay as typed.
- **Messages are sent as written.** The content of emails, SMS and WhatsApp messages is not translated.
- **Some terms stay as they are.** In Spanish, for example, **Leads** and **Marketing** keep their names. Brand and integration names, currency symbols and each language's own name don't change.
- **The setting is shared.** Changing the language in EasyCRM changes your EasyWebinar dashboard too, and the other way round.
- **It isn't Webinar Language.** The language attendees get is set on each webinar.
- **The AI assistant is in Beta.** It follows the chosen language.

## Common questions {#common-questions}

#### How do I change the language in EasyCRM? {#faq-how}

Click the round flag button in the EasyCRM header and pick a language from the list. It applies at once.

#### Which languages does EasyCRM support? {#faq-languages}

11: English, Spanish, Japanese, French, German, Portuguese (Brazil), Chinese (Simplified), Korean, Hindi, Danish and Arabic.

#### Do I need to click Save? {#faq-save}

No. EasyCRM switches as soon as you click a language, and it stays after a reload.

#### If I change the language in EasyCRM, does EasyWebinar change too? {#faq-shared}

Yes. The two apps share one language setting, so changing it in either app changes the other.

#### Are my leads, notes and pipeline stages translated? {#faq-data}

No. Everything your team types stays as typed. Only the interface is translated.

#### Are my emails, SMS and WhatsApp messages translated? {#faq-messages}

No. EasyCRM sends message content exactly as you write it.

#### Do dates change with the language? {#faq-dates}

Yes. Dates follow the chosen language. In Spanish, for example, the chart months read *may, jun, jul*.

#### Does the AI assistant use the same language? {#faq-ai}

Yes. The **Ask AI** assistant shows its text and suggested actions in the chosen language. It is marked Beta.

#### How do I find my language if I can't read the current one? {#faq-own-name}

Each language is shown with its own name underneath, such as *Español* or *日本語*. You can also type part of the name in **Search languages…**.

#### Is the EasyCRM language the same as Webinar Language? {#faq-webinar-language}

No. The EasyCRM language changes the screens you work in. Webinar Language sets the default language attendees get, on each webinar's **Settings** tab.

#### Why do some words stay in English? {#faq-english-words}

Some product terms, such as **Leads** and **Marketing** in Spanish, keep their names. Brand names, integration names and what you typed don't change.

Registration embeds

Put an EasyWebinar registration form on your own website with the Registration Widget embed code, and decide when to use the widget instead of the registration page link.

Markdown

The Registration Widget lets people register for your webinar without leaving your own website. On the webinar's Content tab you copy its Embed Code and paste it into your page wherever the form should appear: a landing page, a blog post, a sidebar. The same snippet is in the Share dialog, and View & Customize lets you change how the widget looks before you copy it.

What a registration embed is

Every webinar has a registration page hosted by EasyWebinar, and a Registration Page Link that sends people to it. The Registration Widget is the other route: instead of sending visitors to EasyWebinar, you bring the form to them, on a page you already control.

Registration WidgetRegistration Page Link
Where people registerOn your own site, in the form you've embeddedOn EasyWebinar's registration page
What you shareNothing: people find the form where you placed itA link
SuitsLanding pages, blog posts, sidebarsEmail, social posts, and pages where EasyWebinar's page does the selling
Where you get itContent tab or Share dialog, as Embed CodeShare dialog, as Registration Page Link

Both register people for the same webinar.

Why registration embeds matter

What it's for

The widget is for keeping people on your site. If a visitor is already reading your page, the shortest path to a registration is a form right there, not a link to somewhere new.

How it helps

  • One less step. Visitors register where they are instead of clicking through to another page.
  • Your page stays in charge. The content around the form is yours: your blog post, your landing page, your sidebar.
  • It's the same snippet everywhere. Copy it once from the Content tab or the Share dialog and paste it into any page.
  • It travels with clones. When you clone a webinar, you can bring its embed widgets along.

What happens without it

Without an embed, every visitor on your own site has to follow a link to the EasyWebinar page to register. Each extra click and page load is a chance for someone to get distracted and leave, and the context that persuaded them (the article they were reading, the page they landed on) is left behind.

Why buyers care

Teams that already run websites and blogs want webinar registration to sit inside the content that brings in traffic. Buyers check whether the platform lets them embed a form, whether they can adjust its look, and whether it's a simple copy-and-paste.

Get the embed code

From the Content tab

  1. Open your webinar and click the Content tab.
  2. Scroll to Registration Widget, below the five webinar pages.
  3. If you want to change how the form looks, click View & Customize first.
  4. Copy the Embed Code.

From the Share dialog

  1. On the webinar's Overview, click Share.
  2. Choose Registration Widget in the dialog.
  3. Copy the snippet.

1 Registration Widget · 2 Share on social media · 3 One-Click Registration Link.

It's the same snippet in both places, so use whichever screen you're on.

Add it to your site

  1. Open the page on your site where people should register.
  2. Paste the embed code wherever the form should appear.
  3. Publish the page on your site.
  4. Register once yourself through the embedded form.
  5. Check the webinar's Overview: the Registrants stat counts people who completed the registration form, so your test registration should show there.

Widget or link: which to use

SituationUse
Someone is already reading your landing page or blog postThe widget
You want a form in your site's sidebarThe widget
You're sending an email to your listThe Registration Page Link
You're posting on social mediaThe Registration Page Link, or Share on social media in the Share dialog
You want EasyWebinar's page, with its design and countdown, to do the sellingThe Registration Page Link
You're emailing contacts you already have details forThe One-Click Registration Link

You don't have to choose one. You can use the widget on your own site and the link everywhere else.

How registration embeds work with other features

The registration page

The registration page is still where your webinar's full pitch lives: the design, the countdown, the copy. Build and publish it in the page builder even if most registrations come through the widget, because the link and the page are what you'll use in email and on social.

Share links

The Share dialog groups every way to share a webinar: Share Links, Registration Widget and Share on social media.

Confirmation emails

Registration Confirmation is sent immediately after someone registers, and it carries their join link.

The People view

People in the sidebar lists every registrant across your webinars. After your test registration, find yourself there to confirm the details came through.

Cloning

The Clone Webinar dialog lists Embed widgets under Webinar assets, alongside registration pages, the webinar schedule and replay settings. Leave it ticked to copy the widget into the new webinar.

Who uses registration embeds, and how

These are typical patterns, shown to illustrate how the widget fits different kinds of sites.

B2B marketing teams

A marketing team writes a blog post on the webinar's topic and embeds the registration form at the end, so readers who reach the bottom can sign up on the spot. The Registration Page Link goes into the newsletter and paid social.

SaaS and product teams

A product team runs a monthly product webinar and keeps the widget in the sidebar of its help centre or product blog, so customers looking for help can register for the next session without leaving.

Coaches and consultants

A coach's website has a "Free masterclass" page. The registration widget sits on that page, under the coach's own story, so the page they already designed does the persuading.

Agencies

An agency builds a client's landing page in the client's site builder and pastes the widget in, so the page stays on the client's domain and in the client's design.

Limits and things to know

  • The widget is a form, not the whole page. When you want EasyWebinar's page to do the selling, send the registration page link instead.
  • Customise first. Use View & Customize to change how the widget looks before you copy the code.
  • Copy the code from the right webinar. The Registration Widget sits on each webinar's own Content tab.
  • Test it after pasting. Register once and check Registrants on the Overview before you send traffic to the page.

Common questions

Can I put the registration form on my own website?

Yes. Copy the Embed Code from Registration Widget on the Content tab and paste it into your page.

Where else can I find the embed code?

In the Share dialog, under Registration Widget. It's the same snippet.

Can I change how the widget looks?

Yes. Click View & Customize before you copy the code.

Where should I paste it?

Wherever the form should appear on your page: a landing page, a blog post or a sidebar.

Should I use the widget or the registration page link?

Use the widget when you want people to register without leaving your site. Use the link when you want EasyWebinar's page to do the selling, or when you're sharing in email or on social.

How do I check the widget is working?

Register once through it, then look at Registrants on the webinar's Overview and find yourself in People.

Does cloning a webinar copy its widget?

It can. Embed widgets is one of the assets listed under Webinar assets in the clone dialog.

Do I still need a registration page if I use the widget?

Yes, for anywhere you share a link, such as email and social posts. The Registration Page Link only works once the page is published.

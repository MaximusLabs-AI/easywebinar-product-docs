# Replay pages

Turn on a webinar's replay page, decide how long it stays available, send viewers somewhere when it ends, choose between Webinar Replica and Custom Build, and tell a replay apart from instant replay.

- Applies to: Every webinar, from its Replay tab · instant replay is a separate evergreen setting
- Status: Live
- Verified: 2026-10-16
- Canonical page: https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/product-docs/webinars/media/replay-pages/

A replay page is where people watch the recording of a webinar session after it has happened. In EasyWebinar every webinar has a **Replay** tab that turns the page on, gives you its link, sets how long the replay stays available, decides where viewers go when the recording finishes, and chooses whether the page copies the live webinar or is designed separately. Most people who register don't make the live session, so the replay is often how the webinar reaches the rest of its audience.

## At a glance {#at-a-glance}

| | |
|---|---|
| **What it is** | A page for watching the recording of a session that has already happened |
| **Where you set it** | Webinar → **Replay** |
| **Turn it on** | **Enable replay page** |
| **Who can watch** | Anyone with the link, while the page is enabled |
| **Availability** | **Always Available**, **Expire after days** or **Specific date** |
| **When it ends** | **After the replay ends** under **Redirects** sends viewers to a URL you choose |
| **Experience** | **Webinar Replica** (same video, offers, polls and chat replay) or **Custom Build** |
| **Where to find the link** | **Replay Page Link** on the Replay tab, and **Attendee Replay link** in the Share dialog |
| **Styling** | The **Replay Page** is one of the five pages on the **Content** tab |
| **Not the same as** | Instant replay (**On Demand**), an evergreen schedule setting |

## What a replay page is {#what-it-is}

The **Replay** tab holds everything about the page people use to catch up on a past session. It has five parts:

| | Section | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Replay Settings** | Turns the replay page on or off |
| 2 | **Replay Page Link** | The URL you send people, with Copy and Open |
| 3 | **Availability** | How long the replay stays accessible |
| 4 | **Redirects** | Where viewers go when the recording finishes |
| 5 | **Experience** | What the replay page looks like |

The replay page is also the fifth of the five webinar pages on the **Content** tab, after the registration page, thank-you page, waiting area and webinar page. The Replay tab decides how the page behaves; the Content tab is where you can [style it in the page builder](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/product-docs/webinars/registration/registration-page-builder/#open-the-builder).

## Why replay pages matter {#why-it-matters}

### What it's for {#what-its-for}

A replay gives everyone who registered a second chance at the session. It serves the people who couldn't make the live time, the people who left early, and the people who want to watch again. For a webinar that ends in an offer, it also [extends the window to act on the offer](https://easywebinar.com/blog/using-webinar-replays-to-increase-sales/).

### How it helps {#how-it-helps}

- **It reaches the larger audience.** **Missed**, people who registered and never showed up, is often the biggest group a webinar has, and the replay link is what they want.
- **It can keep selling.** With **Webinar Replica**, the replay carries the same offers and polls as the live session, so it sells the way the session did.
- **It can carry a deadline.** **Expire after days** or **Specific date** give the replay a closing point, which creates urgency.
- **It hands viewers a next step.** **After the replay ends** sends them to an offer, a booking page, or whatever comes next.
- **It shows up in your numbers.** Replay viewing is counted separately in Analytics, so you can see what the replay added.

### What happens without it {#without-it}

Without a replay page, the session is over for everyone who wasn't there at the time. No-shows receive a follow-up with nothing to watch, a viewer who reaches the end of the recording has nowhere to go, and an offer made live has no second audience. EasyWebinar's guidance is to [leave live recording on](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/product-docs/webinars/live-room/run-a-live-webinar/#check-recording) for this reason: the replay is usually watched by more people than the live session.

### Why buyers care {#why-buyers-care}

For anyone [running webinars to generate leads](https://easywebinar.com/use-cases/generate-leads/) or sales, the replay decides how much value a single session produces. Buyers check whether replays can expire, whether offers carry over, whether the replay can redirect to a sales page, and whether replay viewing is reported separately from live attendance. EasyWebinar covers each of these on one tab per webinar.

## Set up a replay page {#set-it-up}

1. Open your webinar and click the **Replay** tab.
2. Switch on **Enable replay page**. The page then reads *Replay page is live, anyone with the link can watch*.
3. Under **Availability**, choose how long the replay stays open.
4. Under **Redirects**, set **After the replay ends** if you want viewers sent somewhere when the recording finishes.
5. Under **Experience**, choose **Webinar Replica** or **Custom Build**.
6. Copy the **Replay Page Link** with **Copy**, or check the page with **Open**.

![The Replay tab with Replay Settings, the Replay Page Link, Availability options, the After the replay ends redirect and the Experience section](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/images/replay/step-01-replay-settings.png)

*1 Replay Settings · 2 Replay Page Link · 3 Availability · 4 Redirects · 5 Experience. The replay link is blurred because it contains the account's own subdomain.*

> **The replay link is public**
> While **Enable replay page** is on, anyone with the link can watch. Treat the **Replay Page Link** as something that can be shared beyond the people you send it to.


### Choose how long it stays available {#availability}

| Option | What happens | When it suits |
|---|---|---|
| **Always Available** | No expiry. The replay stays up indefinitely | Training, onboarding and evergreen content that keeps its value |
| **Expire after days** | The replay closes a set number of days after the session | A rolling deadline, for example a few days after each session |
| **Specific date** | The replay closes at a date and time you choose | A fixed deadline, such as the end of a launch |

[A deadline creates urgency](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/product-docs/webinars/engagement/countdown-timers/#with-replays), which is why most offers sit behind one. If your offers or follow-up emails mention when the replay closes, make sure they match the option you chose here.

### Send viewers somewhere when it ends {#redirects}

**After the replay ends**, under **Redirects**, sends viewers on as soon as the recording finishes playing. Point it at your offer, a booking page, or the next step you want people to take. Without it, viewers aren't sent anywhere when the recording ends.

### Choose what the replay page looks like {#experience}

| Option | What viewers get | When to choose it |
|---|---|---|
| **Webinar Replica** | A duplicate of your live webinar: the same video, offers, polls and chat replay | The quicker choice; keeps offers and polls working exactly as they did live |
| **Custom Build** | A page you design yourself, with its own layout, CTAs and branding | When the replay needs a different pitch from the live session |

### Style the page in the builder {#style-replay-page}

1. On the **Content** tab, click **Edit** on the **Replay Page** card.
2. Select blocks to change them, use the device toggle to check the mobile layout, and click **Preview** to see the real page.
3. Click **Save** to keep a draft and **Publish** to make it public. Each page has its own **Publish** button.

If you only have time to style two of the five pages, EasyWebinar's guidance is to make them the registration page and the replay page: one carries your signups, the other carries the long tail of views.

## Replay or instant replay? {#replay-vs-instant-replay}

The two are easy to confuse, and they answer different needs.

| | Replay | Instant replay (**On Demand**) |
|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | The recording of a session that has already happened | An evergreen setting that lets someone watch immediately instead of waiting for a session |
| **Where you set it** | The **Replay** tab | The evergreen webinar's schedule |
| **Which webinars** | Every webinar has a Replay tab | Evergreen webinars |
| **Who it's for** | People who missed a session and want to catch up | People who want to watch the moment they register |
| **Button wording** | Not applicable | **Instant replay text**, *Watch instant replay* by default |

If you want people to watch the moment they register, you want **On Demand** on an evergreen webinar. If you want people who missed a session to catch up, you want the replay page. See [On Demand](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/product-docs/webinars/on-demand/).

## How it works with other features {#works-with}

### Offers and polls {#with-offers}

Offers and polls carry over to the replay when the replay uses **Webinar Replica**. If an offer's headline or countdown talks about a deadline, check it still makes sense to someone watching later, and use **Preview** on the replay page to see what viewers get. See [Offers](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/product-docs/webinars/engagement/timed-offers/) and [Polls](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/product-docs/webinars/engagement/polls/).

### Recording {#with-recording}

On a live webinar, the room header shows **Auto Recording Enabled** when recording is on, and recorded sessions land in **Library → Recordings**. Recording can be changed in the room under **Settings → Recording Settings**. See [Run a live webinar](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/product-docs/webinars/live-room/run-a-live-webinar/) and [The Library](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/product-docs/webinars/media/library/).

### Follow-up emails {#with-follow-ups}

Replay emails are set up on the **Notifications** tab, not on the Replay tab. Add a [**Webinar Follow-up**](https://easywebinar.com/features/webinar-follow-up/) after the session, set its audience (everyone who registered, only attendees, or only no-shows) and include the replay link. No-shows are usually the biggest group, and the replay link is what they want. See [Reminder emails](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/product-docs/webinars/notifications/reminder-emails/) for the rest of the email sequence.

### Sharing {#with-sharing}

The replay link also appears in the webinar's **Share** dialog as **Attendee Replay link**, alongside the registration and attendee join links meant for your audience. See [Sharing links](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/product-docs/webinars/registration/sharing-links/).

### Paid webinars {#with-paid-webinars}

A paid webinar can sell a **Live + Replay bundle**, a second option that includes the replay recording with its own price. If you sell replay access, set the replay's **Availability** to match what the bundle promises. See [Paid webinars](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/product-docs/webinars/selling/paid-webinars/).

### Analytics and People {#with-analytics}

| Where | What it shows about the replay |
|---|---|
| **Analytics** → **Replay Watched** | People who watched the recording |
| **Analytics** → **Total Attended** | Unique people, live or replay, split into **Live only**, **Replay only** and **Both** |
| **Overview** → **Total Attended** | Live attendees plus replay viewers |
| **People** → **Replay watch percentage** | How much of the replay each person watched |

Replay views are counted separately from live attendance, and someone who did both shows under **Both**. See [Webinar analytics](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/product-docs/webinars/analytics/webinar-analytics/) and [People](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/product-docs/webinars/analytics/people/).

### CRM and email tools {#with-crm}

A connected CRM can act on replay behaviour with the **Watched replay** and **Didn't watch replay** events, and receive each contact's **Webinar Replay Link** or **Webinar Replay Short Link** in a custom field. See [Connect a CRM and map your webinar data](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/product-docs/integrations/map-webinar-data/).

### Tracking code {#with-tracking}

On the webinar's **Settings** tab, the [**Tracking Code** sub-tab](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/product-docs/webinars/registration/registration-page-builder/#with-tracking) lets you add scripts to each of the five pages, including the **Replay Page**, so your analytics or ad platform can count people who watched afterwards.

### Cloning {#with-cloning}

**Replay settings** are one of the webinar assets you can bring across when cloning a webinar. See [Clone a webinar](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/product-docs/webinars/getting-started/clone-a-webinar/).

## Who uses it, and how {#who-uses-it}

These are typical patterns, shown to illustrate how the replay page fits different kinds of webinar.

### Course creators {#course-creators}

After a [live launch webinar](https://easywebinar.com/use-cases/launch-courses-programs/), a creator sets the replay to **Expire after days**, keeps **Webinar Replica** so the enrolment offer stays on the page, and points **After the replay ends** at the course checkout. A follow-up to no-shows carries the replay link. See [selling online courses with webinars](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/easywebinar-product-docs/preview-v3/product-docs/use-cases/sell-courses/).

### B2B marketing teams {#b2b-marketing}

A marketing team runs a live panel, then uses **Custom Build** to give the replay its own layout and a call to action aimed at people who weren't in the room. **Replay Watched** in Analytics shows how much of the audience came through the replay.

### Customer success teams {#customer-success}

A customer success team [records an onboarding session](https://easywebinar.com/use-cases/onboard-train-customers/) and leaves the replay on **Always Available**, so new customers can watch it whenever they join. The **Replay Page Link** goes into the team's welcome material.

### Coaches and consultants {#coaches}

A coach runs a free masterclass and closes the replay on a **Specific date** that matches the deadline on their offer. The **Watched replay** event in their CRM tags people who caught up, so the follow-up matches what each person saw.

## Limits and things to know {#limits}

- **The link is public while the page is enabled.** Anyone with the **Replay Page Link** can watch.
- **Instant replay is set elsewhere.** **On Demand** belongs to an evergreen webinar's schedule, not to the Replay tab.
- **Viewers go nowhere at the end** unless you set **After the replay ends**.
- **Offers and polls carry over only with Webinar Replica.** A **Custom Build** page has its own layout and CTAs.
- **Replay emails live on the Notifications tab**, as follow-ups that include the replay link.
- **Changes to the page design need publishing.** Each of the five pages has its own **Publish** button.

## Common questions {#common-questions}

#### How do I give people the replay? {#faq-give-replay}

Switch on **Enable replay page** and send the **Replay Page Link**. It's also in the **Share** dialog as **Attendee Replay link**.

#### Can I make the replay expire? {#faq-expire}

Yes. Choose **Expire after days** for a rolling deadline, or **Specific date** for a fixed one.

#### Can the replay stay up permanently? {#faq-always-available}

Yes. **Always Available** has no expiry, which suits training, onboarding and evergreen content.

#### Will my offers and polls show on the replay? {#faq-offers}

Yes, with **Webinar Replica**: the replay shows the same video, offers, polls and chat replay.

#### Where do viewers go when the recording ends? {#faq-redirect}

Nowhere, unless you set **After the replay ends** under **Redirects**.

#### Can I design the replay page differently from the webinar? {#faq-custom-build}

Yes. Choose **Custom Build** to design a page with its own layout, CTAs and branding.

#### What's the difference between a replay and instant replay? {#faq-instant-replay}

A replay is the recording of a past session. Instant replay, or **On Demand**, is an evergreen option that lets someone watch straight away without waiting for a session.

#### Is the replay link private? {#faq-private}

No. While the replay page is enabled, anyone with the link can watch.

#### How do I email the replay to people who missed the session? {#faq-replay-email}

Add a **Webinar Follow-up** on the **Notifications** tab, set its audience to no-shows, and include the replay link.

#### How do I see how many people watched the replay? {#faq-replay-views}

**Replay Watched** in the webinar's Analytics, and **Replay watch percentage** per person in **People**.

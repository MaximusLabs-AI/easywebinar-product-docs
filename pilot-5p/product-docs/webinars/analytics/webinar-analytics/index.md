# Webinar analytics

What the Analytics tab on an EasyWebinar webinar measures, from visitors and registrations to live attendance, replay views, offer performance and revenue, and how to read each number.

- Applies to: Live, simulive and evergreen webinars
- Status: Live
- Verified: 2026-10-16
- Canonical page: https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/analytics/webinar-analytics/

Every EasyWebinar webinar has an **Analytics** tab that shows how many people reached each stage of that webinar and where the rest dropped away. It has three views: **Overview** for the funnel, engagement and offer numbers, **People** for the individuals behind those numbers, and **Revenue** for what the webinar earned. A date range at the top limits everything on the page to the period you choose.

## At a glance {#at-a-glance}

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where to find it** | Open a webinar → **Analytics** tab |
| **Views** | **Overview**, **People** and **Revenue** |
| **Time period** | A date range with **Apply**, covering everything on the page |
| **Funnel metrics** | **Visitors**, **Registered**, **Live Attended**, **Replay Watched**, **Total Attended**, **Missed** |
| **Offer metrics** | **Offers Performance**, **Views to Clicks**, **Clicks to Purchases**, **Revenue by Offer** |
| **Engagement metrics** | **Chat** message totals and **Questions & Answers** |
| **Audience location** | **Registrations by country** |
| **Revenue** | **Total Revenue**, **Paid Registrations**, **Revenue by Source**, and every transaction |
| **Webinar types** | Live, simulive and evergreen |

## What webinar analytics is {#what-it-is}

The Analytics tab is the per-webinar report. It answers two questions: how many people got through each stage, from landing on the registration page to watching, and where the others stopped.

The **Overview** view is built around a funnel. People arrive at the registration page, some register, some of those attend live or watch the replay, and some never show up. Below the funnel sit the numbers for what happened during the session: chat, questions, and how your offers performed.

The **People** view lists who registered and who attended. The **Revenue** view breaks down what the webinar earned, separating paid registrations from offers sold in the room.

For a quick read without opening the tab, the webinar's **Overview** tab shows four live stats (**Registrants**, **Live Attended**, **Total Attended** and **Revenue**) that update as the webinar runs. See [The Overview tab](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/setup/overview-tab/).

## Why webinar analytics matters {#why-it-matters}

{/* 5P */}

When a webinar disappoints, the useful question is which part to fix. Each stage depends on a different part of your setup: the [registration page](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/registration/registration-page-builder/#with-analytics), the [reminder emails](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/notifications/reminder-emails/#with-analytics), the presentation, the offer or the checkout. To choose, you need to see where people stopped, not only how many turned up.

Many teams piece that picture together by hand. They export a CSV of sign-ups, another of attendees and a payment report from a separate checkout, then match them in a spreadsheet. It is slow, and it still gives totals, not [per-stage numbers](https://easywebinar.com/blog/webinar-analytics/), so a team may rewrite the talk when the real problem was attendance. When [comparing webinar platforms](https://easywebinar.com/compare/webinar-platforms/), check whether reporting follows each webinar from page visit to purchase.

A platform can only count the stages it runs. Since March 2026, payments run through EasyWebinar too, because paid registration and paid offers both launched that month. Registration payments and offer sales now land in the same system as the visit, the sign-up and the attendance. The whole funnel, money included, can be read in one place.

EasyWebinar's **Analytics** tab lays out that funnel for each webinar, from **Visitors** and **Registered** to **Total Attended** and **Missed**. **Missed** is your list of [who to follow up](https://easywebinar.com/features/webinar-follow-up/). Below the funnel, EasyWebinar splits offer results into **Views to Clicks** and **Clicks to Purchases**. The **Revenue** view then puts [webinar revenue](https://easywebinar.com/sell-and-monetize/) next to who came.

The definitions are exact enough to check. **Visitors** counts unique IPs on your landing page. **Total Attended** counts unique people, split into **Live only**, **Replay only** and **Both**, so someone who watched live and on replay counts once. One date range with **Apply** limits every number on the tab.

## Read your analytics {#read-your-analytics}

### 1. Open the tab and set the period {#open-and-set-period}

Open your webinar and click **Analytics**. It opens on **Overview**.

![The Analytics Overview with the view switcher, the date range and Apply, and the funnel metrics marked](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/images/analytics/step-01-overview.png)

*1 Overview, People and Revenue · 2 Date range and Apply · 3 Funnel metrics.*

Set the date range and click **Apply**. The range applies to every number on the page, so if a webinar looks emptier than you expected, widen it first.

### 2. Read the funnel {#read-the-funnel}

| Metric | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Visitors** | Unique IPs on your landing page |
| **Registered** | Unique sign-ups |
| **Live Attended** | People who joined the live broadcast |
| **Replay Watched** | People who watched the recording |
| **Total Attended** | Unique people, live or replay, split into **Live only**, **Replay only** and **Both** |
| **Missed** | Registered and never showed up |

**Live Attended** covers the broadcast only. **Total Attended** counts each person once whether they watched live, on replay or both, which is why it's the number to use when you ask how many people the webinar reached.

### 3. Find where people drop off {#find-the-gaps}

Two gaps tell you most of what you need:

| Gap | What it measures | If it's low, look at |
|---|---|---|
| **Visitors** to **Registered** | Your registration page | The headline, the form and the promise on the page |
| **Registered** to attended | Your reminders | [Confirmation emails](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/notifications/confirmation-emails/) and reminders, not the content |

**Missed** is the group most worth contacting after a session. Send them the [replay](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/media/replay-pages/#with-follow-ups).

### 4. Check engagement and offers {#engagement-and-offers}

Further down the **Overview**:

| Panel | What it shows |
|---|---|
| [**Offers Performance**](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/engagement/timed-offers/#measure-offers) | Each offer's views, clicks and purchases, with a click rate |
| **Views to Clicks** and **Clicks to Purchases** | Where your offer loses people |
| **Revenue by Offer** | Total revenue and payment count, with a details link |
| **Chat** | Total messages, which you can filter by session |
| **Questions & Answers** | How many questions were asked and how many you answered |
| **Registrations by country** | Where your audience is |

Read **Views to Clicks** and **Clicks to Purchases** together. A low views-to-clicks rate means the offer itself isn't landing. A low clicks-to-purchases rate means people want the offer but your [checkout](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/selling/paid-webinars/#checkout) or price is stopping them.

After a busy session, the **Not Answered Rate** on [Q&A](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/engagement/questions-answers/#with-analytics) tells you how many questions you left behind. Those are worth answering in your follow-up email.

### 5. See the people behind the numbers {#people-view}

Switch to **People** to see who registered and who attended. For a full list across all your webinars, with columns such as watch percentage, offer clicked and lead score, plus filters and export, use [People](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/analytics/people/) in the sidebar.

### 6. Check revenue {#revenue-view}

Switch to **Revenue**.

![The Revenue view with the Revenue switch, Total Revenue and Paid Registrations, Revenue by Source and the Paid Webinar switch marked](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/images/analytics/step-06-revenue.png)

| | Area | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Revenue** | The revenue view for this webinar |
| 2 | **Total Revenue** and **Paid Registrations** | Everything earned, and the part that came from registrations |
| 3 | **Revenue by Source** | The split between paid registrations and offers sold in the room |
| 4 | **Paid Webinar** | Switches the section below to registration payments: completed, pending and abandoned checkouts, and every transaction |

Offer sales appear under **Paid Offer** in the same view, so registration revenue and in-room sales are kept apart. The transaction list shows each buyer's name, email, session, registration date, amount and status, and you can search it and filter by status.

Transactions show one of these statuses: **Pending**, **Processing**, **Success**, **Failed**, **Canceled**, **Refunded** or **Partially Refunded**. **Checkout abandoned** counts people who reached checkout but didn't pay; they aren't registered. A webinar with no payments shows "No transactions yet."

## What to do with each number {#what-to-do}

| What you see | What it usually means | Where to act |
|---|---|---|
| Few **Visitors** | Not enough promotion reaching the page | Your campaigns and the **Registration Page Link** |
| Low **Visitors** to **Registered** | The page isn't persuading | [Registration page builder](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/registration/registration-page-builder/) |
| Low **Registered** to attended | People forget or lose the link | [Reminder emails](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/notifications/reminder-emails/) |
| High **Missed** | A large audience still to reach | A follow-up to no-shows with the replay link |
| Low **Views to Clicks** | The offer isn't landing | The [offer's headline, button and timing](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/engagement/timed-offers/#write-the-card) |
| Low **Clicks to Purchases** | Interest without purchase | Your checkout page or price |
| High **Not Answered Rate** | Questions left behind | Answer them in your follow-up |

## How analytics works with other features {#works-with}

### Follow-up emails {#with-follow-ups}

**Webinar Follow-ups** on the **Notifications** tab can go to everyone who registered, only attendees, or [only no-shows](https://easywebinar.com/blog/email-segmentation-for-webinar-follow-ups/), which matches the **Missed** number directly. **Attendee Action Follow-ups** send based on engagement and whether someone clicked an offer.

### CRM and email tools {#with-crm}

A connected CRM can tag people by the same behaviour Analytics counts: **Registered**, **Attended**, **Missed**, **Watched replay**, **Watched full** and **Clicked offer**, among others. That lets your sales or email system act on what the numbers show. See [Connect a CRM and map your webinar data](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/integrations/map-webinar-data/).

### Offers and countdowns {#with-offers}

**Offers Performance** reports on the offers you build on the **CTAs** tab. Comparing sessions is the clearest way to see whether a change, such as adding a [countdown timer](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/engagement/countdown-timers/), helped.

### Paid registration {#with-paid-registration}

For paid webinars, the **Revenue** view is where every registration payment and its status appear. See [Paid webinars](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/selling/paid-webinars/).

### Replays {#with-replays}

**Replay Watched** and the **Replay only** and **Both** splits only fill in if people can reach your recording. See [Replay pages](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/media/replay-pages/).

## Who uses webinar analytics, and how {#who-uses-it}

These are typical patterns, shown to illustrate how different teams read the same tab.

### B2B marketing teams {#b2b-marketing}

A [demand generation](https://easywebinar.com/use-cases/generate-leads/) team runs a monthly webinar and tracks **Visitors** to **Registered** after every promotion change. When the rate drops, they test a new registration page headline before changing anything else.

### Course creators {#course-creators}

A creator selling a course through an evergreen webinar watches **Offers Performance** and **Clicks to Purchases**. A healthy click rate with few purchases sends them to the checkout page, not the presentation. See [Course creators](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/use-cases/sell-courses/).

### Coaches and consultants {#coaches}

A coach running [paid workshops](https://easywebinar.com/paid-webinars/) checks the **Revenue** view after each session, compares **Paid Registrations** with offer sales under **Revenue by Source**, and keeps an eye on **Checkout abandoned**.

### SaaS and product teams {#product-teams}

A product team running [onboarding webinars](https://easywebinar.com/use-cases/onboard-train-customers/) cares most about **Total Attended** and **Replay only**, because many users catch up on the recording. A high **Not Answered Rate** tells them to go back through the questions and cover the gaps in their follow-up.

## Limits and things to know {#limits}

- **Analytics is per webinar.** The **People** page in the sidebar is the one that spans every webinar.
- **The date range covers the whole page.** Widen it if numbers look low.
- **A brand-new webinar shows zeros** until registrations start.
- **Visitors are unique IPs** on your landing page.
- **Replay views are counted separately** from live attendance; someone who did both appears under **Both**.
- **Registration and offer revenue are reported separately**, under **Paid Webinar** and **Paid Offer**.
- **An abandoned checkout isn't a registration.**

## Common questions {#common-questions}

#### What's the difference between Live Attended and Total Attended? {#faq-live-vs-total}

**Live Attended** is the live broadcast only. **Total Attended** counts unique people who watched live or on replay.

#### How do I see how many people didn't turn up? {#faq-no-shows}

Use **Missed**: people who registered and never attended.

#### Why is everything showing zero? {#faq-zeros}

Either the webinar hasn't run yet, or your date range doesn't cover the period when it did.

#### How do I know if my offer worked? {#faq-offer-worked}

Check **Offers Performance** for views, clicks and purchases, and **Revenue by Offer** for what it earned.

#### Where do I see who registered? {#faq-who-registered}

In the **People** view on the Analytics tab, or in [People](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/analytics/people/) in the sidebar for every webinar at once.

#### Can I see where my audience is? {#faq-location}

Yes. **Registrations by country** is at the bottom of the Overview.

#### How do I see which questions I didn't answer? {#faq-unanswered}

**Questions & Answers** shows how many were asked and answered, and the **Not Answered Rate** shows how many you left behind.

#### Are registration payments and offer sales reported together? {#faq-revenue-split}

No. **Revenue by Source** splits them, and the section below switches between **Paid Webinar** and **Paid Offer**.

#### What does Checkout abandoned mean? {#faq-checkout-abandoned}

People who reached the checkout for a paid webinar but didn't pay. They aren't registered.

#### Does replay viewing count as attendance? {#faq-replay-attendance}

It counts towards **Total Attended**. **Live Attended** counts the broadcast only.

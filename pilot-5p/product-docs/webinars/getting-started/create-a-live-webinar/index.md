# Create and schedule a live webinar

Create a live webinar, set a one-time or recurring schedule, choose free or paid registration and pick a registration template.

- Applies to: All plans · any team member
- Status: Live
- Verified: 2026-10-16
- Canonical page: https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/getting-started/create-a-live-webinar/

Create a live webinar, set the date, time and timezone your attendees register for, and get a registration page ready to publish. The builder has four steps and takes about two minutes.

**Expected result:** a webinar with its schedule applied, open in the registration page builder, ready for you to publish.

## Before you begin {#before-you-begin}

- Sign in to EasyWebinar. **[Any team member can create a webinar](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/account/team-members/#roles)**; you don't need to be the account owner.
- Decide the [date, time and timezone you will broadcast in](https://easywebinar.com/blog/best-day-and-time-to-host-a-webinar/).
- If you will charge for the webinar, [connect a **Stripe** account](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/selling/paid-webinars/#before-you-begin). You can complete everything without one by leaving pricing on **Free**.
- Decide the webinar type first. **The type can't be changed after the webinar is created.**

### Live, simulive or evergreen? {#choose-type}

| You want | Choose |
|---|---|
| To [broadcast live, in real time](https://easywebinar.com/live-webinars/) | **Live Webinar** |
| Pre-recorded content that plays at scheduled times, with attendees registering for specific dates | [**Simulive Webinar**](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/getting-started/create-a-simulive-webinar/) |
| Pre-recorded content available around the clock, watched on each viewer's schedule | [**Evergreen Webinar**](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/getting-started/create-an-evergreen-webinar/) |

The [three webinar types](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/#webinar-types) use the same pages, emails, polls, offers and analytics. Only the delivery differs.

If you would rather have EasyWebinar write the script, pages, emails, offers and polls for you, choose **[Create with AI](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/ai/create-with-ai/)** instead of **Create Manually** in step 1.

## Step 1: Open the create dialog {#open-create-dialog}

1. In the left sidebar, click **My Webinars**.
2. Click **Create a Webinar** at the top right.
3. Choose **Create Manually**.

![The Create a Webinar dialog showing Create with AI and Create Manually side by side](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/images/create-live/step-02-choose-creation-method.png)

*Two ways to create a webinar. Create with AI generates your pages, emails, offers and polls; Create Manually opens the four-step builder.*

## Step 2: Name your webinar and choose its type {#name-and-type}

The builder opens on **Step 1 of 4: Build**.

1. In **Webinar Name**, type the title attendees will see.
2. Under **Webinar Type**, choose a card. **Live Webinar** is selected by default.
3. Optionally add **Tags**: type one and press Enter, or click a suggested tag (**Business**, **Education**, **Webinar**, **Sales**, **Coaching**).
4. Click **Continue to Schedule**.

![Step 1 of 4 with the webinar name field, the three type cards and the tags field](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/images/create-live/step-01-build-name-and-type.png)

*1 Webinar Name · 2 Webinar Type, Live selected by default · 3 Tags · 4 Continue.*

> **Nothing happens when you click Continue?**
> The webinar name is empty. The builder won't move on until you enter one.


## Step 3: Set your schedule {#set-schedule}

On **Step 2 of 4: Schedule**:

1. Choose a **Schedule Frequency**: **Specific Date**, **Daily** or **Weekly**.
2. Check the **Timezone**. It defaults to your account timezone. Your live session runs at the time you set, in this timezone.
3. Under **Available Sessions**, set the date and time.
4. Optional: click **Add Session** to offer the same webinar at more than one date or time.
5. Under **Schedule Preferences**, choose how many upcoming sessions a registrant can pick from.
6. Click **Continue to Pricing**.

![Step 2 of 4 showing frequency, timezone, session date and time, Add Session and schedule preferences](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/images/create-live/step-02-schedule-specific-date.png)

*1 Frequency · 2 Timezone · 3 Session date and time · 4 Add Session · 5 Schedule Preferences · 6 Continue.*

### Timezone {#timezone}

A live webinar runs at one fixed time for everyone, in the timezone you set. Your registration page shows that time with its timezone. Choose the timezone you will broadcast in.

> **Local times**
> Only [just-in-time sessions](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/automated/just-in-time/#timezones) on evergreen webinars follow each attendee's own local time. Live and scheduled sessions always run in the webinar's timezone.


### Past dates {#past-dates}

You can't schedule a session in the past. Earlier dates are greyed out in the picker.

![The date picker with past dates greyed out](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/images/create-live/step-02b-date-picker-past-dates-disabled.png)

### Run the same webinar every week {#recurring-schedules}

Choosing **Weekly** turns the single session into a recurring schedule and adds four controls:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Days of week** | Which days the webinar runs, Monday to Sunday |
| **Start Date** and **End Date (Optional)** | When the recurrence begins and ends. Leave the end date as **No end date** to run it indefinitely |
| **Session Time** and **Add Time** | Run it more than once on the same day |
| **Blackout Dates** | Stop the webinar running on specific dates, such as holidays or time off |

![Step 2 with Weekly selected, showing days of week, start and end date, session times and blackout dates](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/images/create-live/step-02c-schedule-weekly-recurring.png)

#### Blackout dates {#blackout-dates}

A weekly webinar runs until you stop it. Use **Blackout Dates** to skip individual days without ending the series, or set an end date to stop it.

### Which sessions registrants see {#schedule-preferences}

By default registrants see **only the next available session**. Change **Schedule Preferences** to let them choose from more upcoming sessions.

## Step 4: Choose free or paid {#pricing}

On **Step 3 of 4: Pricing**, choose one:

- **Free** (selected by default): attendees register without paying.
- **Paid**: [attendees pay to register](https://easywebinar.com/paid-webinars/). Requires a connected Stripe account.

Click **Continue to Registration Template**.

![Step 3 of 4 with Free selected and Paid beside it](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/images/create-live/step-03-pricing-free.png)

### Charging for the webinar {#charging}

Selecting **Paid** shows:

- **Pricing Title** and an optional **Description**, shown to attendees at checkout
- **Live webinar price**, in US dollars
- **Live + Replay bundle**, to [sell access to the webinar and its recording together](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/selling/paid-webinars/#live-replay-bundle)

Payments run through **Stripe**. If no account is connected, a **Payments · Action required** panel offers **Connect with Stripe**. Payouts go directly to your own bank account; EasyWebinar never holds your funds. You can switch back to **Free** any time before creating the webinar.

![The paid pricing step with price fields and the Stripe connection panel](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/images/create-live/step-03b-pricing-paid-stripe.png)

Once people pay, the webinar's **Analytics** tab shows [what you've earned under **Revenue**](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/analytics/webinar-analytics/#revenue-view), with a list of every transaction and its status.

## Step 5: Choose a registration template {#registration-template}

On **Step 4 of 4: Registration Template**:

1. Filter by category: **All Templates**, **Agency**, **Coach**, **Education**, **Health & Wellness**, **Lead Magnet**, **Marketing**, or **My Templates** for ones you have saved.
2. Click a template. **Default Register Page** is the simplest one-column option; **Build Your Own** starts from a blank page.
3. Click **Create Webinar**.

![Step 4 of 4 with the template category filters and the template grid](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/images/create-live/step-04-registration-template.png)

*1 Category filters · 2 Template cards · 3 Create button.*

You can skip this step. The button reads **Skip & Create Webinar** until you select a template, and you can [choose one later](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/registration/registration-page-builder/#change-template).

## Verify the result {#verify-the-result}

The webinar is created and opens in the **[registration page builder](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/registration/registration-page-builder/#open-the-builder)**, with your schedule applied and a [live countdown to the session](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/engagement/countdown-timers/#set-up-registration-countdown).

![The registration page builder with the webinar date, countdown and registration form](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/images/create-live/step-05-registration-page-builder.png)

- The default form collects **Full name** and **Email**, both required. Use **Add Custom Field** to [collect more on the form](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/registration/custom-fields/#set-up-the-form).
- Use the desktop and mobile toggle to check both layouts.

> **Not public yet**
> Creating the webinar does not publish it. Your registration page goes live only when you click **Publish**. Use **Preview** to check it and **Save** to keep a draft.


## Limits and variations {#limits-and-variations}

- **The type is fixed.** [Clone the webinar](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/getting-started/clone-a-webinar/#choose-type) and choose another type on the copy.
- **A live webinar runs at one fixed time** in the timezone you set.
- **A weekly webinar runs until you stop it.**
- **Registrants see the next session only** unless you change the schedule preference.
- **Registrant numbers depend on your plan.** [Attendee and registration limits](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/reference/limits-and-defaults/#attendee-capacity) differ between [EasyWebinar's plans](https://easywebinar.com/pricing/); the features on this page are available on all plans.

## Common questions {#common-questions}

#### Can I run the same webinar at more than one time? {#faq-multiple-times}

Yes. Click **Add Session** to add dates and times, or switch the frequency to **Daily** or **Weekly**.

#### Can I change the webinar type after creating it? {#faq-change-type}

No. Clone the webinar and choose the type you want on the copy. This is also how you [turn a live webinar into an evergreen one](https://easywebinar.com/blog/turn-live-webinars-into-automated-funnels/).

#### Do I have to be the account owner? {#faq-owner}

No. Any team member can create and schedule webinars.

#### Is my registration page live as soon as I create the webinar? {#faq-live-immediately}

No. It becomes public only when you click **Publish**.

#### What's the difference between live, simulive and evergreen? {#faq-types}

Live broadcasts in real time. [Simulive](https://easywebinar.com/simulive-webinars/) plays pre-recorded content at scheduled times, with attendees registering for specific dates. [Evergreen](https://easywebinar.com/evergreen-webinars/) makes pre-recorded content available 24/7, with viewers watching on their own schedule. For help choosing, the guide to [live, automated and evergreen webinars](https://easywebinar.com/blog/live-vs-automated-webinars/) sets out when each one suits.

#### How do I stop a recurring webinar from running on a holiday? {#faq-holiday}

Use **Blackout Dates** on the schedule step to list the dates the webinar should skip.

# The green room and backstage

How EasyWebinar's green room lets you prepare a live webinar with nothing broadcast, and how backstage holds co-presenters and moderators until you bring them on stage.

- Applies to: Live webinars
- Status: Live
- Verified: 2026-10-16
- Canonical page: https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/live-room/green-room/

The green room is the private state of the EasyWebinar live room before a broadcast starts. You and your team can test cameras, microphones and screen sharing and arrange the stage, while the header reads **Not Live** and nothing reaches attendees. Backstage is the waiting area inside the room: co-presenters and moderators arrive there, off stage, and appear to the audience only when you move them on.

## At a glance {#at-a-glance}

| | Green room | Backstage |
|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | The room before **Go Live**, with nothing broadcast | A holding area for presenters who aren't on stage |
| **Who is there** | You and anyone who has joined to present or moderate | Co-hosts and moderators who have joined but aren't on stage yet |
| **What attendees see** | Nothing until you go live | Nothing of people waiting backstage |
| **How you get there** | **Enter Room** on the webinar's **Overview**, then **Continue to Green Room** | Joining presenters and moderators land here automatically |
| **How you leave** | **Go Live** to start broadcasting, or **Exit** | You move a person to the stage, and back again when they're done |
| **Where it sits on screen** | The whole room, with the status badge at top left | The **Backstage** panel on the left edge |

## What the green room is {#what-it-is}

When you enter the room for a live webinar, you don't go straight on air. You land in the green room: the full webinar room with every control working, but in a **Not Live** state. Your camera preview, the stage layout, the side rail and the EasyCast controls are all there, so you prepare in the same room your attendees will see once you start.

**Backstage** is the part of the room for people who are present but not presenting yet. Anyone joining as a co-host or moderator arrives backstage rather than on stage. From there you move them onto the stage when it's their turn, and back off when they've finished. This works the same before and after you go live.

The two together are what EasyWebinar's glossary calls the green room: a presenter backstage before the broadcast starts.

## Why the green room matters {#why-it-matters}

### What it's for {#what-its-for}

The green room separates getting ready from being seen. Presenting live involves a handful of [things that can go wrong](https://easywebinar.com/blog/common-mistakes-while-hosting-webinars/) in the first minute: a muted microphone, the wrong camera, a screen share showing the wrong window, a guest who can't hear. The green room gives you time to find those problems while nobody is watching.

### How it helps {#how-it-helps}

- **Mistakes stay private.** Audio checks and false starts happen before the broadcast, not at the top of the recording.
- **Guests arrive quietly.** A co-host can join early, sort out their audio and wait backstage without any of it being shown.
- **The stage changes on your cue.** You decide when each person appears, so handovers between speakers look planned.
- **The start is deliberate.** The broadcast begins only when you click **Go Live** and confirm.

### What happens without it {#without-it}

Without a staging area, the first few minutes of a webinar become the rehearsal. Attendees who arrived on time watch presenters ask each other whether they can be heard, and the recording that will later become your [replay page](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/media/replay-pages/#with-recording) starts with the same fumbling. Guest speakers who join late interrupt the session in progress.

### Why buyers care {#why-buyers-care}

Teams that run webinars with more than one speaker (panels, interviews, product walkthroughs with a moderator) need guests to join without disrupting the session. A green room with a backstage area is what makes a multi-presenter webinar look composed, and buyers comparing tools for [live webinars](https://easywebinar.com/live-webinars/) look at how guests join and whether they can wait off stage.

## Set it up and use it {#set-it-up}

### 1. Add your presenters and moderators {#add-presenters}

Before the session, decide who will present and who will moderate. You have two routes.

**From the Overview tab.** The [**Team & Moderators** panel](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/setup/overview-tab/#team-and-moderators) lists the presenters and moderators who can run this webinar. A new webinar shows "No presenters or moderators added yet." Use **Add presenter** for someone who appears on camera and presents, or **Invite** to send an invitation to join the webinar team.

**With a join link.** Click **Share** on the webinar and copy the link for the role:

| Link | Who it's for |
|---|---|
| [**Co-presenter Join Link**](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/registration/sharing-links/#co-presenter-join-link) | A presenter, so they can join the room |
| **Moderator Join Link for Live room** | A moderator, to handle [chat](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/engagement/live-chat/#moderators) and [questions](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/engagement/questions-answers/#moderators) |

A presenter or moderator joining by link doesn't need an EasyWebinar account. Treat both links as private: anyone holding one can join your room in that role. The registration, attendee and replay links are the ones meant for your audience.

### 2. Enter the green room {#enter-the-green-room}

1. On the webinar's **Overview**, click **Enter Room**.
2. Choose your **camera, microphone and speakers**.
3. Click **Continue to Green Room**.

The button stays greyed out until the room can see a camera and a microphone. If you see **"Not able to access selected media device"**, see [Camera not detected](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/troubleshooting/camera-not-detected/).

### 3. Read the room {#read-the-room}

![The green room with its status badges, top-bar controls, Go Live and Exit, the Backstage panel, the side rail and the Layout control marked](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/images/live-room/step-01-green-room.png)

*1 Status · 2 Settings, Reactions, EasyCast and Full Screen · 3 Camera, Mic and Share · 4 Go Live and Exit · 5 Backstage · 6 Side rail · 7 Layout.*

| | Area | What to check here |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Status** | **Not Live**, and whether recording is on |
| 2 | **Settings · Reactions · EasyCast · Full Screen** | Room setup and multistreaming |
| 3 | **Camera · Mic · Share** | Your own devices and a test of screen sharing |
| 4 | **Go Live · Exit** | Start the broadcast, or leave the room |
| 5 | **Backstage** | Presenters waiting off stage |
| 6 | **Side rail** | **Chat**, **People**, **Offers**, **Polls**, **Design**, **Question** and **Theme** |
| 7 | **Layout** | How presenters are arranged on stage |

### 4. Bring people backstage and onto the stage {#manage-backstage}

As co-hosts and moderators join, they appear backstage. Expand the **Backstage** panel on the left edge of the room to see who's waiting. To see everyone at once, open **People** on the side rail.

![The People panel with Invite People, the Presenters list with the Host marked, and the Attendees list](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/images/live-room/step-02-people.png)

| | Area | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Invite People** | Join links for presenters and moderators, ready to share |
| 2 | **Presenters** | Everyone who can appear on stage, with the **Host** marked |
| 3 | **Attendees** | Everyone watching |

- **Move someone to the stage** when it's their turn to speak.
- **Move them back** when they're done.

If a guest hasn't received their link, **Invite People** gives you the presenter and moderator links from inside the room.

### 5. Finish your checks {#final-checks}

Use the time before going live to:

- **Test Share** with the window or screen you'll actually present.
- **[Confirm recording](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/live-room/run-a-live-webinar/#check-recording).** **Auto Recording Enabled** in the header means the session will be recorded; **Settings → Recording Settings** changes it.
- **Set the look.** Backgrounds, blur, frame overlays and layout are on **Design**. Changes apply immediately, so set them here rather than mid-broadcast.
- **Switch on any [EasyCast destinations](https://easywebinar.com/easycast/).** Click **EasyCast** in the top bar and turn **Stream** on for each channel this session should reach.
- **Decide on camera and mic.** You can switch **Camera** and **Mic** off in the green room and still go live, for a session built on slides or video.

### 6. Leave the green room {#leave-the-green-room}

Click **Go Live** when everyone is ready. The confirmation reminds you that anyone in the waiting area will be moved in automatically, and that [offers](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/engagement/timed-offers/#create-an-offer) and [polls](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/engagement/polls/#create-a-poll) should already exist. Confirm, and the header changes from **Not Live** to **LIVE** with a timer.

To leave without broadcasting, click **Exit**. For what happens once you're live, see [Run a live webinar](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/live-room/run-a-live-webinar/).

## How the green room works with other features {#works-with}

### Team members and roles {#with-team-members}

A team member is someone with their own EasyWebinar login, created under **Settings → Team** as an [**Admin** or **User**](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/account/team-members/#roles). A presenter or moderator is someone running a specific webinar. Add a person to your team when they work in EasyWebinar regularly; send a join link when they're a guest on one session. Sharing one login between hosts means only one of you can be in the live room as a host. See [Team members](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/account/team-members/).

### Share links {#with-share-links}

The **Share** dialog on the webinar holds seven links, including the presenter and moderator ones above. **Shorten URL's** gives you shorter versions. See [Sharing links](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/registration/sharing-links/).

### EasyCast {#with-easycast}

EasyCast destinations are switched on in the green room, before you go live. Once the broadcast starts, a platform icon for each destination appears in the top bar. Connections are made once, under **Integrations → EasyCast** in the sidebar, and work across every webinar.

### Room design {#with-room-design}

The **Design** panel sets the background, a frame overlay and the stage layout. Layout matters most when several presenters are on stage or someone is sharing a screen.

## Who uses the green room, and how {#who-uses-it}

These are typical patterns, shown to illustrate how the green room and backstage fit different webinars.

### B2B marketing teams {#b2b-marketing}

A [marketing team](https://easywebinar.com/solutions/marketing-teams/) runs a panel with two guest speakers from partner companies. Each guest gets the **Co-presenter Join Link**, joins fifteen minutes early, and checks their audio backstage. The host introduces the topic alone, then moves each guest onto the stage in turn.

### Customer success teams {#customer-success}

A customer success manager presents a [customer training session](https://easywebinar.com/solutions/customer-success-teams/) while a colleague joins with the **Moderator Join Link for Live room** to handle questions. The moderator never needs to appear on stage, so they stay off it for the whole session.

### Coaches and consultants {#coaches}

A coach runs an [interview-style masterclass](https://easywebinar.com/webinar-platform-for-coaches-course-creators/) with a guest expert who has no EasyWebinar account. The guest joins by link, waits backstage during the coach's opening, and comes on stage for the interview segment.

### SaaS and product teams {#product-teams}

A product team rehearses a [product launch demo](https://easywebinar.com/use-cases/product-demos/) in the green room: they test the screen share of the product, pick a layout that gives the demo most of the stage, and switch on a LinkedIn Organisation destination in EasyCast before going live.

## Limits and things to know {#limits}

- **A camera and microphone are needed to get past the device check,** even if you switch them off in the green room.
- **The green room broadcasts nothing.** The header reads **Not Live** until you go live.
- **Co-hosts and moderators arrive backstage,** never straight onto the stage.
- **Presenter and moderator links grant that role** to whoever holds them. Keep them private.
- **Joining by link needs no account,** but a person with a team login can also join as a presenter.
- **Design changes apply as you make them.** Anything you change after going live is visible.
- **[Only one event can be live](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/troubleshooting/camera-not-detected/#one-event-live) at a time.** If another webinar is live, **Enter Room** shows "You can only have one event live at a time."

## Common questions {#common-questions}

#### Does anything I do in the green room get broadcast? {#faq-broadcast}

No. The header reads **Not Live**, and nothing is sent to attendees until you click **Go Live** and confirm.

#### Where do my co-hosts appear when they join? {#faq-cohosts}

Backstage. Expand the **Backstage** panel on the left edge to see them, and move them to the stage when you want them seen.

#### How do I invite a guest speaker? {#faq-guest-speaker}

Send them the **Co-presenter Join Link** from the **Share** dialog, or add them under **Team & Moderators** on the Overview tab.

#### Does a guest presenter need an EasyWebinar account? {#faq-account-needed}

No. Presenters and moderators can join with their link without an account.

#### What's the difference between a team member and a presenter? {#faq-team-vs-presenter}

A team member has their own login to your EasyWebinar account. A presenter or moderator is running a specific webinar, and may be a guest with only a join link.

#### Can I invite people from inside the room? {#faq-invite-from-room}

Yes. **Invite People** at the top of the **People** panel shares the presenter and moderator join links.

#### Can I take someone off the stage? {#faq-move-back}

Yes. Move them back off the stage when they've finished, and they return to backstage.

#### Can I leave the green room without going live? {#faq-exit}

Yes. Click **Exit**.

#### Should I set up EasyCast in the green room? {#faq-easycast}

Yes. Switch **Stream** on for each destination before you click **Go Live**.

#### Is the green room the same as the waiting area? {#faq-waiting-area}

No. The green room is for you and your presenters. The waiting area is where attendees wait before the session, and they're moved into the webinar automatically when you go live.

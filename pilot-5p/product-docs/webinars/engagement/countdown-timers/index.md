# Countdown timers

How EasyWebinar's countdown timers work on offers and registration pages, why they matter for selling with webinars, and how to set them up honestly.

- Applies to: Live, simulive and evergreen webinars
- Status: Live
- Verified: 2026-10-16
- Canonical page: https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/engagement/countdown-timers/

EasyWebinar has two countdowns. An **offer countdown** sits on an offer card in the webinar room and shows how long that offer stays open. A [**registration-page countdown**](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/registration/registration-page-builder/#countdown-block) shows how long is left until the session starts. Both are built in, and neither needs a separate timer tool or embedded widget.

## At a glance {#at-a-glance}

| | Offer countdown | Registration-page countdown |
|---|---|---|
| **Where it appears** | On an offer card, in the webinar room | On the registration page, before people sign up |
| **What it counts down to** | The end of the offer | The start of the session |
| **How you turn it on** | **Countdown timer** switch in the offer editor | Countdown block in the page builder; present by default on a new registration page |
| **Where you set it** | Webinar → **CTAs** → an offer | Webinar → **Content** → **Registration Page** |
| **Webinar types** | Live, simulive and evergreen | Webinars with scheduled sessions |

## What a countdown timer is {#what-it-is}

A countdown timer is a visible clock that shows the time remaining before something happens. In EasyWebinar it does one of two jobs:

- **On an offer**, it tells attendees how long they have to act on what you are selling: a course enrolment, a coaching call, a bonus, a programme.
- **On a registration page**, it tells visitors how soon the session begins, which turns "sometime" into "in two days".

The offer countdown lives inside the **offer** itself, so it travels wherever that offer is shown. The registration-page countdown is a **block** on the page, so you style and position it like any other part of the page.

## Why countdown timers matter {#why-it-matters}

{/* 5P */}

Selling webinars often end on an offer with a real end point: launch pricing, an enrolment window or an in-session bonus. That end point only works if attendees know it when they decide. A clear deadline is one of the few things that [changes behaviour at that moment](https://easywebinar.com/blog/webinar-copywriting-psychological-triggers/). Before the session, registration page visitors need the start time just as clearly.

Many hosts say the deadline aloud, or copy it into the script by hand. Some also embed a separate timer widget, which is one more tool to set up and keep in step with the offer. Anyone who joins late or steps away misses a spoken deadline. Unsure whether the offer is still open, they wait, and in webinar selling that usually means they don't buy.

Recorded sessions make the spoken deadline even less reliable. On [simulive and evergreen webinars](https://easywebinar.com/automated-webinars/) nobody is presenting, and a line recorded weeks ago can name a date that has passed. The webinar plays without you, so the deadline has to run without you as well.

EasyWebinar attaches the deadline to the offer card, not to the presenter. In the offer editor on the **CTAs** tab, the **Countdown timer** switch adds a countdown to that offer. Attendees then see a [visible time limit](https://easywebinar.com/blog/scarcity-tactics-for-webinar-conversions/) instead of relying on what they heard. For the start time, EasyWebinar adds a countdown to every new registration page, which gives visitors [a reason to register now](https://easywebinar.com/blog/improve-your-webinar-registration-page/).

You can check both in the product. **Countdown timer** is setting 2 in the offer editor screenshot below, between **Offer Style** and **Offer Price**, and it is one toggle per offer. When you create a webinar, the registration page builder opens with your schedule applied and a live countdown to the session already running.

## Set up an offer countdown {#set-up-offer-countdown}

1. Open your webinar and click the **CTAs** tab.
2. Open an existing offer, or use **+** at the bottom of the list on the left to create one.
3. Switch on **Countdown timer**.
4. Fill in the rest of the offer: **Offer Style** (**Text Offer** or **Image Offer**), **Offer Price** (or switch on **Free**), the **Headline** and **Sub Headline**, and the **Button** text and URL.
5. Check the preview in the centre of the editor. It shows the offer exactly as attendees will see it and updates as you type.
6. Save.

*The offer editor: 1 Offer Style · 2 Countdown timer · 3 Offer Price · 4 Title, Headline and Sub Headline · 5 Button · 6 Colours.*

### Offer settings that work with the countdown {#offer-settings}

| Setting | What it does | Why it matters with a countdown |
|---|---|---|
| **Offer Style** | Text Offer or Image Offer | Choose the style that keeps the timer easy to see |
| **Offer Price** | A price, or **Free** | A countdown on a priced offer usually signals a price or bonus that ends |
| **Headline** and **Sub Headline** | What attendees read | Say what ends when the timer runs out, in plain words |
| **Button** text and URL | Where the click goes | Point it at the checkout or booking page for exactly this offer |
| **Colours** | Button and button text colour | Keep the button readable next to the timer |

The offer's **Title** is internal. Attendees never see it, so name it in a way that helps your team, for example "Course enrolment, launch week".

### Save without showing it yet {#prepare-in-advance}

You can build the offer, countdown included, before the webinar and hold it back:

- Turn the offer's toggle off in the **CTAs** list, or
- Set **Published** to off in the editor.

The offer stays ready but does not appear until you switch it on.

## Set up the registration-page countdown {#set-up-registration-countdown}

New registration pages carry a countdown to your session automatically. When you [create a webinar](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/getting-started/create-a-live-webinar/#registration-template), the builder opens with your schedule applied and the countdown already running.

![The registration page builder showing the webinar date, a countdown timer and the registration form](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/images/create-live/step-05-registration-page-builder.png)

To change it:

1. Open your webinar and click **Content**, then [**Edit** on **Registration Page**](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/registration/registration-page-builder/#open-the-builder).
2. Click the countdown block on the canvas. An orange toolbar appears above it, and the settings panel on the right fills with its options.
3. Adjust its settings across the **Settings** and **Advanced** tabs.
4. Use the device toggle to check the mobile layout, then **Preview**, **Save** and **Publish**.

### Countdown block settings {#registration-countdown-settings}

| Setting | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Visibility** | Where the block shows, including a **Desktop Only** option |
| **Margins** and **width** | The block's spacing and size on the page |
| **Fonts**, **sizes** and **alignment** | How the date, time and timezone are styled |

> **Publish to make it public**
> Changes to the registration page, including the countdown, only reach visitors after you click **Publish**. **Save** keeps a draft.

## How countdowns work with other EasyWebinar features {#works-with}

### Offers and checkout {#with-offers}

The countdown is part of an offer, so everything about the offer still applies: its price, its button and where that button goes. Point the button at the checkout for exactly the offer the timer describes.

### Replays and replay deadlines {#with-replays}

With **Webinar Replica**, the replay page shows the same video, offers, polls and chat replay as the session, so your offers keep selling after the event. The replay itself can also have a deadline: [**Expire after days**](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/media/replay-pages/#availability) closes it a set number of days after the session, and **Specific date** closes it at a time you choose. A replay deadline and an offer deadline are separate settings; keep them consistent so attendees aren't told two different end times.

### Create with AI {#with-create-with-ai}

**Create with AI** [writes offers for you](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/ai/create-with-ai/#generate-offers). Its **Direct Pitch** positioning is "offer-focused, repeated CTAs, strong urgency", while **Soft Pitch** is education-focused with the offer at the end. Whichever you choose, the offers it creates are normal offers on the **CTAs** tab, so you can add a countdown to any of them.

### Analytics {#with-analytics}

After the webinar, open [**Analytics**](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/analytics/webinar-analytics/#engagement-and-offers). **Offers Performance** shows views, clicks and purchases for each offer with a click rate. **Views to Clicks** and **Clicks to Purchases** show where the offer loses people, and **Revenue by Offer** shows what each offer earned. Comparing an offer with and without a countdown across sessions is the clearest way to see what the timer is doing for you.

### Follow-up and CRM tags {#with-follow-up}

People who click an offer can be treated differently afterwards:

- [**Attendee Action Follow-ups**](https://easywebinar.com/features/webinar-follow-up/) on the **Notifications** tab send emails based on what someone did, including whether they clicked an offer.
- In a connected CRM, the [**Clicked offer** event](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/integrations/map-webinar-data/#events) can apply a tag, for example `webinar-hot`, so sales can follow up.

## Who uses countdown timers, and how {#who-uses-it}

These are typical patterns, shown to illustrate how the feature fits each kind of webinar.

### Course creators {#course-creators}

An evergreen webinar teaches the core idea of a course and ends with an [enrolment offer](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/use-cases/sell-courses/#sell-during-the-webinar). A countdown on the offer marks the window for an enrolment bonus. Because the webinar is automated, the timer carries the deadline for every session without the creator being there.

### Coaches and consultants {#coaches}

A [live masterclass](https://easywebinar.com/webinar-platform-for-coaches-course-creators/) ends with an offer to book a call. The countdown shows how long the booking link stays open after the session, and the registration-page countdown builds attendance in the days before.

### Paid workshops and live events {#paid-workshops}

For a [paid workshop](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/selling/paid-webinars/#paid-workshops), the registration-page countdown shows how long is left to register before the session starts. Inside the workshop, a countdown on an offer can mark a follow-on programme available to attendees only.

### Product and SaaS teams {#product-teams}

A [recorded product demo](https://easywebinar.com/use-cases/product-demos/) ends with an offer for a trial or a call with sales. A countdown is optional here; many teams use it only during launch periods.

## Use countdowns honestly {#use-honestly}

A countdown persuades because people trust it. Keep that trust:

- **Only use a countdown for a real deadline.** The time shown should match what actually happens when it ends.
- **Say what ends.** Use the headline or sub headline to state what expires: the bonus, the price or the enrolment window.
- **Keep deadlines consistent.** Match the offer countdown, the replay deadline and what your follow-up emails say.

## Limits and things to know {#limits}

- **The offer countdown is set per offer.** Each offer has its own **Countdown timer** switch.
- **Polls have no countdown setting.** The **Countdown timer** switch is in the offer editor only.
- **The registration-page countdown is a page block.** It can be hidden on mobile with **Desktop Only** visibility, styled, or removed like any other block.
- **Live-room timers are different.** Once you go live, the [room header shows **LIVE**](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/live-room/run-a-live-webinar/#go-live) with a timer of how long you have been broadcasting. That timer counts up and is separate from offer countdowns.

## Common questions {#common-questions}

#### Can I add a countdown to an offer? {#faq-add-countdown}

Yes. Open the offer on the **CTAs** tab and switch on **Countdown timer**.

#### Does every offer need a countdown? {#faq-every-offer}

No. It's optional per offer. Use it when the offer genuinely ends or changes at a set time.

#### Can I add a countdown to a poll? {#faq-poll-countdown}

No. The countdown setting is in the offer editor; the poll editor has question, response options and **Published**.

#### Is there a countdown on my registration page? {#faq-registration-page}

Yes. New registration pages show a countdown to the session. You can restyle it, hide it on mobile with **Desktop Only**, or remove the block.

#### Will my offers, and their countdowns, show on the replay? {#faq-replay}

Offers carry over to the replay when the replay page uses **Webinar Replica**. Use **Preview** on the replay page to check it looks the way you want.

#### Can I prepare an offer with a countdown before the webinar? {#faq-prepare}

Yes. Build it, then turn its toggle off in the **CTAs** list, or set **Published** to off, until you want it shown.

#### How do I know whether the countdown is helping? {#faq-measure}

Compare sessions in **Analytics → Offers Performance**: views, clicks, purchases and click rate. **Clicks to Purchases** tells you whether people who clicked went on to buy.

#### Does EasyWebinar write the offer for me? {#faq-ai}

It can. **Create with AI** generates offers with a title, headline, sub headline, button text and link, and you can add a countdown to any of them.

#### Do I need a separate countdown tool? {#faq-separate-tool}

No. Both countdowns are built into EasyWebinar.

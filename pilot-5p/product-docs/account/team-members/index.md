# Team members and roles

Give colleagues their own EasyWebinar login, choose Admin or User, and understand how team members differ from presenters and moderators.

- Applies to: All plans · seats depend on your plan · Owner or Admin
- Status: Live
- Verified: 2026-10-16
- Canonical page: https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/account/team-members/

Anyone who [builds webinars](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/getting-started/create-a-live-webinar/), moderates sessions or [reads your analytics](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/analytics/webinar-analytics/) regularly should have their own login. Sharing one login means you can't tell who did what, and only one of you can be in the [live room](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/live-room/run-a-live-webinar/) as host.

**Expected result:** a new team member listed under **Settings → Team** with the right role, able to sign in with the details you give them.

## Before you begin {#before-you-begin}

- Check your seat allowance on the **Settings** overview, for example *2 of 10 seats used*. Seats are [part of your plan](https://easywebinar.com/pricing/).
- Each member needs their own email address.

## Open the Team screen {#team-screen}

Click **Settings** in the sidebar, then **Team**.

![The Team screen listing members and their roles](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/images/team/step-01-team-list.png)

| | Area | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Add user** | Creates a new teammate |
| 2 | **Column headings** | Member, Role, Joined, Actions |
| 3 | **Role** | Owner, Admin or User |

## Add a team member {#add-a-team-member}

1. Click **Add user**.
2. Enter their **Name** and **Email**, and optionally a **Photo**.
3. Set a **Password** and **Confirm password**.
4. Choose a **Role**: **Admin** or **User**.
5. Click **Add user**.

![The Add user dialog with name, email, password and role](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/images/team/step-02-add-user.png)

> **No invitation email is sent**
> You set their password and nothing is emailed to them. There is no invitation to accept, so pass the login details on yourself and ask them to change the password once they are in.


## Roles {#roles}

| Role | Access |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | The account holder. One per account |
| **Admin** | "Full access to everything in the organization including all settings." |
| **User** | "Basic access to organization features." |

### Which role to give {#which-role}

Give **Admin** to people who need to change billing, team or account settings. **User** suits [presenters, moderators](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/live-room/green-room/#add-presenters) and anyone who works on webinars without touching the account.

### Changing roles and ownership {#change-role}

Change someone's role from the **Actions** column on their row. The **Owner** can't be changed from this screen; contact support if ownership needs to move.

## Team members, presenters and moderators {#presenters-and-moderators}

A **team member** has a login. A **presenter** or **moderator** runs a specific webinar and is added on that webinar's [Overview tab](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/setup/overview-tab/#team-and-moderators). Presenters and moderators can join with a link, without an account.

- Add someone to your team when they work in EasyWebinar regularly.
- Send a **Co-presenter** or **Moderator** join link from the [Share dialog](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/registration/sharing-links/) when they are a guest on one session.

## How team members work with other features {#works-with}

### The Overview tab {#with-overview-tab}

Each webinar's **Team & Moderators** panel shows everyone who can run that webinar. **Add presenter** adds someone who appears on camera and presents. **Invite** sends an invitation to join the webinar team. The Overview page explains [how presenters differ from team members](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/setup/overview-tab/#team-members-vs-presenters).

### Join links {#with-share-links}

The Share dialog holds a [Co-presenter Join Link](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/registration/sharing-links/#co-presenter-join-link) for a presenter and a **Moderator Join Link for Live room** for someone who handles chat and questions. Treat both as private. Anyone holding one can join your room in that role.

### The live room {#with-live-room}

Co-hosts and moderators who join land [backstage, not on stage](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/live-room/green-room/#manage-backstage). Your audience doesn't see or hear them until you bring them on. That lets a colleague join early and sort out their audio before they speak.

### Chat and Q&A moderation {#with-moderation}

Moderators work with the settings on the **Engagement** tab. With [Private Q&A](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/engagement/questions-answers/#public-or-private), questions are visible only to hosts and moderators. With chat set to [Hosts only](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/engagement/live-chat/#who-can-see-messages), each attendee talks privately to your team. [AI Moderation (Beta)](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/webinars/engagement/live-chat/#with-ai-moderation) doesn't replace a human moderator on a live session; someone still spots the question worth answering on air.

### Your plan {#with-plan}

Seats come with your plan. **Change plan**, on the **Plan** tab under **Settings → Manage Plan**, moves you up or down, so [compare plans](https://easywebinar.com/pricing/) before you add people in bulk.

### Client workspaces {#with-client-workspaces}

If you run webinars for clients, a client workspace gives each one a separate EasyWebinar account that you manage, on its own plan. It is the setup for [marketing agencies running client webinars](https://easywebinar.com/solutions/marketing-agencies/). Unlike adding a team member, the client does get an email confirmation to set their password, so use an address they read.

## Verify the result {#verify-the-result}

The new member appears in the Team list with their role and joined date, and the seat count on the Settings overview goes up by one.

## Limits and variations {#limits-and-variations}

- **Seats depend on your plan.** Adding a member costs more only if you have used all the seats your plan includes. Seat and role rules are also listed under [team and account limits](https://maximuslabs-ai.github.io/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/easywebinar-product-docs/pilot-5p/product-docs/reference/limits-and-defaults/#team-and-account).
- **There is no per-permission matrix.** Access is set by role alone; custom roles were not available at the time of checking.
- **Client workspaces are separate** from team seats; adding a workspace doesn't use a seat.

## Common questions {#common-questions}

#### How do I invite someone to my account? {#faq-invite}

You don't invite; you create their account with **Add user**, including their password, then pass the details to them.

#### Can someone join a webinar as a presenter without an account? {#faq-presenter-no-account}

Yes. Use the Co-presenter or Moderator join link from the Share dialog.

#### What's the difference between Admin and User? {#faq-admin-user}

Admin can change everything, including settings and billing. User gets basic access to the features.

#### How many team members can I have? {#faq-how-many}

It depends on your plan. The Settings overview shows seats used out of your allowance.

#### Can I change someone's role later? {#faq-change-role}

Yes, from the **Actions** column on their row in the Team list.

#### Where do co-hosts appear when they join the room? {#faq-co-hosts}

Backstage. Move them to the stage when you want them seen and heard, and back again when they're done.

#### Does a client workspace use one of my team seats? {#faq-workspace-seat}

No. Seats and workspaces are separate, and each workspace is billed on its own plan.
